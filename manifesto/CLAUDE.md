# The Roadman Manifesto — working rules (condensed)

60–80 page position paper on reverse periodisation and masters cycling methodology. €27–47, sits between the free Plateau Diagnostic and NDY membership. A position paper, not a training manual.

**Core thesis:** Traditional periodisation is designed for 25-year-old professionals with unlimited time. The masters athlete training 8–12 hours a week needs the model inverted — and the evidence has been hiding in plain sight across 1,400 expert interviews.

**Reader:** Plateau Tom — 33–46, Cat 3–4, 8–12 h/week, FTP stuck, quietly ashamed. Explain *why* he's stuck before telling him what to do.

## Workflow

- Work per-chapter, idempotent, resumable. Every session ends with a STATUS.md update.
- Per chapter: source pull → draft → verify → flag → **stop for Anthony** (at least first three chapters).
- Phase gates: Phase 0 audit reviewed by Anthony → outline (`/outline/proposed-v1.md`) approved by Anthony → chapters.
- Evidence: every empirical claim traces to a specific episode (number/title + approximate timestamp). Quotes verbatim from transcript with speaker confirmed; if attribution uncertain, paraphrase and flag. Expert disagreement across episodes is content, not a problem.
- Read `blocklist.md` before every verify step. Never guess coach-rider attributions.
- Do not invent expert positions to fill gaps. A thinner honest chapter beats a padded one.
- Knowledge graph production tables: read-only transcript access only.
- Reviews must be fast for Anthony: flagged items up top, diffs, no process narration.

## Voice — written long-form (Dan Koe style overrides voice-guide.md, which is podcast/video only)

- Short punchy lines. Heavy whitespace. Single-sentence paragraphs.
- Declarative philosophical openers. Contrarian reframes. Pain → insight → action within sections.
- Active voice. No hedging. Credibility-first, peer-to-peer, cycling-specific, no hype.

**Banned openers:** "Here's what nobody tells you," "the cycling internet won't tell you," "let me break this down," "stick around to the end."

**Banned words:** delve, unpack, multifaceted, in today's world, it's worth noting, straightforward, game-changer, leverage (verb), robust, synergy, at its core, at the end of the day, journey, unlock your potential, elevate.

**Structural tells to avoid:** em-dash overuse; uniform paragraph lengths; "It isn't X. It's Y." on loop; intro→three points→conclusion shape; neat balanced perspectives; generic observations that fit any topic.
