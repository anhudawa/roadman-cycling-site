## How This List Was Built

1. **Archive audit:** 320 episodes catalogued from `content/podcast/`, cross-referenced against the episode-topic-map (310 indexed episodes across endurance, nutrition, strength, recovery, and culture clusters).
2. **Keyword mapping:** 15 priority keyword clusters matched to episodes featuring named experts with real credentials — World Tour coaches, published researchers, practising nutritionists.
3. **Gap analysis:** Checked against ~280 existing blog posts. Several companion posts exist (episode recaps, guest profiles), but almost none are structured as standalone keyword-targeted pillar content. The opportunity is wide open.
4. **Internal link audit:** Mapped tools (/tools/ftp-zones, /tools/fuelling, /tools/race-weight, /tools/masters-ftp-benchmark, etc.), coaching pages (/coaching/masters, /coaching/time-crunched, /coaching/weight-loss, etc.), diagnostics (/diagnostic), community (/community/not-done-yet), and The Method (/method).
