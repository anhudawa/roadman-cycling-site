What's Inside

Five pillars. Each one built from conversations with the coaches and scientists behind the best riders on earth. Each one written for the rider with a job, a family, and 8–12 hours a week to train.

  1. 01

    Coaching

    Why 90% of age-group cyclists are stuck in the grey zone — and the polarised model that Seiler proved and Lorang applies to Grand Tour winners. Includes the exact sessions World Tour coaches prescribe for time-limited riders and the low-cadence torque protocol the science has finally validated.

  2. 02

    Nutrition

    Why "eat less, ride more" is making you slower and fatter. The fuelling-first approach that helped me drop 7kg in 12 weeks while eating more food than ever. Plus the in-ride nutrition protocol the pros use — and why you're probably bonking at 60km from home.

  3. 03

    Strength & Conditioning

    The exercises to avoid after 40 (and why heavy barbell work is a bad bet at this stage). Five movements adapted from Pogačar's own programme for amateur cyclists — designed to build power off the bike, prevent injury, and add years to your riding.

  4. 04

    Recovery

    The over-40 recovery equation: why what worked at 30 doesn't work now. Sleep, stress, and HRV — the three metrics that determine whether your training is building you up or breaking you down. Plus the adaptation protocol for riders who can't afford to get this wrong.

  5. 05

    Community — Le Métier

    Why the lone wolf cyclist always plateaus. Le métier — the craft of being a cyclist — is more than watts and weight. What riding with serious cyclists actually does to your physiology, your motivation, and your ceiling. And how 113 riders are applying all five pillars together inside Not Done Yet.
