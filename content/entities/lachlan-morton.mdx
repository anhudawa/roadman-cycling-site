Lachlan Morton is an Australian professional cyclist on EF Pro Cycling's 2026 Far Beyond programme. His current role combines gravel events, road races, ultra-distance rides, fastest-known-time attempts and filmed expeditions rather than a conventional WorldTour road calendar.

The verified career record is broader than the shorthand “quit the WorldTour.” Morton raced for Garmin-Sharp from 2012 to 2014, spent 2015 and 2016 with Jelly Belly, returned to the top tier with Dimension Data in 2017 and 2018, and joined EF in 2019. EF announced in 2024 that their relationship would continue and lists him in its 2026 Far Beyond programme.

His recent results include the 2021 Alt Tour, a roughly 5,500km unsupported ride covering Tour de France stages and transfers; the 2024 Unbound Gravel 200 win in 9:11:47; a 12-day, 12-hour, 21-minute Tour Divide ride; and the supported 14,200km Around Australia record in 30 days, 9 hours and 59 minutes.

Morton's Roadman interview supplies a different kind of evidence. He says the isolation and single-mindedness required for his best road performance stopped feeling worth the personal cost, and that bikepacking opened another path. Those statements describe his experience. EF and UCI records establish the team timeline, current role, dates, distances and race results.
