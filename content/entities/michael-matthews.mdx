Michael “Bling” Matthews is an Australian professional road cyclist for [Team Jayco AlUla](https://greenedgecycling.com/2026/michael-matthews/). The official team and [Australian Olympic Committee](https://www.olympics.com.au/olympians/michael-matthews/) records confirm the central career facts: the 2010 U23 road world title, stage victories in all three Grand Tours and the 2017 Tour de France points classification. He made his Olympic debut in Paris in 2024 and finished 15th in the road race.

Matthews has appeared twice on the Roadman Cycling Podcast. The earlier conversation covers his nickname, the Orica-GreenEDGE years, Peter Sagan and the pursuit of the green jersey. The [2026 interview](/podcast/ep-4-15-years-of-pro-riding-what-amateurs-don-t-know-matthews) focuses on the training and recovery practices he says have sustained a long WorldTour career.

The most important boundary is attribution. Matthews said that he does not use a traditional base-miles block, rides much of a training day in the high-200s to low-300s watts, and then adds prescribed efforts. He also said that an experiment with easier riding and harder intervals left him able to complete the efforts but underdone for racing. Those statements describe one professional rider's experience. They are not evidence that every cyclist should copy his workload or abandon polarised training.
