Dr Andy Galpin is [Professor and Executive Director of the Human Performance Center at Parker University](https://www.parker.edu/research-at-parker/research-staff/andy-galpin/).

In his [Roadman interview](/podcast/the-science-of-getting-faster-after-40-dr-andy-galpin), Anthony Walsh asks whether a cyclist over 40 can improve strength after years away from the gym. Galpin discusses training goals, learning unfamiliar movements and the recovery demands of an existing riding programme.

Our [companion article](/blog/andy-galpin-fast-twitch-fibres-cyclist-after-40) separates his explanation from Roadman’s practical interpretation. His appearance is an editorial contribution to the podcast, not an endorsement of Good Legs.
