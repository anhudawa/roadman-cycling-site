    answer: >-
      Van der Poel won Stage 2 atop the Mûr-de-Bretagne by targeting
      intermediate time bonuses on the first ascent of the climb, then attacking

  - question: >-
      Why was Mathieu van der Poel wearing yellow so emotional at the 2021 Tour
      de France?
    answer: >-
      Van der Poel's grandfather is Raymond Poulidor, a French cycling legend
      who wore the maillot jaune eight times across his career but never won the
      Tour de France. Taking the yellow jersey at the same finish where Poulidor
      once raced made the moment deeply personal, and Van der Poel said he had
      no words to describe how proud his grandfather would have been.
  - question: What happened to Geraint Thomas on Stage 2 of the 2021 Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      Geraint Thomas conceded 17 seconds to the stage's key movers on the
      Mûr-de-Bretagne, adding to Ineos Grenadiers' difficult start to the race.
      The loss of time compounded their GC problems after the opening stages.
  - question: >-
      Who is Raymond Poulidor and what is his connection to the Tour de France
      yellow jersey?
    answer: >-
      Raymond Poulidor is one of the most celebrated French cyclists of his era,
      famous for finishing on the Tour de France podium eight times without ever
      winning the race or wearing the yellow jersey. His grandson Mathieu van
      der Poel taking the maillot jaune in 2021 fulfilled a symbolic moment the
      family had never experienced before.
