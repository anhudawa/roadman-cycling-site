faq:
  - question: What does a sports director do in a professional cycling team?
    answer: >-
      A sports director manages race strategy, rider selection, and in-race
      decisions from the team car. Matt White explains that the role also
      involves handling rider personalities under the extreme psychological
      pressure of three-week Grand Tours, making people management as important
      as tactical knowledge.
  - question: How does the biological passport work in cycling anti-doping?
    answer: >-
      The biological passport tracks a rider's blood and hormone values over
      time, flagging suspicious fluctuations rather than requiring a direct
      positive test. Matt White notes that retroactive testing means cheaters
      can be caught years after the fact, which he argues makes doping a genuine
      and credible risk for any rider tempted to try it.
  - question: >-
      How does Mitchelton-Scott's Backstage Pass series affect riders during
      races?
    answer: >-
      The Backstage Pass series gives fans behind-the-scenes access throughout
      major races. Matt White found that filming quickly revealed which riders
      are comfortable in front of a camera mid-competition and which need to be
      shielded from that additional pressure to perform at their best.
  - question: What does it take to build a successful WorldTour cycling team?
    answer: >-
      According to Matt White, success at WorldTour level requires balancing
      strong leadership, careful rider management, and a clear team identity.
      Consistency in how the programme is structured and how staff handle
      high-pressure race situations separates elite teams from the rest of the
      peloton.
