  - question: Why did Richard Carapaz get booed at Tour de France 2023?
    answer: >-
      Carapaz refused to take a single turn on the front during the final climb
      of Stage 17, sitting on the wheels of Pogačar and Vingegaard without
      contributing any work. This tactic drew widespread criticism from fans and
      commentators, earning him the reputation of least liked rider in the
      peloton that day. Anthony Walsh covers the controversy in detail in this
      episode.
  - question: What is rope a dope tactics in cycling?
    answer: >-
      Rope a dope in cycling refers to a rider conserving energy by sitting on
      rivals' wheels and refusing to share the workload, forcing others to burn
      matches while contributing nothing. The tactic is borrowed from Muhammad
      Ali's boxing strategy of letting an opponent exhaust themselves. Carapaz
      used this approach on the Col du Portet climb during Stage 17 of the 2023
      Tour de France.
