faq:
  - question: Is there a curse of the rainbow jersey in cycling?
    answer: >-
      The rainbow jersey curse is a long-standing idea in cycling that world
      champions suffer unusual bad luck or poor form in the season after
      winning. Julian Alaphilippe's 2020 and 2021 experiences added fuel to the
      theory, with crashes and near-misses costing him several major results.
      Anthony Walsh examines the evidence in this episode and weighs whether it
      is genuine misfortune or simply the heightened scrutiny that comes with
      wearing the world champion's stripes.
  - question: Why did Julian Alaphilippe not win Amstel Gold Race 2021?
    answer: >-
      Alaphilippe celebrated too early on the Amstel Gold Race finish, believing
      he had won, only for Primoz Roglic to come past him on the line and take
      the victory. It was a costly error that denied him what would have been a
      significant Monument result as reigning world champion. The moment became
      one of the most talked-about mistakes of the 2021 spring Classics season.
  - question: What happened to Alaphilippe at Tour of Flanders 2020?
    answer: >-
      Alaphilippe crashed out of the 2020 Tour of Flanders while part of a
      leading three-man group alongside Wout van Aert and Mathieu van der Poel.
      His exit effectively removed the world champion from what looked like a
      decisive move for the race victory. The incident was seen as another
      example of the bad luck that followed him after claiming the 2020 World
      Championship title.
  - question: Who is João Almeida and why was he leading the Giro d'Italia?
    answer: >-
      João Almeida is a Portuguese rider who, at just 22 years old, took the
      race leadership at the Giro d'Italia and held it for a significant stretch
      of the race. He rode for Deceuninck-QuickStep and surprised the peloton
      with his consistency and composure in the mountains. His performance was
      widely regarded as one of the breakthrough stories of the 2020 racing
      season.
