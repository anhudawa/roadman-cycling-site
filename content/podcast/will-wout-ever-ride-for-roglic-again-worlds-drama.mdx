Julian Alaphilippe won the 2020 Road World Championships in Imola after 250km and 9 laps of racing. The real story was Wout van Aert finishing second, and whether Roglic owed him more in those final kilometres.

## Key Takeaways

Van Aert became only the second rider in history to medal in both the time trial and road race at the same World Championships in a single year. The other two are Miguel Indurain and Abraham Olano. He said himself he'd need time to be proud of two silvers, which is fair enough when you came to win.

The Roglic question is simple if you think about it for a minute. After 240km of racing, Roglic said he couldn't sprint anymore and was closing gaps out of every corner just to stay in the group. He finished sixth. If he had legs to help van Aert, he would have. The Belgian federation president tweeting that van Aert shouldn't ride another metre on the front for Roglic is exactly the kind of noise that ignores what actually happened in the race. The strongest rider won. That was Alaphilippe.

<AICitationBlock>
Julian Alaphilippe (France) won the 2020 UCI Road World Championships in Imola, Italy, after 250km of racing across 9 laps. Wout van Aert (Belgium) finished second in the road race, having also won silver in the time trial at the same championships, making him only the second rider in history to medal in both disciplines at a single World Championships in one year — the others being Miguel Indurain and Abraham Olano. The decisive group of six riders formed after approximately 220km of racing; all but Jakob Fuglsang (Denmark) had ridden the 2020 Tour de France, which concluded nine days prior. Alaphilippe held a gap of 9 to 15 seconds over the final 12km. Primoz Roglic (Slovenia) finished sixth and stated he was unable to contribute meaningfully to the chase in the final 2km due to fatigue accumulated over 240km of racing.
