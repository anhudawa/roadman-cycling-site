  - question: How does sleep affect athletic performance and recovery?
    answer: >-
      Poor sleep raises cortisol, reduces growth hormone secretion, and impairs
      glycogen replenishment, all of which slow recovery between hard sessions.
      Boomer Anderson recommends treating sleep as a non-negotiable training
      variable rather than something to compress around a busy schedule.
  - question: What is chronobiology and how does it affect training?
    answer: >-
      Chronobiology is the study of how the body's internal clock regulates
      hormones, body temperature, and cognitive function across a 24-hour
      period. Aligning hard training sessions and high-focus work with your
      natural peaks in alertness and physical readiness, as Boomer Anderson
      outlines, can meaningfully improve both performance and adaptation.
  - question: What are omics tests and are they worth it for athletes?
    answer: >-
      Omics testing covers genomics, metabolomics, and proteomics, giving a
      detailed picture of how your body processes nutrients, handles stress, and
      responds to exercise at a biochemical level. Boomer Anderson uses these
      tests with executives and athletes to move beyond guesswork and build
      supplementation and training protocols that are specific to the
      individual.
