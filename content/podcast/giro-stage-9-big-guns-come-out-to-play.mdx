  Stage 9 of the 2021 Giro d'Italia delivered the race's first genuine GC
  battle, with Egan Bernal attacking on the gravel finish at Campo Felice to
  claim his first Grand Tour stage win and the maglia rosa from Attila Valter.
  Host Anthony Walsh analyses Gianluca Brambilla teammate Gianni Moscon's
  decisive lead-out, Remco Evenepoel's premature crosswind move as a tactical
  inexperience tell, and Aleksandr Vlasov's emergence in third. The episode also
  covers Mikel Landa's race-ending crash and broken collarbone, and notes how
  the leader's jersey appears to extract performances beyond a rider's normal
  capacity.
