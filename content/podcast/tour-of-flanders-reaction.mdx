answerCapsule: >-
  Anthony Walsh reviews the 2021 Tour of Flanders, where 26-year-old Kasper
  Asgreen of Deceuninck-Quick-Step outsprinted Mathieu van der Poel in
  Oudenaarde to claim the team's eighth Ronde van Vlaanderen title. Walsh argues
  the result hinged on fatigue mechanics: a low-speed two-up sprint after 254km
  favored the fresher-finishing Asgreen over the pre-race favorite. The episode
  also covers Wout van Aert being dropped on the final climb, Greg Van
  Avermaet's podium for AG2R, Julian Alaphilippe's tactical selflessness, and
  Peter Sagan's underwhelming ride heading into contract negotiations.
