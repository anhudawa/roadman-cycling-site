      share a daily journaling habit. For cyclists, the practice helps align
      daily training and lifestyle choices with a defined goal, rather than

  - question: How long should I journal each day as a cyclist?
    answer: >-
      Coach Anthony Walsh recommends just ten minutes per day. That is enough
      time to reflect on whether your actions are moving you toward your North
      Star goal and to set a clear intention for the day ahead.
  - question: What should I write in a cycling training journal?
    answer: >-
      Start by defining your North Star, the single overarching goal driving
      your season. Then use each daily entry to audit whether your choices,
      training load, sleep, nutrition, and mindset, are properly aligned with
      that goal or pulling you away from it.
