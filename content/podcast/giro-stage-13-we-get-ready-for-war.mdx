  - question: Who won Giro d'Italia stage 13 2021?
    answer: >-
      Giacomo Nizzolo won stage 13 of the 2021 Giro d'Italia, a 198km sprint
      stage from Ravenna to Verona. Edoardo Affini finished second and Peter
      Sagan third. It was a significant result as Nizzolo had previously
      finished runner-up eleven times at the Giro without taking a stage win.
  - question: How many times did Giacomo Nizzolo finish second at the Giro d'Italia?
    answer: >-
      Nizzolo finished second eleven times at the Giro d'Italia before finally
      winning a stage. His stage 13 victory in 2021 ended one of the most
      prolonged near-miss streaks in recent Grand Tour sprinting history. As an
      Italian rider winning into Verona, the result carried particular
      significance for the home crowd.
  - question: What happened to Mathieu van der Poel at the 2021 Giro?
    answer: >-
      Mathieu van der Poel crashed during the 2021 Giro d'Italia, which affected
      his participation in the race. Anthony Walsh analyses the incident in this
      episode, covering what it meant for Van der Poel's condition heading into
      the crucial mountain stages that followed.
  - question: How hard is Monte Zoncolan climb in the Giro d'Italia?
    answer: >-
      Monte Zoncolan covers 13.2 kilometres at an average gradient of 9%, making
      it one of the most brutal summit finishes in professional cycling. Despite
      its severity, the Maglia Rosa has historically never changed hands on the
      Zoncolan, which made stage 14 of the 2021 Giro a particularly intriguing
      prospect for the general classification battle.
