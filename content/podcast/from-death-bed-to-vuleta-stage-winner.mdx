  - question: What happened to Fabio Jakobsen at the 2020 Tour de Pologne?
    answer: >-
      Jakobsen was involved in a horrific high-speed crash at the finish line of
      Stage 1, caused by Dylan Groenewegen closing the road on him at over 80
      km/h. He suffered brain contusions and severed vocal cords, and required
      emergency surgery to keep him alive. His recovery is widely regarded as
      one of the most remarkable in professional sport.
  - question: How did Fabio Jakobsen win Stage 4 of the 2022 Vuelta a España?
    answer: >-
      Jakobsen won an uphill bunch sprint to the finish, beating Jasper
      Philipsen and Mads Pedersen. The decisive move came with just 75 metres to
      go, with Jakobsen timing his sprint later than his rivals. He rode for
      Quick-Step and was the strongest finisher on the day.
  - question: How do you win an uphill sprint finish in cycling?
    answer: >-
      Patience is the key tactical factor in uphill sprint finishes. As Anthony
      Walsh explains using Jakobsen's Stage 4 win as an example, the winning
      move came with only 75 metres remaining, meaning riders who committed
      earlier were already fading. Conserving energy and launching late, rather
      than hitting the front into the wind too soon, gives you a decisive
      advantage on rising ground.
  - question: How long did Fabio Jakobsen's comeback from his crash take?
    answer: >-
      Jakobsen returned to professional racing in 2021, roughly six months after
      his near-fatal crash in August 2020. He went on to win stages at major
      races including the Vuelta a España in 2022, completing a full return to
      the top level of professional sprinting within two years of the incident.
