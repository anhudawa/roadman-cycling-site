answerCapsule: >-
  Host Anthony Walsh frames depression as a continuum rather than a fixed
  diagnosis, distinguishing hereditary genotype depression, which requires
  clinical care, from environmentally driven mood states that respond to
  lifestyle interventions. He argues that daily sunlight exposure influences
  roughly 500 genetic on-off switches, and that diet shapes gut bacteria linked
  to memory and mood. His practical takeaway: barefoot grounding, ocean contact,
  whole-food nutrition, and therapy or meditation to process developmental
  trauma will not cure clinical depression but reliably shift listeners toward
  the lighter end of the continuum.
