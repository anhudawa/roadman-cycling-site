answerCapsule: >-
  In this episode of the Roadman Podcast, host Anthony Walsh argues that the
  aero bike versus climbing bike distinction is largely a marketing construct
  now that UCI rules cap race bikes at 6.8 kilograms. He cites that shedding 2
  kilograms from the frame saves roughly 30 seconds over a 10-kilometer climb,
  while losing the same weight from the rider's body yields a two-minute gain.
  Walsh contends that frame aerodynamics contribute marginally to CdA compared
  with position, helmet, and clothing, and recommends buyers prioritize fit and
  personal feel over category labels.

    body weight would give you a cheaper and bigger performance return.
