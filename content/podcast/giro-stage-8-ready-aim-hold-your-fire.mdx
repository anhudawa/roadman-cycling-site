  - question: Why do breakaways in cycling become political?
    answer: >-
      Breakaways are not decided purely by who is strongest on the day. Teams
      use narrow road sections to physically close the road and control which
      riders get into the move, protecting their own interests and blocking
      rivals. Anthony Walsh explains this clearly in his Stage 8 recap, noting
      that the composition of a break reflects negotiation as much as
      aggression.

      once the sprinting opportunities suited to his profile had largely been
      exhausted, making it a calculated decision rather than a forced
      abandonment.
