  - question: How many hours of sleep do you need to stay healthy in 2021?
    answer: >-
      Eight hours of sleep per night is the target for optimal health and
      longevity. Anthony Walsh references Matt Walker's book Why We Sleep, which
      outlines the significant physical and cognitive consequences of
      consistently sleeping less than this amount.
  - question: What are Blue Zones and what can you learn from them?
    answer: >-
      Blue Zones are regions of the world, including Okinawa in Japan and
      Sardinia in Italy, where unusually high concentrations of centenarians
      live. Research from these populations points to consistent habits
      including a diet of seasonal vegetables and lean proteins, regular
      physical activity, and frequent socialising as key contributors to long
      life.
  - question: >-
      How often should you socialise to improve your mental health and
      wellbeing?
    answer: >-
      Two to three social interactions per week is the recommended frequency for
      maintaining good mental health and happiness. Anthony Walsh draws on Blue
      Zones research to support this, noting that strong social connections are
      a common trait among the world's longest-living populations.
  - question: How do you know if a food is actually good for you?
    answer: >-
      A straightforward test is to ask whether bacteria would eat it. If
      bacteria will not break it down, it is too heavily processed to count as
      real food. Anthony Walsh uses this principle to advocate for whole foods,
      specifically seasonal vegetables and lean proteins, over heavily
      manufactured alternatives.
