      Professional cyclists consume large amounts of carbohydrate during races,

      that elite riders rely heavily on rice cakes, gels, and bars, with
      nutrition timing and quantity planned around the demands of each stage
      rather than personal preference.
  - question: How do cyclists recover mentally after a bad crash?
    answer: >-
      Returning to racing after a severe crash requires structured psychological
      work alongside physical rehabilitation. Mitch Docker, who suffered a
      serious face-first impact at Paris-Roubaix, describes using mindset
      routines and deliberate exposure to manage fear and rebuild confidence in
      descending and bunch riding.
  - question: Should young cyclists drop out of school to focus on racing?
    answer: >-
      Mitch Docker argues strongly against leaving education early to pursue
      professional cycling. He believes that completing a degree, as he did in
      nutrition, removes the financial desperation that leads riders to make
      poor performance and contract decisions, and gives them a credible
      identity outside the sport.

    answer: >-

      genuine evidence behind them and which are largely driven by sponsor
      relationships and peer influence within the peloton.
