  - question: What happened at Giro d'Italia 2021 Stage 16?
    answer: >-
      Stage 16 of the 2021 Giro d'Italia was drastically shortened by organisers
      due to severe weather conditions on the planned route from Sacile to
      Cortina d'Ampezzo. The bad weather also grounded helicopters, leaving
      broadcasters without aerial footage for the majority of the race. Despite
      the chaos, Egan Bernal won the stage and strengthened his lead in the
      overall classification.
  - question: Who won Stage 16 of the 2021 Giro d'Italia?
    answer: >-
      Egan Bernal of Ineos Grenadiers won Stage 16 and also claimed the Cima
      Coppi, the award given to the first rider over the highest point of the
      race. The stage victory tightened his grip on the maglia rosa heading into
      the final week. Anthony Walsh analyses the win in this episode of the
      podcast.
  - question: What happened to Remco Evenepoel at the 2021 Giro d'Italia?
    answer: >-
      Remco Evenepoel suffered a severe collapse on Stage 16, finishing over 24
      minutes down on the stage winner. He had returned to racing after a
      nine-month injury layoff, and Anthony Walsh argues that cycling media bore
      some responsibility for overhyping his participation before he had proven
      his condition. The result raised serious questions about expectations
      placed on young riders returning from long absences.
  - question: Why was the 2021 Giro Stage 16 shortened?
    answer: >-
      Organisers shortened Stage 16 due to severe weather in the mountains on
      the route between Sacile and Cortina d'Ampezzo. The conditions made it
      unsafe to run the full queen stage as planned, meaning fans and
      broadcasters were robbed of what should have been one of the most decisive
      days of the race. The helicopter grounding further reduced the quality of
      coverage available to viewers.
