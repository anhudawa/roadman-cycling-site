faq:
  - question: What app should I use to plan a bikepacking route?
    answer: >-
      Komoot is the recommended app for bikepacking route planning, not Strava.
      Its crowdsourced waypoints highlight landmarks and points of interest that
      are worth detouring for, making the ride far more rewarding than a
      straightforward navigation tool would.
  - question: Should I plan a bikepacking trip as a loop or point to point?
    answer: >-
      Plan point to point rather than a loop for your first bikepacking trip. A
      loop forces you back to the start regardless of how your legs or schedule
      are holding up, whereas a point-to-point route gives you a clear
      destination and makes logistics more manageable.
  - question: How do I prepare my body for a multi-day bikepacking ride?
    answer: >-
      Anthony Walsh recommends training your back specifically, as sustained
      time in the saddle with loaded bags places far more stress on the lower
      back than a typical road ride. Building up your long ride duration in
      training and practising with your loaded bike before the event will reduce
      the risk of a miserable first day.
  - question: How should I pace myself on a bikepacking trip?
    answer: >-
      Drop the focus on average speed entirely and build generous coffee stops
      into your day. Save any off-route exploration for the morning when your
      legs are fresh, and treat the ride as an adventure rather than a
      performance effort.
