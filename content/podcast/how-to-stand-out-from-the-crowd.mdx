    answer: >-

  - question: What does being authentic actually mean in a professional context?
    answer: >-
      Walsh draws a clear line between privacy and secrecy. Being authentic does
      not mean oversharing personal details; it means not hiding who you are or
      what you stand for. People respond to genuine character, and trying to
      project a manufactured image tends to undermine trust rather than build
      it.

      are bringing something to the table rather than taking from it.
