    answer: >-

      winter months, making conditions cold and damp even on otherwise clear
      days. Jay Vine cited this as one of the practical limitations for riders
      who need consistent quality training through the off-season. Andorra's
      altitude and different terrain offer a more controlled environment for a
      climber looking to train seriously year-round.
