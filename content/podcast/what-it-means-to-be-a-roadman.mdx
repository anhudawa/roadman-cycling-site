answerCapsule: >-
  Host Anthony Walsh, a former professional cyclist with Aqua Blue Sport,
  defines the "roadman" ethos that gives his podcast its name, tracing the term
  to a 2017 stage of the Rás Tailteann shared with teammate Robbie John
  McCarthy. He argues a roadman is defined by habits, craft, and tradition:
  maintaining equipment, never begging spare tubes, respecting the group ride,
  and mentoring newer riders rather than showboating. Walsh contends this value
  system is being eroded by Zwift, Strava, and TrainerRoad culture, which
  prioritise individual metrics over the intergenerational customs cyclists once
  passed down.

faq:
  - question: What does roadman mean in cycling?
    answer: >-
      In cycling, a roadman is a rider defined by craft, habit, and respect for
      the traditions of the sport. As Anthony Walsh explains, it means
      maintaining your equipment properly, never scrounging spare tubes from
      other riders, and understanding your role within a group ride rather than
      chasing individual metrics.
  - question: Is Zwift and Strava killing traditional cycling culture?
    answer: >-
      Anthony Walsh argues that platforms like Zwift, Strava, and TrainerRoad
      are eroding the intergenerational customs that once defined road cycling.
      By focusing riders on personal data and indoor performance, these tools
      discourage the mentorship and unwritten etiquette that experienced
      cyclists traditionally passed down to newer riders.
  - question: What are the unwritten rules of road cycling etiquette?
    answer: >-
      Core road cycling etiquette includes carrying your own spare tubes and
      tools, respecting the pace and structure of a group ride, and helping less
      experienced riders learn the customs of the sport. Walsh frames these not
      as strict rules but as habits that define a cyclist's character and
      commitment to the craft.
  - question: What is the Rás Tailteann and why is it important to Irish cycling?
    answer: >-
      The Rás Tailteann is Ireland's premier stage race and a significant test
      of road cycling ability. Anthony Walsh traces the inspiration for his
      roadman ethos to a 2017 stage of the Rás shared with teammate Robbie John
      McCarthy, where the values of graft, solidarity, and tradition were on
      clear display.
