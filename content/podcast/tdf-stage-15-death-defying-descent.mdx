  a decade as Sepp Kuss of Jumbo-Visma soloed to victory in Andorra after
  dropping former world champion Alejandro Valverde on the Col de Beixalís and
  holding him off through a high-risk descent. Host Anthony Walsh analyses how

  Coppi climb.
keyTakeaways:
  - >-

    using just five riders — outnumbering your rivals in a break can outweigh
    having a full domestique train.

    rival means nothing if your own leader can't follow up when it matters.

    less likely to crack you.

    than burning your whole team protecting a podium spot you can't
    realistically defend.
  - >-

    marginal chance to survive a hard day is a smarter play than blowing up
    trying to go with the front group.

    answer: >-

      isolation. This contrasted sharply with Ineos Grenadiers' attritional
      approach, which stripped Tadej Pogačar of support without actually gaining
      time on the yellow jersey.

    answer: >-

      climb, taking it from Michael Woods. Poels accumulated enough mountain
      points across the stage's major climbs to move ahead in the King of the
