    answer: >-
      Mark Cavendish has won 35 Tour de France stages in total, but at the time
      of Stage 4 of the 2021 Tour he claimed his 31st career stage win. That
      2021 victory was his first Tour stage win since 2016, ending a five-year
      drought that included serious illness and a difficult period with
      depression.
  - question: Why did Mark Cavendish almost miss the 2021 Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      Cavendish was only included in the Deceuninck–Quick-Step squad after Sam
      Bennett, the team's first-choice sprinter, fell out of selection.
      Cavendish had returned to the team on a minimum-wage contract, and his
      place on the Tour roster was far from guaranteed. His resilience through
      illness and depression made his Stage 4 win one of the most emotional
      moments in recent Tour history.
  - question: What happened at the Stage 4 Tour de France 2021 rider protest?
    answer: >-
      Riders staged a protest at the start of Stage 4, delaying the race over
      safety concerns about the course design. Anthony Walsh argues in the
      episode that the protest was poorly organised and that the CPA riders'
      union needs to negotiate course changes with race organisers well in
      advance, rather than making last-minute appeals on the morning of a stage.
