  - question: Is CBD oil legal for competitive cyclists?
    answer: >-
      CBD is legal for competitive cyclists and is not on the WADA prohibited
      list. It is non-psychoactive and legally distinct from THC, which is the
      compound in cannabis responsible for psychoactive effects. Cyclists should
      ensure any product they use is third-party tested to confirm it contains
      no detectable THC, as contaminated products remain a risk.

      which directly influences anxiety levels. For pre-race nerves, a measured
      dose taken in the lead-up to an event can take the edge off without
      impairing alertness or performance. Topical CBD application is also used
      alongside oral dosing for a combined approach to managing both physical
      and mental pre-race tension.

      results. Topical CBD can be applied directly to sore muscles after
