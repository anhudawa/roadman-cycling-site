    answer: >-
      Riders ran non-standard gearing to cope with the extreme gradients,
      typically pairing a 36-tooth chainring with a 30-tooth cog. This gave them
      a low enough ratio to turn the pedals on pitches that regularly exceeded
      20 percent.
