    answer: >-
      Anthony Walsh argues in this episode that a salary cap is necessary to
      prevent wealthy teams like Ineos Grenadiers from buying up the sport's
      best riders and suffocating competition. Without financial regulation,
      smaller teams cannot assemble the depth required to mount a genuine
      challenge, which reduces the spectacle for fans.
  - question: How are Ineos Grenadiers tactics hurting cycling as a sport?
    answer: >-
      Ineos ride in a highly clinical, power-meter-driven style that prioritises
      control over attacking racing, which Walsh argues strips the sport of
      excitement and unpredictability. Their ability to field multiple Grand
      Tour contenders means rivals are outnumbered on the critical mountain
      stages where races are decided.
