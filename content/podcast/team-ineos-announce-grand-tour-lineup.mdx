  - question: Who is leading Team Ineos at the 2021 Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      Geraint Thomas was announced as Team Ineos Grenadiers' protected leader
      for the 2021 Tour de France. Dave Brailsford cited the race's heavy
      time-trial kilometres and crosswind-exposed opening week as key reasons
      Thomas was selected over teammates.
  - question: >-
      Why is Egan Bernal riding the Giro d'Italia instead of the Tour de France
      in 2021?
    answer: >-
      Dave Brailsford stated that Bernal "needs to find the joy of racing
      again," suggesting the reigning Tour champion was struggling mentally
      rather than physically. Sending him to the Giro rather than defending at
      the Tour was framed as a way to rebuild his confidence in a race
      environment.
  - question: What races is Adam Yates leading for Ineos in 2021?
    answer: >-
      Adam Yates, who joined Team Ineos Grenadiers as a new signing, was given
      leadership of the Vuelta a España. This gave the squad a designated leader
      across all three Grand Tours, with Thomas at the Tour and Bernal at the
      Giro.
  - question: How does Team Ineos' 2021 Grand Tour leadership compare to Jumbo-Visma?
    answer: >-
      Host Anthony Walsh argues that Ineos' four-Grand-Tour-winner roster lacks
      a clear internal hierarchy, which creates structural ambiguity.
      Jumbo-Visma, by contrast, built their entire programme around a single
      protected leader in Primož Roglič, making their team roles far more
      defined and straightforward.
