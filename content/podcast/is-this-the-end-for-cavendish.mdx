faq:
  - question: Did Mark Cavendish retire after Gent-Wevelgem 2020?
    answer: >-
      Cavendish did not formally announce retirement after Gent-Wevelgem 2020,
      but his post-race comments strongly suggested it could be his final race.
      He said "That's perhaps the last race of my career" while visibly
      emotional in the finishing area. At the time, no team had offered him a
      contract for the 2021 season.
  - question: How many Tour de France stage wins did Mark Cavendish have in 2020?
    answer: >-
      Cavendish had 30 Tour de France stage wins at the time of Gent-Wevelgem
      2020, placing him joint second on the all-time list. He also held 15 Giro
      d'Italia stage wins, a Milan-San Remo title, and the 2011 UCI World Road
      Race Championship. These are among the most decorated sprinting numbers in
      the history of professional cycling.
  - question: Why did Mark Cavendish struggle to find a team in 2020?
    answer: >-
      Despite his palmares, no team had offered Cavendish a contract for the
      2021 season as of late 2020, which host Anthony Walsh argues reflects the
      sport pushing him out rather than him choosing to leave. Walsh draws a
      contrast with Bradley Wiggins, who was able to control the terms of his
      own exit from professional cycling. The situation raised questions about
      how the sport treats ageing sprinters who no longer fit a team's immediate
      commercial or sporting needs.
  - question: What happened to Mark Cavendish after 2020?
    answer: >-
      Cavendish signed with Deceuninck-Quick-Step for 2021 and went on to equal
      Eddy Merckx's all-time record of 34 Tour de France stage wins in 2021. He
      then surpassed that record entirely, winning his 35th Tour stage in 2024
      at the age of 39. His comeback is widely regarded as one of the most
      remarkable in professional sport.
