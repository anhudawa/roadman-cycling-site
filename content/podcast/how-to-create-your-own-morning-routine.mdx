    answer: >-
      Start by designing your routine around core principles rather than copying
      someone else's protocol. Anthony Walsh recommends preparing the night
      before, removing your phone from the bedroom, and building in at least one
      deliberate action that sets a purposeful tone for the day ahead.
  - question: Should I keep my phone in another room at night?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Walsh advises keeping your phone powered off in a separate room
      overnight so you do not start the morning in reactive mode. Use an
      analogue alarm clock instead, which removes the temptation to check
      notifications the moment you wake up.
  - question: What is a momentum event in a morning routine?
    answer: >-
      A momentum event is a small, deliberate task you complete early in the
      morning that you would rather avoid, such as a cold shower or making your
      bed. Walsh recommends including one because completing an undesirable task
      first thing generates an early win and establishes a purposeful,
      disciplined tone for the rest of the day.
  - question: How should I prepare the night before to have a better morning?
    answer: >-
      Walsh outlines two key steps: switch to an analogue alarm clock and leave
      your phone off in another room. This preparation means you wake up in
      control of your attention rather than immediately responding to external
      demands from messages or social media.
