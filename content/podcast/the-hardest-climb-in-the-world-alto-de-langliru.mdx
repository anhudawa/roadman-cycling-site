answerCapsule: >-
  Host Anthony Walsh breaks down stage 12 of the 2020 Vuelta a España, when
  British rider Hugh Carthy of EF Education First conquered the Alto de
  l'Angliru, a 12.5-kilometre climb averaging 10.3 percent with pitches up to 24
  percent. Carthy finished 16 seconds clear of Aleksandr Vlasov and Enric Mas,
  becoming the sixth-fastest rider in history up the ascent. The stage shifted
  the red jersey to Richard Carapaz by 10 seconds, while Primož Roglič conceded
  only 26 seconds heading into the following day's time trial.
keyTakeaways:
  - >-
    The Angliru's 24% pitches punish pure climbers — riders who recover fastest
    on the easier 15-16% ramps often take the stage win.
  - >-
    Losing only 10 seconds on the hardest climb in a Grand Tour can still leave
    you firmly in control if a long time trial follows.
  - >-
    Hugh Carthy's win shows that consistent, under-the-radar Grand Tour riders
    can announce themselves on a single mythical climb — form and timing matter.
  - >-
    Don't read a GC leader's bad day on an extreme steep climb as a collapse —
    match their loss to the course profile before writing them off.
  - >-
    When your domestique is strong enough to contest a stage win but stays to
    support you, that team depth is often worth more than any time bonus.

  - question: How hard is the Alto de l'Angliru climb?
    answer: >-
      The Alto de l'Angliru is 12.5 kilometres long with an average gradient of
      10.3 percent and pitches that reach 24 percent on its most brutal
      sections. It is widely regarded as one of the hardest climbs ever used in
      professional cycling, with the final kilometres making it almost
      unrideable for most amateur cyclists.
  - question: Who won the Angliru stage at the 2020 Vuelta a España?
    answer: >-
      Hugh Carthy of EF Education First won the Angliru stage at the 2020 Vuelta
      a España, finishing 16 seconds ahead of Aleksandr Vlasov and Enric Mas.
      His performance made him the sixth-fastest rider in history up the Angliru
      and remains one of the standout rides of his career.
  - question: Did Hugh Carthy win the 2020 Vuelta a España?
    answer: >-
      Hugh Carthy did not win the 2020 Vuelta a España overall. His stage win on
      the Angliru shifted the general classification lead to Richard Carapaz by
      10 seconds, while Primož Roglič conceded only 26 seconds and went on to
      retain a strong position heading into the following day's time trial.
  - question: What is the steepest gradient on the Alto de l'Angliru?
    answer: >-
      The steepest pitches on the Alto de l'Angliru reach 24 percent,
      concentrated in the upper section of the climb. These ramps make it
      near-impossible to maintain momentum and force even elite riders to the
      very limit of what is physically achievable on a bicycle.
