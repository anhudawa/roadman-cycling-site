  - question: Why did the UCI ban the supertuck position in 2021?
    answer: >-
      The UCI banned the supertuck descending position and the forearms-on-bars
      aero posture in April 2021, citing safety concerns. As Anthony Walsh
      argues in this episode, neither position had produced any documented
      crashes, making the ban a solution to a problem that did not exist.
  - question: >-
      What cycling safety issues should the UCI have prioritised instead of
      banning the supertuck?
    answer: >-
      Anthony Walsh identifies several actually dangerous issues the UCI
      overlooked. These include Fabio Jakobsen's near-fatal sprint crash at the
      Tour de Pologne, Remco Evenepoel's bridge fall at Il Lombardia, and the
      hazardous technical finish at E3 Saxo Classic flagged by Sam Bennett as a
      serious risk to riders.
  - question: What happened to Fabio Jakobsen at the Tour de Pologne?
    answer: >-
      Fabio Jakobsen suffered a near-fatal crash during a sprint finish at the
      2020 Tour de Pologne after being forced into a barrier at high speed.
      Walsh uses this incident as a clear example of a documented,
      life-threatening danger that the UCI should have addressed with formal
      rule changes before turning its attention to riding positions.
  - question: Is there a pay gap between men's and women's professional cycling teams?
    answer: >-
      Yes, there is a significant pay disparity between men's and women's
      professional cycling. Taylor Wiles highlighted the issue publicly, and
      Anthony Walsh argues it is one of the most pressing reforms the UCI should
      have acted on rather than focusing on uncontroversial riding positions
      that had caused no recorded harm.
