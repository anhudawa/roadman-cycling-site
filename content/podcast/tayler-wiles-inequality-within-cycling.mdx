  - question: How much do women's professional cyclists get paid?
    answer: >-
      Women's WorldTour minimum salaries remain significantly lower than those
      in the men's peloton, with many riders unable to live on their cycling
      income alone. Tayler Wiles discusses this directly in the podcast, noting
      that some women's professionals rely on second jobs or a partner's income
      to sustain their racing career. The UCI has made incremental improvements
      to minimum wage requirements, but a substantial structural gap remains.
  - question: What is the difference between men's and women's cycling pay?
    answer: >-
      Men's WorldTour riders benefit from minimum salaries that far exceed those
      set for the women's WorldTour, even at the lower end of the scale. Tayler
      Wiles explains that this disparity is not simply about prize money but
      reflects the overall investment, sponsorship, and media coverage directed
      at the men's peloton. Many top women's riders compete at the same physical
      and professional standard as their male counterparts but earn a fraction
      of the equivalent wage.

      peloton does not fall into that category.
