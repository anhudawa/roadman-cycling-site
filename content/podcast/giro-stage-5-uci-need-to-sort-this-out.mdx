keyTakeaways:
  - >-
    Road furniture inside the final 4km is a genuine safety hazard — push for
    races you organise locally to strip obstacles from sprint finishes.
  - >-
    The UCI spends enforcement energy on aero tucks and littering while kamikaze
    finishes go unchecked — know the difference between rule-following and
    actual safety.
  - >-
    Bike handling is the skill most exposed as you move up racing categories; if
    you're getting squeezed and panicking in the bunch, that's the gap to close
    first.
  - >-
    At around 8–10km to go in a bunch race, positioning fights peak before any
    team commits to the front — expect that window to be the most dangerous part
    of the race.
  - >-
    When a GC favourite abandons due to avoidable course design, every sponsor,
    support staffer, and training block behind them is wasted — race
    organisation decisions have real consequences.
