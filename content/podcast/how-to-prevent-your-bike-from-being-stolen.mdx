faq:
  - question: How do I stop my bike being stolen outside a café?
    answer: >-
      Park your bike in full view of where you are sitting inside the café so
      you can watch it at all times. Tangle your bike together with other
      riders' bikes, use your helmet strap to lock your wheel to the frame, and
      loosen the front skewer so the bike cannot be ridden away quickly.
      Carrying a lightweight cable lock and combination padlock in your
      handlebar bag gives you a reliable physical deterrent for café stops.
  - question: What is the best lightweight lock for road cycling?
    answer: >-
      A thin cable lock paired with a combination padlock is the most practical
      option for road cyclists, as it adds minimal weight to your kit. Store it
      in a handlebar bag so it is always accessible without affecting your
      bike's handling. It is not impenetrable, but it significantly slows down
      an opportunistic thief.
  - question: Does Garmin have a bike theft alarm?
    answer: >-
      Yes, most Garmin cycling computers include a built-in movement alarm that
      triggers if the bike is disturbed while you are stopped. Activating this
      feature at café stops adds an extra layer of deterrence against
      opportunistic thieves. It is a simple setting worth switching on every
      ride.
  - question: How was Geraint Thomas's bike stolen?
    answer: >-
      Geraint Thomas, the Tour de France champion, had his bike taken outside a
      café during a routine ride stop. The theft was opportunistic, highlighting
      that even high-profile professional cyclists are vulnerable when bikes are
      left unattended or out of sight. Simple precautions like choosing a
      visible parking spot and using a cable lock would reduce the risk
      considerably.
