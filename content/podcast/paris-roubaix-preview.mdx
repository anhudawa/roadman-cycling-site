    answer: >-
      Wout van Aert and Mathieu van der Poel are the clear favourites heading
      into the 2021 edition. Julian Alaphilippe is considered a wildcard

  - question: What is the weather forecast for Paris-Roubaix 2021?
    answer: >-
      Heavy rain is expected throughout the 2021 race, making it the first wet
      edition of Paris-Roubaix since 1985. The wet cobbles significantly change
      the dynamics of the race, favouring riders with a cyclocross background
      who are comfortable handling bikes in slippery conditions.
  - question: What is the argument between Wout van Aert and Remco Evenepoel about?
    answer: >-
      The feud stems from Belgium's failed team tactics at the 2021 Road World
      Championships, where the two riders publicly clashed over how the race was
      ridden. Host Anthony Walsh sides with Van Aert in the dispute, framing
      Evenepoel's public comments as damaging to team cohesion ahead of a major
      cobbled Classic.
  - question: Does rain affect Paris-Roubaix and who does it help?
    answer: >-
      Rain makes the cobbled sectors far more treacherous and technically
      demanding, rewarding riders who can handle unpredictable surfaces at
      speed. Cyclocross specialists such as Van Aert and Van der Poel are
      considered the primary beneficiaries, though riders like Stybar and Sagan
      with long cobbled racing experience also benefit from wet conditions.
