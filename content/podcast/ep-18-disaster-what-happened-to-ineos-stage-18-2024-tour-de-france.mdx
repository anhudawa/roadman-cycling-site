faq:
  - question: What happened to INEOS Grenadiers at the 2024 Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      INEOS Grenadiers arrived at the 2024 Tour de France without a genuine GC
      contender, a stark contrast to their dominance between 2012 and 2019 when
      they won seven Tours under the Team Sky banner. The Roadman Cycling
      Podcast attributes the decline to internal friction, Dave Brailsford
      stepping back into a broader Ineos Sport role, and reduced autonomy for
      coach Rod Ellingworth.
  - question: Why has INEOS gone from winning the Tour de France to struggling?
    answer: >-
      Several factors compounded at once: Egan Bernal's serious crash removed
      their most promising GC leader, Brailsford shifted focus away from
      day-to-day team operations, and internal structural changes reduced the
      cohesion that defined the Team Sky era. This decline is particularly
      striking given that INEOS still operate on the largest budget in
      professional cycling, reportedly around 55 million euros.
  - question: Who won Stage 18 of the 2024 Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      Stage 18 of the 2024 Tour de France was won from a breakaway rather than
      by one of the GC favourites. The Roadman Cycling Podcast used the stage
      result as a starting point to analyse the broader collapse of INEOS as a
      competitive Tour de France team.
  - question: What is Dave Brailsford's role at INEOS now?
    answer: >-
      Brailsford has moved into a wider role across Ineos Sport, the umbrella
      organisation that covers multiple sporting ventures, meaning he is no
      longer as hands-on with the cycling team as he was during the Team Sky
      years. The Roadman Cycling Podcast argues this shift in focus has
      contributed directly to the team losing its competitive edge at Grand Tour
      level.
