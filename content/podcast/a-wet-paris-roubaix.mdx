  - question: Who won the 2021 Paris-Roubaix?
    answer: >-
      Sonny Colbrelli won the 2021 Paris-Roubaix, his first ever appearance in
      the race. The Italian beat Florian Vermeersch and Mathieu van der Poel in
      a dramatic three-way sprint finish at the Roubaix velodrome.
  - question: Why was the 2021 Paris-Roubaix so special?
    answer: >-
      It was the first wet edition of Paris-Roubaix in 19 years, producing
      treacherous cobblestone conditions that caused crashes, mechanicals, and
      disc brake failures throughout the peloton. Anthony Walsh of the Roadman
      Cycling Podcast called it the best bike race he had ever seen, with drama
      at nearly every turn including Gianni Moscon losing a winning position to
      a puncture and crash.
  - question: Why did Wout van Aert not win Paris-Roubaix 2021?
    answer: >-
      Wout van Aert arrived at Paris-Roubaix overraced and mentally drained
      after an extremely heavy Classics programme. Anthony Walsh argued this
      made him a poor favourite and backed Sonny Colbrelli at 40-to-1 odds
      before the race started.
  - question: What happened with disc brake failures at Paris-Roubaix 2021?
    answer: >-
      Multiple teams suffered disc brake failures during the wet 2021
      Paris-Roubaix, raising serious questions about the reliability of disc
      brakes in extreme cobblestone conditions. The failures were widespread
      enough to be a significant talking point after the race, though the full
      causes were not conclusively explained at the time.
