  kilometres on Strava between Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, with over

  long-distance riding.

    nightmare ride on New Year's Eve.
  - >-

  - question: What is the Rapha Festive 500 challenge?
    answer: >-
      The Rapha Festive 500 is a cycling challenge where you must ride 500
      kilometres and log them on Strava between Christmas Eve and New Year's
      Eve. Now in its 11th year, the event attracted over 120,000 participants
      in the previous edition and is free to enter through the Rapha website.
  - question: How do you complete the Rapha Festive 500 without ruining Christmas?
    answer: >-
      Front-load your mileage by riding as much as possible on Christmas Eve
      before family commitments begin. Anthony Walsh recommends scheduling rides
      around social and family obligations rather than trying to cram kilometres
      in at the last minute, which can leave you facing a 190-kilometre effort
      on New Year's Eve.
  - question: Is it better to ride solo or in a group for the Festive 500?
    answer: >-
      Riding in a group is strongly recommended. Sharing pace work reduces the
      physical load on each rider and makes long winter kilometres pass far more
      quickly than grinding through them alone.
  - question: >-
      How many kilometres per day do you need to ride to complete the Festive
      500?
    answer: >-

      Front-loading bigger rides early in the period makes the final days far
      more manageable and removes the pressure of a large catch-up ride on New
      Year's Eve.
