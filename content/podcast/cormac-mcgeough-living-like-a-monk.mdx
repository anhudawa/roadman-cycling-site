  - question: What is the difference between a Continental and WorldTour cycling team?
    answer: >-
      Continental teams sit one tier below WorldTour squads in professional
      cycling's hierarchy and typically race smaller events with fewer
      guaranteed race invitations. McGeough argues that the actual strength gap
      between Continental and WorldTour riders in the peloton is much narrower
      than the jersey categories imply, and that dismissive attitudes from
      WorldTour riders often mask the fact that they are also suffering deeply
      in the same races.
