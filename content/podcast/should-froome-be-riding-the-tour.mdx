    answer: >-
      Anthony Walsh argues Froome deserves his place at the 2022 Tour based on
      his four Tour de France victories and the experience that comes with that

  - question: What does a road captain do in professional cycling?
    answer: >-
      A road captain is a senior rider who protects the team leader through
      technically dangerous sections of a race, including crosswinds,
      cobblestone stages and high-speed bunch sprints. The role has
      traditionally been filled by specialists like Luke Rowe and Tony Martin,
      who are built for sustained, physical domestique work rather than grand
      tour leadership.
  - question: Can Chris Froome still compete at Grand Tour level after his crash?
    answer: >-
      Froome has not returned to anywhere near his pre-crash form since his
      near-fatal accident at the 2019 Critérium du Dauphiné. His preparation
      results heading into the 2022 Tour were poor, making it highly likely he
      will be fighting to stay within the time cuts rather than influencing the
      race tactically for leader Mike Woods.
  - question: Is naming Froome as road captain at the Tour de France a mistake?
    answer: >-
      Walsh believes the road captain title creates more problems than it solves
      for Israel Start-Up Nation. Froome has no history performing the physical,
      protective domestique function the role requires, so attaching that label
      to him sets an expectation the team cannot realistically expect him to
      meet in his current condition.
