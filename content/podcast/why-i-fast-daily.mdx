faq:
  - question: What is 16/8 intermittent fasting?
    answer: >-
      16/8 intermittent fasting means eating all your meals within an eight-hour
      window and fasting for the remaining sixteen hours each day. Anthony Walsh
      follows this protocol daily, citing reduced caloric intake and cellular
      repair as the two core reasons for doing so.
  - question: >-
      Does intermittent fasting help with weight loss if you have a sedentary
      lifestyle?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Compressing your eating into an eight-hour window naturally lowers
      total caloric intake, which helps offset the reduced energy demands of a
      sedentary lifestyle. Walsh uses this as one of his two primary
      justifications for following a daily 16/8 protocol.
  - question: What is autophagy and how does fasting trigger it?
    answer: >-
      Autophagy is a cellular repair process in which the body identifies and
      clears damaged or dysfunctional cells, including those associated with
      ageing and pre-cancerous changes. When digestion pauses during a fasting
      period, the body redirects resources toward this repair mechanism, making
      daily fasting a practical way to activate it regularly.
  - question: Can you drink coffee while intermittent fasting?
    answer: >-
      Black coffee and other non-caloric drinks are permitted during the fasting
      window without breaking the fast. Walsh advises avoiding drinks sweetened
      with aspartame, as it can still provoke an insulin response even though it
      contains no calories.
