  - question: How many Tour de France stage wins does Mark Cavendish have?
    answer: >-
      As of Stage 10 of the 2021 Tour de France, Mark Cavendish had 33 stage
      wins, putting him one victory behind Eddy Merckx's all-time record of 34.
      He took his third win of the 2021 race on the 190.5km stage from
      Albertville to Valence.
  - question: How does the Deceuninck Quick-Step lead-out train work in a sprint finish?
    answer: >-
      Deceuninck–Quick-Step's lead-out is built backwards from the sprinter's
      200-metre launch point, with each rider peeling off at a pre-assigned
      distance to deliver Cavendish at maximum speed. Each domestique
      essentially rides themselves into the red so that Cavendish hits the final
      straight with the best possible momentum. The Stage 10 finish to Valence
      was a textbook example of this method working to perfection.
  - question: What is Lachlan Morton's Alt Tour and how much money did it raise?
    answer: >-
      Lachlan Morton's Alt Tour is an unsupported solo ride covering the full
      Tour de France route, including all transfer stages that the peloton skips
      by bus. By Stage 10 of the 2021 race, Morton had raised over £224,000 for
      World Bicycle Relief, a charity that provides bicycles to people in
      developing countries.
