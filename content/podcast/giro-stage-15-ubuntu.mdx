  - question: What does Ubuntu mean in cycling and why does Qhubeka use it?
    answer: >-
      Ubuntu is a Southern African philosophy meaning "I am because we are",
      reflecting collective identity and shared success. Qhubeka-Assos adopted
      it as their team motto to reflect both their collaborative racing style
      and their wider mission to put African children on bicycles through
      charitable work.

  - question: How does a breakaway know when to stop working together and start racing?
    answer: >-
      The alliance between breakaway riders typically holds as long as the
      peloton remains a threat behind them. Once a sufficient gap is established
      and the finish is close enough, the dynamic shifts from collaborative
      pacemaking to direct competition, with each rider looking to time their
      attack to win.
