faq:
  - question: does fasted cycling help burn belly fat
    answer: >-
      Fasted zone-one or zone-two riding for 30 to 45 minutes forces the body to
      draw on stored fatty acids for fuel rather than dietary carbohydrates.
      Anthony Walsh recommends pairing this with black coffee or green tea
      beforehand, as the caffeine further mobilises free fatty acids and
      increases the rate of fat oxidation during the session.
  - question: does cold water immersion help with weight loss
    answer: >-
      Three to five minutes of cold water immersion stimulates the conversion of
      white adipose tissue into brown adipose tissue, which is metabolically
      active and burns calories to generate heat. Anthony Walsh includes this as
      one of five physiological tactics for reducing body fat, noting that the
      effect is distinct from calorie restriction and works through a separate
      hormonal pathway.
  - question: what supplements help control blood sugar to lose weight
    answer: >-
      Ceylon cinnamon, apple cider vinegar taken before carbohydrate-heavy
      meals, and bitter melon extract are three well-researched options for
      blunting post-meal blood sugar spikes. Anthony Walsh recommends these
      specifically because sustained high blood sugar drives insulin release,
      which promotes fat storage and makes waist reduction significantly harder.
  - question: how does breathwork reduce cortisol and help with fat loss
    answer: >-
      Elevated cortisol signals the body to store fat, particularly around the
      abdomen, so lowering it through structured breathwork directly supports
      waist reduction. Anthony Walsh draws on James Nestor's research to outline
      a breathing protocol that shifts the nervous system away from a stress
      response, reducing cortisol and the metabolic disruption that accompanies
      it.
