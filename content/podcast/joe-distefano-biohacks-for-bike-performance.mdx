  - question: What is the 4-7-8 breathing technique and does it work for athletes?
    answer: >-
      The 4-7-8 technique involves inhaling for 4 counts, holding the breath for
      7, and exhaling for 8. Joe DiStefano uses it as a rapid state-change
      protocol to shift athletes from a sympathetic stress response into
      parasympathetic recovery, which is directly useful before races or between
      intervals.
  - question: How long does it actually take to return to sport after a skull fracture?
    answer: >-
      Joe DiStefano experienced this himself after a severe basketball-related
      skull fracture and found that medical clearance for "full recovery" does
      not equate to readiness for athletic performance. Rebuilding coordination,
      output, and mental resilience after serious head trauma can take
      considerably longer than a doctor's discharge timeline suggests, and
      targeted protocols like breathwork and EFT tapping can help bridge that
      gap.

    answer: >-

      stress. Joe DiStefano used it as part of his rehabilitation from a skull
      fracture and dystonia diagnosis, and recommends it to athletes dealing
      with performance anxiety, pain, or recovery from injury.
