faq:
  - question: What happened at Tour de France 2021 Stage 3?
    answer: >-
      Stage 3 of the 2021 Tour de France, a 183km run from Lorient to Pontivy,
      was disrupted by multiple serious crashes on the narrow roads of Brittany.
      Primož Roglič lost over a minute, Geraint Thomas suffered a separated
      shoulder, Robert Gesink abandoned, and both Caleb Ewan and Peter Sagan
      crashed in the final sprint, which was won by Tim Merlier.
  - question: Why were there so many crashes at the 2021 Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      The narrow, technical roads of Brittany gave the peloton very little
      margin for error, and the high-speed, nervous racing in the opening stages
      compounded the danger. Anthony Walsh argues the repeated crashes were not
      simply bad luck but a structural failure in race organisation, pointing to
      the rider union's ignored request to extend the neutralised finish zone
      from 3km to 8km as a clear missed opportunity to reduce the carnage.
  - question: How do you stop crashes at the Tour de France?
    answer: >-
      One concrete step is applying the 3km safety rule at a greater distance
      from the finish. The rider union specifically requested an 8km
      neutralisation zone for the 2021 Brittany stages, a request that was not
      acted upon. Extending that buffer would prevent GC riders losing time and
      suffering injuries caused by sprint-finish pile-ups they have no direct
      involvement in.
  - question: Did Geraint Thomas crash at the 2021 Tour de France Stage 3?
    answer: >-
      Yes, Geraint Thomas crashed during Stage 3 and suffered a separated
      shoulder. He continued racing but the injury left him compromised for the
      remainder of the race. It was part of a wave of incidents that affected
      nearly every major general classification contender across the first three
      stages.
