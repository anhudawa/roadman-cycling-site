faq:
  - question: >-
      Why did Mathieu van der Poel crash at the Tokyo Olympics mountain bike
      race?
    answer: >-
      Van der Poel misjudged a jump section on lap one because a wooden ramp
      present during his course reconnaissance had been removed before race day
      without his knowledge. The communication failure meant he approached the
      feature expecting a different take-off, sending him over the bars and
      ending his medal hopes. His teammate Niki Terpstra confirmed the same
      issue had occurred as far back as the 2019 test event.
  - question: Who won the Olympic mountain bike race Tokyo 2021?
    answer: >-
      Tom Pidcock took gold in the men's cross-country mountain bike race at the
      Tokyo Olympics. Mathias Flückiger finished second and David Valero took
      bronze. Pre-race favourite Van der Poel crashed out on the opening lap and
      was unable to continue.
  - question: Was the Tokyo Olympics MTB course ramp removed before the race?
    answer: >-
      Yes, a wooden ramp on the course was present during reconnaissance but
      removed ahead of race day without adequate communication to riders. Van
      der Poel publicly stated he was unaware of the change, and the lack of
      notification from organisers is widely considered a significant failure in
      race management.
  - question: Who won the mens road race at the Tokyo Olympics 2021?
    answer: >-
      Richard Carapaz won the men's road race at the Tokyo Olympics. His
      tactical patience in a breakaway group proved decisive, with more powerful
      riders like Wout van Aert and Tadej Pogačar unable to overcome the
      deficit. Anthony Walsh argues in the episode that Carapaz's race craft
      beat raw power on the day.
