  - question: What is a Huberman husband in cycling?
    answer: >-
      A "Huberman husband" refers to an amateur athlete, typically male, who
      becomes obsessed with optimising micro-habits such as cold exposure,
      supplement stacking and sleep tracking after following influencers like
      Andrew Huberman. Sarah and Anthony argue on Rider Support that this
      obsession often distracts cyclists from the high-impact fundamentals
      that actually drive performance: training volume, consistent sleep and
      solid nutrition.
  - question: Does optimising micro-habits improve cycling performance?
    answer: >-
      For amateur cyclists, micro-habit optimisation produces minimal gains
      compared to the basics. The Rider Support hosts make the case that
      increasing weekly training volume, prioritising sleep quality and eating
      enough of the right foods will outperform any supplement or biohacking
      protocol for the vast majority of riders.
