People have asked us about camps for about three years.

The answer was always the same: not until we could do it the way we'd want to do it ourselves.

October 2026, we're finally doing it.

Two camps in Girona. One road, one gravel, back-to-back. Same private farmhouse for both. Sixteen riders per camp, three of us hosting. That's the whole shape of it.

- **Road camp** — 10 to 15 October 2026
- **Gravel camp** — 16 to 21 October 2026
- **€995 per camp.** €1,700 for both back-to-back (saves €290).
- **Sixteen spots each.** First-come.

The longer version — why Girona, why now, what the rides actually look like — is in the announcement post:
