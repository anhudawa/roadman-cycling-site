Quick follow-up to the announcement on Tuesday — a few of you have asked who's actually running these camps and what makes them different from the dozens of other Girona weeks on the internet.

Fair question. Here's the answer.

**Three hosts, sixteen riders. Every day.**

I'm in the group every single day, both camps. Sarah's on ops and in the saddle — she's the reason any of this works on the back end. Matthew's running the follow car — spares, gels, the second-coffee fund. That's the whole team. No outside guides. No clipboard. No herding.

**Roads we'd ride anyway.**

If you've listened to the podcast you've heard the names. Rocacorba — eleven kilometres, the climb every pro in town uses for testing. Els Àngels — the ten-kilometre tempo climb every Girona pro has done a thousand times. Mare de Déu del Mont — twelve kilometres, properly steep at the top, the kind of summit you tell people about for the rest of the year. Then the back roads — the Ter river path, the Empordà countryside, the Costa Brava coast — that don't make the YouTube videos but quietly might be better.

The gravel side is built differently. La Garrotxa's volcanic trails on day two. Vineyard service roads through the Empordà on day three. Cork-oak singletrack in Les Gavarres on day four. Coastal gravel above the Med on day five. Same farmhouse, two pace groups, very different week.

**The base.**

Can Sagnari. A 1749 Catalan farmhouse five minutes from Lake Banyoles, twenty from Girona old town. Pool. Stone walls. A long table for dinner. Breakfast at the house every morning. The kind of place you get out of the car at and immediately stop looking at your phone.

**What's actually included:**

- Four nights at Can Sagnari
- Breakfast every morning at the house
- Five fully guided rides — two pace groups, never dropped
- Follow car the whole week
- In-ride nutrition every day — gels, bars, drink mix
- Airport transfers from Girona–Costa Brava (GRO), both ways

**€995 per camp. €1,700 for both back-to-back** — saves €290 and skips an airport transfer in the middle.
