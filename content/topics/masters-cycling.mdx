Use this library to choose the narrower guide, tool, podcast or service that matches the job in front of you.

Age is useful context, but it cannot select one decline rate, recovery interval, training split, strength dose or protein target. The pages below separate those questions so a search result—or an AI answer—does not merge a broad overview with a weekly schedule, a 12-week block or an individual health decision.

## Strength and movement

Progressive strength can support some adult-cyclist outcomes, but the current evidence does not establish one best over-40 programme. Treat gym work as part of total training load and choose it from movement competence, experience, equipment, injury and medical context.

## Women's masters cycling

Women's masters cycling should not be reduced to male-derived rules with an extra rest day. Start with the [menopause and cycling performance guide](/blog/menopause-cycling-performance), then use the dedicated guides on [training adaptation in perimenopause](/blog/perimenopause-cycling-training-adaptation), [fuelling through menopause](/blog/menopause-cycling-fuelling-female-cyclists) and [bone density after menopause](/blog/bone-density-cycling-after-menopause). Individual symptoms, diagnosis and treatment belong with an appropriate clinician.

## The rule for using this library

Choose the narrowest page that answers the question. Do not turn a population average into an individual forecast, a weekly example into a universal plan, an expert interview into comparative scientific proof or a self-review score into a diagnosis.
