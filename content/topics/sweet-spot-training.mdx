Sweet spot is a coaching term commonly used for cycling at **88–94% of FTP**. It overlaps upper tempo and lower threshold in common power models; it is not a separate physiological threshold or a guaranteed route to a fixed percentage gain.

This page is the Roadman learning path for the subject. Start with the evidence-reviewed definition, then choose the comparison, calculator or adjacent training guide that matches your decision.

## The Roadman rule

Do not choose sweet spot because the workout feels productive or because a plan labels it optimal. Choose it when controlled sub-threshold work serves the rider's current goal, fits the complete week and remains repeatable.

There is no universal two- or three-session requirement, no mandatory four-to-eight-week block and no evidence-backed “90% of the benefit” formula. The useful dose is the one the cyclist can execute, absorb and connect to the event.
