# /go Hero Video — Piece to Camera Script

**Shoot date:** 13 May 2026
**Speaker:** Anthony Walsh
**Duration target:** 45–60 seconds (primary) / 20 seconds (short cut)
**Word count:** ~185 words (primary) / ~55 words (short)
**Purpose:** Build instant trust with cold PPC traffic. Position diagnostic as zero-risk first step.
**Page context:** Visitor arrived from Google Ads ("cycling coach", "FTP stuck", etc.). They have three other tabs open. They don't know who Anthony is.

---

## Setting Notes

Dark background, clean. Roadman purple or charcoal — nothing busy. Alternatively: post-ride, jersey still on, slightly flushed. Either works. The key is Anthony looks like a cyclist, not a marketer. No desk. No whiteboard. Camera at eye level, tight frame (chest up). Natural light or single soft key.

---

## PRIMARY SCRIPT — 45–60 seconds

*[Anthony looks directly at camera. Relaxed, slight lean forward. Talking to one person, not an audience.]*

I'm Anthony Walsh. I run the Roadman Cycling Podcast — about a hundred million downloads at this point.

*[Beat.]*

And I keep seeing the same pattern.

Cyclists come to me — serious cyclists, training eight, ten, twelve hours a week — and their FTP hasn't moved in a year. Sometimes two.

*[Slight head shake. Genuine.]*

They're not lazy. They're not undertrained. They're usually doing one of four things wrong. And it's almost always fixable.

*[Leans in slightly.]*

I built this diagnostic because I got tired of watching people throw money at coaching plans before anyone had actually identified the problem.

It takes four minutes. Twelve questions. We'll tell you exactly which of the four plateau patterns you match — and what to do about it.

*[Small pause. Direct.]*

It's built on methods I've picked up from coaches like Dan Lorang — that's Pogacar's coach — Professor Seiler, Dr. David Dunne. The people I've spent the last few years interviewing.

*[Slight nod toward the CTA below.]*

Take it. It's free. No email to start. And if you're stuck — I'd genuinely like to help you get unstuck.

*[Holds eye contact for a beat, then cut.]*

## Delivery Notes

**Pace:** Conversational. Not rushed, not performative. The way you'd explain it to someone at a coffee stop who just told you they're frustrated with their training.

**Energy arc:** Calm authority at the open → genuine frustration on behalf of the stuck rider in the middle → confident and warm on the close.

**Namedrops:** Throw Lorang, Seiler, Dunne away mid-sentence. Don't pause for them. Don't list them like credentials on a CV. They should feel like mates you're referencing, not trophies.

**Eye line:** Straight to lens the whole time. No looking off-camera, no cuts. One take, one person.

**What to avoid:** Don't smile too much at the top — you're not selling. Don't speed up at the end — the CTA should feel like a favour, not a pitch. Don't say "we" until the diagnostic section — the open is "I", establishing the person behind the brand.

**If it runs long:** Cut "It's built on methods..." paragraph. The short version proves the script works without it. Only include the expert namedrop if the take feels natural at full length.
