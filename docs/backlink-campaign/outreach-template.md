# Guest Backlink Outreach Templates

Three email templates for reaching out to past podcast guests. Pick the one that fits the situation. Copy, paste, personalise, send.

---

## Template A — Basic (episode page + expert profile)

Use this for any guest who has both a /guests/ page and an /experts/ page on the site.

**Subject:** Your episode page on Roadman Cycling

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Anthony asked me to send this across — your episode with us has a dedicated page on the site:

- Your guest page: https://www.roadmancycling.com/guests/[slug]
- Your expert profile: https://www.roadmancycling.com/experts/[slug]

Both pages rank for your name alongside cycling and performance topics. They include your bio, key quotes from the episode, and links out to your work.

If you'd like to link to either from your site, newsletter, or bio — that'd be appreciated. Completely up to you.

Best,
Sarah
Roadman Cycling

---

## Template B — With training guide or tool mention

Use this when the guest's area of expertise connects to a training guide, calculator, or resource on the site. Check the guest's expert profile to see which topic pages exist.

**Subject:** Your expert page + a new [topic] guide on Roadman

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Anthony asked me to send this over. We've built out your expert profile on the site — it covers your key positions on [topic area] and links to your episode appearances:

- Your expert profile: https://www.roadmancycling.com/experts/[slug]
- Related guide: https://www.roadmancycling.com/topics/[topic-slug]

The expert page specifically references your work on [specific idea from their keyIdeas — e.g. "polarised training and the 80/20 intensity split"]. Both pages are ranking for search terms related to your name and [topic].

If any of these are useful to link to from your site or share with your audience, we'd be glad of it. No pressure.

Best,
Sarah
Roadman Cycling

---

## Template C — Follow-up (2 weeks after initial email)

Short. No guilt. One nudge only — if they don't reply to this, move on.

**Subject:** Re: Your episode page on Roadman Cycling

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Just bumping this in case it got buried. Here are the links again:

- [episode or expert URL]

Happy to update anything on the page if needed — bio, photo, links to your current work. Just let me know.

Best,
Sarah

---

## Notes for Sarah

- Always personalise the first line. "Anthony asked me to send this across" is the warm opener — use it every time.
- Do not send Template C if they replied to Template A or B, even if they said no.
- If a guest replies and asks for changes to their page (updated bio, new photo, corrected URL), note it in the tracking sheet under "Notes" and flag it for the team.
- If a guest asks what kind of link they should add, suggest: a "Featured in" or "As seen on" section on their about page, or a mention in a newsletter. Don't prescribe exact anchor text — let them write it naturally.
- Never chase more than once. One Template A or B, one Template C. That's it.
