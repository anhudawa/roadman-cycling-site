## Why these 20 first

These guests share three things:

1. **They have their own website or organisation site** — not just social media. A backlink from a real domain carries SEO weight.
2. **Their domain is topically relevant** — coaching, nutrition, sports science, cycling. Google values topical relevance in backlinks far more than raw domain authority from an unrelated site.
3. **They have substantial pages on our site to link to** — not just a thin guest page, but curated expert profiles with editorial content, topic pages, and featured articles. When someone links to us, the page they land on needs to be worth the click.

## Second tier (after you've worked through the top 20)

Guests with strong public profiles who may not have personal websites but have:
- Active newsletters or Substacks
- Coaching platforms with client-facing pages
- LinkedIn profiles with portfolio or featured sections
- Podcast appearances pages on their team or org site

These include: Olav Bu, John Wakefield, Vasilis Anastopoulos, Dr David Lipman, Dr Michael Gervais, Chris Voss, Colin O'Brady, David Millar, Brian Smith, Alistair Brownlee, Erin Ayala, Michael Ormsbee.

For pro riders (Greg LeMond, Lachlan Morton, Andre Greipel, George Hincapie, etc.), the link would typically come from their team site or personal brand site. These are worth pursuing but harder to land — a team's web admin needs to action it, which adds a layer of friction.
