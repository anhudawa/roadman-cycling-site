# Cold-water immersion search owner — GSC decision (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cold-water-immersion-cyclists` as the single broad owner for cold
water immersion, ice baths and cold plunges for cyclists. The direct answer
`/answers/do-ice-baths-help-cycling-recovery` remains a concise supporting
surface and now links to the reviewed owner.

No second editorial page competes for the broad query, so this phase is an
evidence and CTR rebuild rather than a consolidation or redirect.

## Search Console baseline

Latest three-month Search Console view on 31 August 2026:

| URL | Clicks | Web impressions | CTR | Average position | Google AI-feature impressions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `/blog/cold-water-immersion-cyclists` | 27 | 2,443 | 1.1% | 6.6 | 646 |

Visible queries included `cold plunge for cyclists`, `tour de france ice bath`
and research-led searches about post-exercise immersion and sleep. The current
page-one position and low CTR justify strengthening the existing URL.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the owner around a direct decision table and separate acute recovery,
  immediate performance, long-term strength, endurance adaptation, protocol and
  safety questions.
- Removed the blanket claim that cold after training cancels adaptation. The
  clearest review-level caution is frequent post-strength immersion; chronic
  endurance reviews have not shown reduced aerobic performance adaptation.
- Removed claims that cold showers deliver a smaller version of the same effect,
  that one protocol is optimal, and that professional use proves benefit.
- Added a strong cold-shock boundary for cardiovascular strain, arrhythmia,
  breath-holding, face immersion and solo open-water risk.
- Checked claims against acute-recovery meta-analyses, chronic adaptation meta-
  analyses, exercise-specific protocol reviews, American Heart Association
  guidance and cold-shock physiology.
- Aligned the recovery hub and the two principal direct-answer surfaces with the
  reviewed owner.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=cold-water-guide` inside the existing
  single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added bounded LLM discovery, curated IndexNow discovery and AI benchmark
  prompt 364.

## Measurement

Compare rolling 28-day and three-month performance after recrawl:

- clicks, impressions, CTR and average position for the owner;
- non-brand queries containing `cold water`, `ice bath`, `cold plunge`,
  `cycling`, `cyclist`, `recovery`, `adaptation`, `strength` and `safety`;
- Google AI-feature impressions and monthly citation results for prompt 364;
- app-waitlist conversions attributed to `cold-water-guide`; and
- emergence of another page for the same broad intent.

IndexNow supports participating engines. Google discovery continues through the
canonical URL, internal links, sitemap and normal Search Console workflows.
