# Strength training for cyclists search-owner decision — 26 August 2026

## Decision

Keep and strengthen the established broad-query owner:

`/blog/cycling-strength-training-guide`

Permanently redirect the near-duplicate:

`/blog/strength-training-cyclists-complete-guide`

Keep `/topics/cycling-strength-conditioning` as the differentiated research
library. Keep exercise, beginner, masters, in-season and minimum-dose articles
for their narrower intents, and make them link to the broad owner.

## Problems found

- Two pages used near-identical `Strength Training for Cyclists` complete-guide
  titles and targeted the same broad query.
- Both converted a low-certainty research result into universal instructions
  for frequency, repetitions, exercise choice, scheduling and maintenance.
- The later duplicate claimed the 17-study meta-analysis contained a compelling
  masters subset. The published abstract reports no such subgroup.
- The pages said strength training cannot add mass even though a cited elite
  cyclist study reported an increase in lower-body lean mass.
- Injury prevention, guaranteed FTP transfer and fixed loss-of-gain timelines
  were presented without evidence from the cited cyclist-performance review.
- The topic and pillar pages repeated the same head-term answer and
  overconfident claims instead of serving navigation and library intent.
- Internal links reinforced both broad pages, dividing sitewide signals.

## Changes prepared

- Rewrote the established owner around the exact query and the latest
  cyclist-only meta-analysis (17 studies, 262 participants, 60 women).
- Added a direct answer, evidence table, effect sizes, explicit certainty
  rating, adaptable starting plan, masters boundary, scheduling evidence,
  FAQs, human review trail and visible primary sources.
- Distinguished statistically significant effects for performance, efficiency
  and anaerobic power from non-significant VO2max, pVO2max, maximal-steady-state
  and anaerobic-capacity outcomes.
- Labelled two sessions per week as a practical starting hypothesis, not a
  research-proven minimum; the review included 1–3 weekly sessions and found no
  significant frequency moderator.
- Removed the duplicate source page and added a permanent redirect to the
  established owner.
- Repointed internal links and content relationships to the owner.
- Rebuilt the strength topic as a question-led research library and
  differentiated the strength pillar as collection/navigation intent.
- Pinned the owner in both LLM discovery documents, added the owner cluster to
  IndexNow and added high-priority AI benchmark prompt 236.
