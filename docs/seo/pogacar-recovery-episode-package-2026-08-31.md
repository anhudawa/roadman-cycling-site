# Pogačar recovery episode knowledge package — 31 August 2026

## Why this page

Google Search Console recorded 9,910 impressions and 46 clicks for queries
containing "recovery" in the three months to 29 August 2026: 0.5% CTR at an
average position of 6.5. The active-recovery guide is already the search owner,
so this phase upgrades an adjacent podcast result and sends users into that
owner, Roadman's recovery tools and the single app waiting list.

The episode audit started at 79/100. The missing fields were chapters,
`citationsReviewed` and `guestBio`. More importantly, retrievable summaries
repeated a 15-minute refuelling deadline, one mandatory 4:1 ratio, guaranteed
tart-cherry soreness relief, immune protection from layers and recovery claims
for stretching that the reviewed evidence does not support.

## Evidence boundary

- Active cool-downs lower blood lactate faster but do not consistently improve
  later recovery or performance.
- Rapid refuelling is most urgent when the next demanding session is under four
  hours away. Position guidance supports roughly 1.2 g/kg/hour carbohydrate, or
  0.8 g/kg/hour carbohydrate with 0.2-0.4 g/kg/hour protein, for that scenario.
- The 2026 tart-cherry review found selected possible benefits alongside high
  heterogeneity and low-to-moderate certainty; soreness was not significantly
  improved in the pooled analysis.
- Layers are a practical comfort and temperature-management choice, not proof of
  immune protection.
- Post-exercise stretching alone has not shown a significant recovery advantage
  in recent pooled evidence.
- Pogačar-specific details remain host observation or interpretation, not
  controlled evidence or a claim about every race.
