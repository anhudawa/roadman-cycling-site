# Tour de France: Unchained search-owner decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**Window:** Three months through 24 August 2026

## Decision

- `/blog/why-netflix-unchained-failed-cycling` keeps its established canonical and becomes the broad **tour de france unchained** information owner.
- `/podcast/ep-2034-how-netflix-s-unchained-failed-why-every-pro-hated-it` remains Roadman's opinion and source-record page, with corrected visible facts.
- Tour race guides retain race, route and result intent; the Unchained owner explains the Netflix series.
- No redirect or new broad URL is created because the incumbent already owns virtually all exact-query visibility.

The intervention fixes intent mismatch and trust problems. The old article led with a failure thesis and an incorrect views-versus-watch-hours comparison. The maintained owner now answers season count, episode count, race years, where to watch, final-season status and viewing data before presenting Roadman's critique.

## Exact-query baseline: `tour de france unchained`

| Metric           | Baseline |
| ---------------- | -------: |
| Clicks           |       21 |
| Impressions      |    2,479 |
| CTR              |     0.8% |
| Average position |     10.0 |

Search Console reported an unusually clean page split:

| URL                                           | Clicks | Impressions |  CTR | Position | Assigned role             |
| --------------------------------------------- | -----: | ----------: | ---: | -------: | ------------------------- |
| `/blog/why-netflix-unchained-failed-cycling`  |     21 |       2,479 | 0.8% |     10.0 | Broad series owner        |
| `/blog/andy-mcgrath-pogacar-not-just-a-freak` |      0 |           1 |   0% |      2.0 | Unrelated isolated result |

This is a click-through-rate, intent and factual-trust job rather than a cannibalisation job.

## Trust and answer-layer changes

- Replaced the failure-first title with a broad series title covering seasons, episodes and ending status.
- Added a direct answer, season-to-race table, six FAQs, four reviewed claims and a scoped reviewer line.
- Confirmed through Netflix that the series has three seasons and that season 3 is final.
- Confirmed through the Tour de France that season 3 covers the 2024 race.
- Corrected the old 1.5 million figure from “watch hours” to Netflix's views metric.
- Removed the invalid direct comparison with a different Drive to Survive metric and time window.
- Removed unsupported claims that eight featured teams proved 14 teams withdrew in protest.
- Stopped treating low audience, rider criticism or team access as a proven single cause of the series ending.
- Kept Wout van Aert's documented objection, while separating it from Roadman's wider interpretation.
- Corrected the podcast's visible metadata, takeaways, claims, FAQs and citations without rewriting its archived transcript.
- Added AI discovery, priority recrawl and a monthly citation-monitoring prompt.

## Measurement plan

### Seven-day directional check — earliest 5 September 2026

- Confirm Google has recrawled the established URL and is showing the new series-first title and description.
- Compare CTR for `tour de france unchained`, season, episode, watch and season-4 modifiers.
- Confirm the opinion podcast remains support rather than becoming a second broad result.

### Twenty-eight-day decision check — earliest 26 September 2026

- Compare clicks, impressions, CTR and average position with this baseline and a matched preceding period.
- Success means the owner retains at least 95% of exact-query impressions, improves CTR from 0.8% and gains qualified season/episode visibility.
- If position holds around 10 but CTR does not improve, test the title against the dominant live SERP formats before changing the URL or canonical.
