## Change and measurement contract

The retired version claimed that the score could make the decision, used fixed five-beat and three-day rules, equated subjective reports with objective measures and attached injury/illness outcomes to an unvalidated score. The replacement keeps the query owner while adding validation limits, a transparent decision sequence, primary citations and distinct handoffs to the daily tool, wider recovery screen and app.
