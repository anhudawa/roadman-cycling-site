- This URL owns how to choose, evaluate and compare an online cycling coach.
- `/coaching` owns Roadman online coaching service, delivery and application
  intent.
- `/blog/how-much-does-online-cycling-coach-cost-2026` owns current market price
  bands and cost comparison.
- `/compare/coach-vs-app` owns the coach-versus-training-app decision.
- `/coaching/masters` owns Roadman's masters-specific coaching offer.

## Problems in the previous version

The title promised a nine-point checklist while the body supplied five. It
prescribed weekly communication as a universal minimum, claimed online coaching
was equally effective for most amateur cyclists, published one unsupported
monthly price range, treated inability to provide client references as a
negative despite confidentiality constraints, blurred coaching with nutrition
and health scope, and prescribed a universal three-month trial. It did not check
safeguarding, insurance, first aid, privacy, platform access, data export,
contract terms, cancellation or coach absence.

The old examples also presented named performance gains without the starting
context, typical-result information or advertising caveat needed for a reliable
buyer decision.

## Decision

1. Deliver the promised nine-point buyer checklist in a scannable comparison
   table and repeatable question set.
2. Separate verifiable qualifications, insurance and safeguarding from claims
   that a credential guarantees quality.
3. Define professional scope and referral boundaries for injury, clinical
   nutrition, health and mental-health concerns.
4. Replace universal contact rules with written service-level questions about
   review, adaptation, communication and coach absence.
5. Add a health-data checklist covering platforms, people, automated tools,
   purpose, retention, export and deletion.
6. Treat case studies as examples rather than forecasts, require context and
   reject outcome guarantees.
7. Move price-band intent to the dedicated cost guide while retaining the
   contract, cancellation, pause and exit checks needed to choose a coach.
8. Disclose Roadman's commercial conflict and explicitly tell readers to apply
   the same standard to Roadman.

## Measurement

At the 7-day and 28-day checkpoints, compare clicks, impressions, CTR and
average position for the URL. Monitor the `cycling coach`, `online cycling
coach`, `best online cycling coach`, `cycling coach online` and `online cycling
coaching` groups; assisted clicks to `/coaching`, `/apply`, `/find-your-fit`, the
cost guide and coach-versus-app comparison; and whether cost and service queries
remain with their declared owners.
