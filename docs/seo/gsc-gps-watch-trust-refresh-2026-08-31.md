# GPS watch running-and-cycling guide refresh — 31 August 2026

## Opportunity

In the rolling Search Console comparison through 29 August 2026, `/blog/gps-watches-cycling-running-guide` grew from **19 clicks / 1,626 impressions** to **34 clicks / 7,376 impressions**. This is a rapidly expanding discovery surface. The rolling periods overlap recent site work, so these figures select the opportunity; they are not a causal ranking claim.

## Audit findings

- The article treated Garmin, Wahoo and COROS as equivalent native watch/head-unit ecosystems even though Wahoo has no matching watch.
- It compared battery and price claims across different modes without showing their basis.
- It claimed months of hands-on use without a published test protocol or evidence trail.
- It prescribed a 170–180 steps-per-minute running cadence as though it were universal.
- It described TrainingPeaks connections as automatic without explaining the Apple Health/Workout App setup.
- It had no visible review date or source list despite making purchase recommendations.

## Release decision

- Preserve the URL and its running-plus-cycling buying intent.
- Reframe the verdict by actual ecosystem: Garmin Edge, COROS DURA, Wahoo/no native watch, and Apple/no native head unit.
- Tie device, battery and sync claims to official Garmin, COROS, Apple and TrainingPeaks documentation.
- Add a compact comparison table while warning that manufacturer battery modes are not a like-for-like test.
- Replace the universal cadence target with the evidence-supported boundary: cadence changes affect biomechanics, while long-term injury and performance outcomes remain uncertain.
- Remove the unverified hands-on claim and label the recommendations as editorial use-case judgements.

## Measurement

Track the exact URL and its qualified running-and-cycling watch queries. Protect the established owner; do not create a second generic GPS-watch article. Recheck after a complete seven-day window, then at 28 days.
