## Trust problem

The old article attributed a back-squat, Romanian-deadlift and Bulgarian-split-
squat “big three” to Derek Teel. Those exercise names and that ranking do not
appear in the cited Roadman episode transcript. The page also converted visual
similarity into guaranteed single-leg transfer, invented a 50% unilateral-
volume rule, promised equivalent stimulus with lower injury risk, and presented
fixed weekly and seasonal prescriptions as research facts.

## Release decision

Preserve the established URL and exact-query ownership while rebuilding the
page around three clearly labelled layers:

1. what Derek Teel actually said in the source episode;
2. what the current cyclist-only and resistance-training reviews can support;
3. Roadman's adaptable exercise and scheduling framework.

The page now links to the canonical gym-routine owner for the full A/B template,
the broad strength owner for the research question and the original podcast
transcript for source verification.

## Product handoff

The page turns exercise-selection demand into a product-relevant job: select a
30-, 45- or 60-minute strength menu, protect priority rides and make explainable
readiness adjustments. It routes to the same Beehiiv `app-waitlist` audience at
`/app?source=derek-teel-exercises`; this adds attribution, not another list.
