## Problems corrected

The former cluster stated or implied that:

- Zone 2 is universally defined by one fixed FTP or maximum-heart-rate percentage;
- power, heart rate or RPE can act as an infallible referee;
- any one metric crossing a band automatically changes the physiological domain;
- nasal breathing identifies a precise first threshold;
- Zone 2 uniquely maximises mitochondrial growth and fat oxidation;
- aerobic adaptation begins only after 60 or 90 minutes;
- every masters cyclist needs a fixed weekly long ride or age-based dose;
- heart-rate drift has a universal normal range and should always be ignored or always stop the ride; and
- an exact 80/20 weekly distribution applies to every cyclist.

The replacement names the zone model, distinguishes a conventional cycling power band from research intensity domains, treats LT1 and VT1 as preferred individual anchors, separates external dose from internal response, reports method variability and gives practical examples without universal thresholds or duration guarantees.
