# Overtraining recovery owner refresh — 2026-08-31

`/blog/cycling-recovering-from-overtraining-guide` recorded:

- **27 clicks**;
- **1,453 web impressions**;
- **1.9% CTR**;
- **9.5 average position**; and
- **694 Google AI-feature impressions**.

Visible queries included:

- `cycling overtraining` — 24 impressions;
- `OTS recovery` — 9 impressions;
- `overtraining syndrome recovery` — 8 impressions;
- `overtraining symptoms cycling` — 5 impressions; and
- `how to recover from overtraining syndrome` — 1 click from 2 impressions.

The owner is at the edge of page one and already appears in AI search, but the
existing answer contained unsupported treatment, testing and return-to-cycling
prescriptions that undermine trust.

## Ownership decision

Keep this URL as the recovery and return owner for a rider with persistent
underperformance being clinically assessed or managed. Keep
`/blog/overtraining-vs-overreaching-cyclists` as the category-comparison owner,
and `/blog/cycling-fatigue-signs-when-to-back-off` as the symptom and immediate
training-decision owner.

## Trust and answer upgrade

The refreshed owner:

- removes the unsupported 8–16-week and six-month recovery promises;
- removes a mandatory two-to-four-week complete-rest phase;
- removes fixed walking, spinning, FTP, TSS and intensity calendars;
- removes cortisol-to-testosterone, ferritin, CRP and HRV clearance rules;
- makes clinical assessment and differential diagnosis the first step;
- provides a criterion-based continuum from daily function to participation,
  cycling, normal training and performance;
- separates nutrition, sleep and psychological support from medical treatment
  claims;
- cites consensus and systematic-review evidence; and
- routes interested riders into the single Beehiiv app-waitlist audience with
  `source=overtraining-recovery-guide`.
