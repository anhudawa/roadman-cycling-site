## Release decisions

1. Preserve the existing search title, description, self-canonical, event dates,
   prices, sold-out state and available-camp state.
2. Link the evidence-reviewed camp nutrition guide beside the first-camp,
   preparation and load/recovery guides.
3. Connect the CollectionPage structured data to both canonical Event nodes with
   `hasPart` relationships.
4. Replace the anonymous team review label with Anthony Walsh, Sarah Ann Egan
   and Matthew Devins plus their specific camp responsibilities.
5. Add a high-priority AI benchmark for Girona cycling training camps in 2026.
