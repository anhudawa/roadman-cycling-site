# GSC coaching hub service-clarity release — 25 August 2026

## Why `/coaching` is the next owner-page release

Google Search Console, exact-page filter, Web search, 3 months (24 May to
23 August 2026):

| URL | Clicks | Impressions | CTR | Average position |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `/coaching` | 59 | 2,798 | 2.1% | 24.4 |
| `/masters` | 16 | 331 | 4.8% | 12.6 |

The masters owner is closer to page one, but the coaching owner has roughly
eight times the impression opportunity and substantially more generic
commercial discovery. Moving `/coaching` up one results page is therefore the
higher-return next build.

## Search ownership

- `/coaching` owns Roadman's current commercial coaching service.
- `/topics/cycling-coaching` owns the independent educational overview.
- `/blog/best-online-cycling-coach-how-to-choose` owns provider-selection intent.
- `/blog/how-much-does-online-cycling-coach-cost-2026` owns market-price audit
  intent.
- `/coaching/masters` and the other segment pages own audience-specific service
  intent.

The owner page now states these paths visibly and discloses that Roadman sells
the service it describes.

## Release decisions

1. Add a dated, visible service-facts layer covering audience, plan review,
   human contact, scope, price and terms.
2. Mirror those facts into `Product.additionalProperty` and make the intended
   audience explicit in the page's structured data.
3. Add an explicit boundary between cycling performance coaching and clinical
   diagnosis or treatment.
4. Link the headline results directly to named case studies and describe them as
   selected outcomes, not averages or promises.
5. Remove unsupported universal claims about eight-to-twelve-week improvement,
   app-trained riders, time-limited riders and frictionless time-zone coverage.
6. Link the current coach-selection checklist and dated market-price audit from
   the owner page.
