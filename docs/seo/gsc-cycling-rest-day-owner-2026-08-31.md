# Cycling rest-day search owner — GSC decision (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cycling-rest-day-what-to-do-guide` as the broad owner for cycling
rest days: how many, what to do, full rest versus active recovery, nutrition,
strength training, masters riders and unplanned days off.

The direct answers on how many rest days and active versus passive recovery
remain supporting surfaces. The recovery-week article retains the distinct
multi-day deload intent. No competing editorial URL needs deletion or redirect.

## Search Console baseline

Latest three-month Search Console view on 31 August 2026:

| URL | Clicks | Web impressions | CTR | Average position | Google AI-feature impressions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `/blog/cycling-rest-day-what-to-do-guide` | 21 | 3,834 | 0.5% | 6.6 | 400 |

Visible queries included:

- `cycling rest days` — 21 web impressions;
- `cycling rest day` — 10 web impressions;
- `cycling on rest days` — 9 web impressions; and
- several ambiguous professional-cycling queries about a compulsory 20-day rest
  period.

The URL has a page-one average position but a low CTR. The phase therefore
strengthens the established owner and broad cyclist answer rather than creating
a new page. Measurement will separate the core training intent from compulsory-
rest news or regulation queries.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the article around a direct 60-second decision and a clear boundary
  between complete rest, active recovery, easy training and a recovery week.
- Removed fixed heart-rate and HRV stoplights, guaranteed active-recovery
  benefits, universal masters schedules, exact detraining claims and a generic
  rest-day calorie prescription.
- Checked claims against the 2024 endurance-recovery umbrella review, active-
  recovery systematic review, athlete-monitoring systematic review, athlete
  sleep consensus, joint sports-nutrition position statement, detraining meta-
  analysis and masters-athlete recovery review.
- Aligned the recovery and masters hubs plus both closely related direct answers
  with the owner.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=rest-day-guide` inside the existing
  single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added bounded LLM discovery, curated IndexNow discovery and AI benchmark
  prompt 363.

## Measurement

Compare rolling 28-day and three-month performance after recrawl:

- clicks, impressions, CTR and average position for the owner;
- non-brand queries containing `cycling`, `cyclist`, `rest day`, `rest days`,
  `active recovery`, `complete rest`, `masters` and `how many`;
- the share of impressions coming from the separate compulsory-rest news intent;
- Google AI-feature impressions and monthly citation results for prompt 363;
- app-waitlist conversions attributed to `rest-day-guide`; and
- emergence of another page for the same broad intent.

IndexNow supports participating engines. Google discovery continues through the
canonical URL, internal links, sitemap and normal Search Console workflows.
