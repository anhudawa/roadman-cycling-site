## Problems found

- The article repeatedly said Morton quit the WorldTour, while the verified
  timeline shows he left Garmin after 2014, returned with Dimension Data and EF,
  and remains an EF Pro Cycling athlete on the 2026 Far Beyond programme.
- The page lacked a current biography, road-team timeline, quick facts, visible
  source list, structured citations and explicit official-record/interview
  boundary.
- Major verified achievements—the Alt Tour distance and transfers, 2024
  Unbound win, Tour Divide elapsed time and supported Around Australia
  record—were absent.
- Unsupported claims about audience response and amateur riders' fitness were
  presented as facts.
- The episode title competed with the broad entity owner and presented the
  move away from road racing as one permanent retirement.
- Guest, entity and canonical Person records described Morton as a generic
  WorldTour rider and omitted current Far Beyond status, UCI identity and
  Wikidata disambiguation.

## Changes prepared

- Broad owner title: `Lachlan Morton: Alt Tour, Unbound & EF Far Beyond`.
- Current 2026 identity, corrected road-team timeline and explicit distinction

- Five visible claim/evidence/implication rows, five FAQs and seven linked
