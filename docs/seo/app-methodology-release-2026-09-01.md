# App methodology release — 1 September 2026

## Shipped

- A public decision-policy page at `/app/methodology` with a stable prelaunch
  policy version.
- Four inspectable stages: map the cycling week, apply a same-day guardrail,
  record the actual strength work and set the next exposure.
- Six invariant rules covering the training ceiling, symptoms and pain,
  external-plan protection, non-diagnosis, versioned reasons and uncertainty.
- Input-by-input boundaries for sleep, subjective wellbeing, soreness, resting
  signals and recent training load.
- Primary-source evidence with an explicit statement that research informing a
  rule does not validate this exact app.
- Discovery links from `/app`, the sitemap, product feed, MCP and both LLM
  reference files.

## Product claim boundary

The policy describes intended prelaunch behaviour, not a clinical validation or
performance outcome. A favourable morning cannot add unplanned training; a
score cannot diagnose recovery, illness, injury or overtraining; thresholds
remain under testing until Roadman publishes evidence for them.
