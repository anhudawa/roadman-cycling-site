**Prepared:** 31 August 2026

**Public owner:** `/research/cycling-podcast-archive-study`

**Status:** outreach copy is prepared; no external message should be sent
without Roadman owner approval.

## Why this asset exists

Roadman's public archive already makes hundreds of conversations searchable.
This release turns the archive itself into a citable first-party media asset:
one frozen version, a visible method, explicit limitations, a downloadable
table and a correction route. It owns narrow Roadman archive-data and cycling
podcast-dataset intent. `/podcast` remains the broad show and episode-archive
owner.

This is not a listening or market-demand study. Every pitch must describe it as
a case study of Roadman's searchable on-site records, not the global cycling
podcast market.

## Release facts editors can verify

- 818 searchable episode records dated from 28 June 2016 to 24 July 2026.
- 161 unique named-guest display values after exact whitespace normalisation.
- Community and coaching comprise 670 records, or 81.9% of the snapshot.
- 153 of the 340 records dated 2024–2026 are interviews (45.0%), compared with
  263 of 818 (32.2%) across the full snapshot.
- 68 of all 95 nutrition and strength records are dated 2024–2026 (71.6%).
- 707 records include an inline transcript field (86.4%); 380 have a matching
  dedicated transcript file (46.5%).
- The CSV is licensed CC BY 4.0 and the public page supplies the required
  attribution and suggested citation.

## Send sequence

1. Publish and verify the report, CSV, schema, sitemap, `llms.txt` and public
   archive/press links.
2. Send only the three Priority 1 notes on day one, each rewritten for the
   named outlet and sent as a plain-text personal email.
3. Wait two working days. Follow up once with one additional useful fact, not
   “just checking in.”
4. Send Priority 2 only after recording any questions from the first wave and
   improving the press notes accordingly.
5. Approach Priority 3 only through a real relationship or a clearly published
   current route.
6. Offer the raw CSV and methodology freely. Do not make coverage, backlink or
   dofollow-link demands.

  sports publisher.”

  cannot prove.”

  45.0% recent interview share is a starting point, not a required headline.”

## Newsletter and owned-media brief

- **Subject:** We opened the Roadman podcast archive as a dataset
- **Lead:** “A searchable archive is more useful when readers can inspect how it
  was built. Today we are publishing the table behind 818 on-site episode
  records.”
- **Body:** lead with the interview-format shift; show one topic chart; explain
  the market-demand limitation; link to the report first and the CSV second.
- **Podcast mention:** 30–45 seconds in the next relevant episode. Invite
  researchers and journalists to challenge the method or request another
  aggregate rather than asking listeners to “share everywhere.”
- **Social:** one four-panel carousel: population → recent interview shift →
  transcript coverage → limitation/download. The limitation must be on the
  image, not hidden in the caption.
