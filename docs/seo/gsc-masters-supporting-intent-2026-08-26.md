# Masters supporting-page intent release — GSC evidence and decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026
**GSC property:** `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`
**Search type:** Web
**Baseline window:** 25 May–24 August 2026

## Decision

Preserve all four established URLs. Google Search Console shows independent
search history on each page, so a redirect would discard useful demand without
evidence that one URL is redundant. Separate the jobs in visible copy,
structured metadata, owner routing and AI-discovery files instead.

The canonical broad owner remains `/masters`. The topic page
`/topics/masters-cycling` is a routed guide library rather than another broad
answer page.

Do not redirect any of the four supporting URLs from this evidence set.

## Problems found

The weekly and 12-week plans had already received an evidence-boundary review,
but the other supporting surfaces still stated that:

- trained riders lose one predictable percentage of VO2max per decade;
- 80/20 is the proven masters distribution;
- one or two hard sessions and 48–72 hours of spacing are universal;
- two heavy strength sessions are mandatory and proven for masters cyclists;
- one high-protein target applies to active masters riders;
- recovery is always the cause of a post-40 plateau;
- the cycling-strength meta-analysis established a masters subgroup effect.

Those claims contradicted the reviewed canonical owner and made it difficult
for search engines or AI systems to identify a consistent Roadman position.

## Release boundary

1. `/masters` owns broad masters-cycling evidence and limitations.
2. The complete guide owns the whole-rider orientation and routes narrower jobs.
3. The plateau guide verifies the signal, audits absorption and changes one
   likely constraint over four weeks; it no longer publishes another weekly
   template.
4. The weekly schedule remains the day-by-day owner.
5. The 12-week plan remains the block owner for gran fondo, racing and comeback
   examples.
6. The topic page is a navigation library with explicit clinical boundaries.
7. Every page preserves its URL and publication date while receiving a
   26 August 2026 review date where frontmatter supports it.

## Primary-source boundaries

- Rogers et al. observed 5.5% versus 12% VO2max decline per decade in 15 male
  masters athletes and 14 sedentary men; it is not an individual forecast:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2361923/>
- A 2022 longitudinal review reported masters-athlete estimates from 5% to 46%
  per decade and linked much of the variation to training changes:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36078762/>
- The recovery-and-ageing review does not establish one masters-cyclist clock:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18268815/>
- The 2025 intensity-distribution network meta-analysis found no overall
  polarised-versus-pyramidal winner for its stated outcomes and is not a
  masters-only dataset: <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39888556/>
- The 2025 cycling strength meta-analysis reported some benefits, no VO2max
  effect and low-certainty evidence without an established masters subgroup
  programme: <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40632222/>
- The 2025 masters-protein scoping review found 12 heterogeneous studies and
  uncertain population-specific guidance:
  <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39940356/>
