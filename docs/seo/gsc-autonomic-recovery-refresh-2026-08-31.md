# Autonomic-recovery search owner — GSC refresh (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cycling-autonomic-nervous-system-recovery-guide` as the long-form
owner for autonomic recovery, parasympathetic recovery, sympathetic versus
parasympathetic exercise, post-exercise autonomic reactivation and practical
breathing/cold-water questions for cyclists.

The existing `/blog/cycling-hrv-training-guide` remains the owner for HRV
measurement, device choice, baselines and HRV-guided training decisions. The
two pages link to each other without repeating the same answer.

## What changed

- Replaced the accelerator/brake and master-switch story with bounded cardiac
  autonomic physiology.
- Separated parasympathetic reactivation from muscle repair, glycogen,
  adaptation and whole-body readiness.
- Corrected HRV from a direct balance or overload diagnosis to a contextual,
  method-dependent cardiac measure.
- Replaced universal 7-day/60-day thresholds and higher-is-better claims with a
  multi-input decision framework.
- Corrected the physiological-sigh claim: the cited randomised study used five
  minutes daily for one month and did not test a 60-second cortisol reset or
  cycling recovery.
- Removed breath-hold cold-face immersion as a routine recommendation and added
  the cold-water autonomic-conflict/arrhythmia boundary.
- Removed invented after-40 switching delays and added older-adult exercise
  evidence.
- Added medical red flags, app attribution, priority recrawl, LLM discovery and
  AI benchmark prompt 369.
