## Andrew Feather media package

### Clip brief

- **Hook:** “Did Andrew Feather actually beat Pogačar?”
- **Source window:** 03:03–04:05.
- **Edit:** lead with the roughly six-minute head start, then the approximately
  three-and-a-half-minute elapsed-time gap. End on the distinction: Feather
  crossed first; Pogačar climbed faster.
- **Destination:** the episode owner, not a new article.

### Newsletter brief

- **Subject idea:** The amateur who crossed before Pogačar—what really happened
- **Promise:** a transparent explanation of the pursuit format, power claim and
  training context in under four minutes of reading.
- **Primary links:** episode owner, guest profile and Roadman podcast archive.

### Social brief

- Carousel: pursuit format → head start → elapsed-time result → self-reported
  397W → “case study, not prescription”.
- Short caption should use “crossed before” rather than an unqualified “beat”.
- Do not turn the self-reported power file into an independently verified fact.

### Follow-on article brief

- **Working title:** How to Pace a Long Hill Climb When You Expect to Be Caught
- **Intent:** narrow pacing education, not another Andrew Feather biography.
- **Evidence:** use 04:54–06:26 as first-person context, then source the general
  pacing guidance independently. Link back to the episode as the interview
  record and to the coaching/training-plan owners for next steps.

## Daryl Fitzgerald media package

### Clip brief

- **Hook:** The first fit variable a high-volume bike fitter checks
- **Source window:** 01:48–03:34.
- **Edit:** Fitzgerald's high-saddle observation followed by the flexibility,
  pelvic movement and “individual rider” boundary.
- **Destination:** the episode for the statement; the companion saddle-height
  review for the researched answer.

### Newsletter brief

- **Subject idea:** Before buying a new saddle, test this one variable
- **Promise:** a reversible bike-fit process: record the starting point, change
  one variable in 1–2mm steps and compare rather than copying a universal rule.
- **Primary links:** episode, companion evidence review, bike-fit hub.

### Social brief

- Carousel: common observation → why it may matter → one-client 7mm story → why
  that story does not prove causation → the safe 1–2mm testing principle.
- Never publish “most riders are 5–7mm too high” or promise a watt/time gain.

### Follow-on article brief

- **Working title:** Bike-Fit Changes: What to Test First and What to Leave Alone
- **Intent:** a decision tree for saddle height, reach, cleats and pain red flags.
- **Evidence:** distinguish practitioner heuristics from peer-reviewed evidence;
  route pain or neurological symptoms to a qualified clinician.
- **Commercial boundary:** education first; coaching and app links only where
  they solve the reader's next training problem.
