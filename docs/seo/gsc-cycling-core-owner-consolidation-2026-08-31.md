## Duplicate complete guide

`/blog/cycling-core-training-complete-guide` recorded only one click from 244
impressions, 0.4% CTR and average position 9.8. Its visible queries duplicated
the broad core-training intent, and its content repeated the same exercise
library while making stronger unsupported transfer and injury claims.

Release decision:

1. keep `/blog/cycling-core-workout-routine` as the broad exercise and routine
   owner;
2. keep `/blog/core-strength-cyclists-beyond-planks` as the plank and progression
   owner; and
3. permanently redirect the duplicate complete guide to the broad owner, remove
   it from the topic map and repoint active links and AI measurement.

## Trust rebuild

The previous pages presented anti-rotation as a proven watt-transfer mechanism,
ranked a single best exercise, prescribed fixed weekly doses, promised better
late-ride form and injury prevention, and treated arbitrary plank times as
validated thresholds.

The current evidence supports narrower claims:

- an acute fatigue study in 15 competitive cyclists changed lower-limb motion
  but not pedal force or work and did not measure future injury;
- a small 12-week study of 36 trained road cyclists favoured conventional
  resistance training over the tested core programme for the measured power
  durations;
- a randomized athlete meta-analysis found clearer improvements in core
  endurance and balance than in sport-specific performance; and
- a review of eight cyclist biomechanical studies described lower-back-pain
  associations but did not validate one exercise routine as prevention or
  treatment.

The broad page now provides a direct 10–15-minute routine with regressions,
progression boundaries, bike-week placement and outcome tracking. The plank page
answers the high-impression question directly and removes unsupported 60-, 90-
and 120-second rules.

## Product handoff

- `/app?source=core-workout`; and

The values preserve origin attribution only. Every signup continues to use the
shared `app-waitlist` tag and campaign in the existing publication.
