# Recovery-between-hard-sessions owner — GSC refresh (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/recovery-between-hard-sessions-cycling` as the long-form owner for
cycling recovery time, recovery time after cycling, recovery between interval
sessions, and the decision between roughly 24, 48 or 72 hours.

The active-recovery page owns recovery-ride intensity and modality detail. The
HRV and resting-heart-rate pages own measurement. The fatigue guide owns
symptom triage. This page combines those inputs only to answer when the next
hard cycling session should happen.

## What changed

- Reframed the title and first answer around the 24/48/72-hour decision.
- Replaced the universal 48-hour and “72 hours after 40” rules with a practical
  starting point and individual decision framework.
- Separated the recovery timelines for performance, glycogen, soreness,
  muscle damage, subjective wellbeing and cardiac autonomic measures.
- Added the direct cycling muscle-damage meta-analysis, endurance-recovery
  umbrella review, masters recovery review, athlete monitoring review, sleep
  consensus, nutrition position and current interval-training synthesis.
- Removed universal +5 bpm, HRV, soreness, first-interval and 55% FTP proof
  thresholds.
- Reframed two hard days per week as a useful default rather than a maximum.
- Removed the claim that fitness loss begins at 7–10 days and added the taper
  and detraining evidence boundary.
- Added medical red flags, app attribution, priority recrawl, LLM discovery and
  AI benchmark prompt 371.
