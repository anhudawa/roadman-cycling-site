# Andrew Feather search-owner decision — 26 August 2026

## Decision

Keep and strengthen the established owner:

`/podcast/ep-24-i-asked-a-40-year-old-amateur-how-he-beat-pogacar`

Retain the URL because it already owns most exact-query visibility. Lead the
search title with Andrew Feather, make the 2025 pursuit handicap explicit and
separate verified race records from interview claims. Keep the guest page as
the canonical Person support page and the expert page as topic navigation, with
different titles for biography and advice intent. Do not create another broad
Andrew Feather page or redirect a useful supporting surface.

Page impressions are not additive because more than one Roadman URL can appear
in the same result set. The episode appeared in 73.8% of exact-query
impressions and earned 61.8% of exact-query clicks, making it the clear current
owner. The expert page is a productive supporting result, but its title needs
to describe advice intent rather than compete as another generic name page.

## Problems found

- Episode and profile copy dated the Pogi Challenge to 2024; the event and
  contemporary report establish 2025.
- Multiple pages described a 2023 same-conditions Strava result. The actual
  story was a challenger-first pursuit event, and Pogačar recorded the faster
  elapsed climb time.
- Interview-reported power, training and equipment figures were labelled as
  study evidence or presented as independently established facts.
- The episode did not lead with Andrew Feather in its search title, despite the
  exact name query being the demonstrated opportunity.
- The guest profile lacked verified results, visible sources, FAQs, a review
  disclosure and a current 2025 championship result.
- The expert and guest pages used overlapping broad-name metadata instead of
  distinct biography and topic-advice intent.

## Changes prepared

- Episode title: `Andrew Feather: Pogi Challenge Power & Hill Climbs`.
- Transparent answer capsule explaining that Feather crossed first after a
  head start while Pogačar was about three and a half minutes faster on elapsed
  time.
- Seven reviewed citations, five FAQs, a verified-record section and an
  explicit boundary around self-reported 397W, body mass and training figures.
- Guest biography with six reviewed sources, five FAQs, current credential,
  editorial review disclosure and 26 August review date.
- Expert metadata differentiated around hill-climb training and pacing.
- False Strava/2023 claims removed from the climbing guide, climbing-time guide,
  answer pages and internal episode-topic map.
- `llms.txt`, IndexNow priorities and AI benchmark prompt 234 updated.
