## Why the page needed a trust rebuild

The previous article turned a useful beginner query into a rigid sequence. It
presented mandatory mobility and power phases, fixed weekly placement,
compulsory jumps, specific exercise-transfer claims, injury-prevention promises
and deterministic concurrent-training rules without evidence that one complete
12-week cyclist package had been tested.

The release preserves the established URL while separating:

1. low-certainty cyclist performance evidence for heavy strength training;
2. broader resistance-training and concurrent-training evidence; and
3. Roadman's conservative beginner implementation framework.

The revised plan learns and tolerates in weeks 1–2, builds repeatability in
weeks 3–6 and progresses selected movements in weeks 7–12. No named lift,
plyometric phase, one-repetition-maximum test or universal weekly arrangement is
required.
