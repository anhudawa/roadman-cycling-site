## Trust corrections

The previous guide presented team observations as universal facts and attached
precise outcomes to weak or uncited claims. The release removes the universal
20-minute window, “50% faster” glycogen claim, lactate-flushing performance
promise, fixed Tour sleep protocol, common melatonin claim, deterministic HRV
commands and the claim that the protocol scales perfectly.

The replacement grades each intervention against primary research:

- Nutrient timing: aggressive carbohydrate restoration is the special case
  when recovery is under four hours; total daily intake and turnaround govern
  the practical decision.
- Active cool-down: faster blood-lactate clearance without a consistent later
  performance or recovery benefit.
- Massage: small soreness and flexibility effects, no pooled direct performance
  benefit.
- Pneumatic compression: trivial-to-small muscular-function effects and
  variable soreness outcomes.
- Sleep: protect individual sleep need, opportunity and continuity rather than
  asserting one universal number.
- Cold-water immersion: possible short-term use with a repeated-use strength-
  adaptation trade-off.
- HRV: one contextual trend among load, sleep, soreness, symptoms and perceived
  effort—not a diagnosis or automatic instruction.
