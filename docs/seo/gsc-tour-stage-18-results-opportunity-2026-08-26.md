# Tour de France 2026 stage 18 result-owner decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**Window:** Three months through 24 August 2026

## Decision

- `/tour-de-france/stage/18` keeps its established canonical and becomes the verified result owner for **stage 18 tour de france 2026**.
- Historical stage-18 podcast pages retain their year-specific recap intent.
- `/podcast/tdf-stage-17-hardest-climb-in-the-world` remains a 2021 episode and is not linked as a 2026 result source.
- No redirect or new result URL is created because the incumbent has essentially all exact-query visibility.

The intervention fixes freshness and intent mismatch. The old page still led with a prediction after the stage had finished. The maintained owner now gives the winner, winning time, podium, gaps, decisive race events, classification context and official sources before Roadman's training interpretation.

## Exact-query baseline: `stage 18 tour de france 2026`

| Metric           | Baseline |
| ---------------- | -------: |
| Clicks           |       19 |
| Impressions      |    2,283 |
| CTR              |     0.8% |

Search Console reported a clean page split:

| URL                                                | Clicks | Impressions |  CTR | Position | Assigned role           |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -----: | ----------: | ---: | -------: | ----------------------- |
| `/tour-de-france/stage/18`                         |     19 |       2,284 | 0.8% |      8.3 | 2026 stage result owner |
| `/podcast/tdf-stage-17-hardest-climb-in-the-world` |      0 |           1 |   0% |     10.0 | Historical podcast      |

Page-level impressions can slightly exceed the query card because Search Console can count multiple site URLs on an impression. This is a freshness and CTR job, not a cannibalisation job.

## Result and trust changes

- Changed the search title to `Tour de France 2026 Stage 18 Results: Carapaz Wins`.
- Added Richard Carapaz's 4:26:21 winning time and the official podium.
- Added Mauro Schmid and Matteo Jorgenson at 45 seconds.
- Replaced the prediction-first page with a direct result, five key moments and classification context.
- Recorded the two final-climb attacks and three-kilometre solo from the official race report.
- Added official classification, race-report, route and post-stage interview sources.
- Removed the speculative named-expert card from the completed result view.
- Added completed-event and FAQ structured data for result intent.
- Updated the result page's sitemap date and priority without pretending the other stage previews have verified result records.
- Corrected the dormant 2026 Tour result feed from stage 13 to the final stage and final general classification.
- Added AI discovery, priority recrawl and a monthly citation-monitoring prompt.

## Measurement plan

### Seven-day directional check — earliest 5 September 2026

- Confirm Google has recrawled the established URL and shows the result-first title and description.
- Compare CTR for stage 18, Carapaz, Orcières-Merlette, result and winner modifiers.
- Confirm historical podcast pages do not become competing 2026 result owners.

### Twenty-eight-day decision check — earliest 26 September 2026

- Compare clicks, impressions, CTR and average position with this baseline and a matched preceding period.
- Success means the stage page retains at least 95% of exact-query impressions and improves CTR from 0.8%.
- If position holds around 8 but CTR does not improve, compare the live title and snippet with official results and publisher competitors before changing the URL.
