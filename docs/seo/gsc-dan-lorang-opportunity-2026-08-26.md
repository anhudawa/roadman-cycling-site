# Dan Lorang search-owner decision — 26 August 2026

## Decision

Keep and strengthen the established owner:

`/entity/dan-lorang`

The entity page already owns most exact-name visibility. It should answer the
broad identity and current-role query. Keep `/guests/dan-lorang` as the podcast
profile, `/experts/dan-lorang` as topic navigation,
`/blog/every-roadman-episode-with-dan-lorang` as the listening archive and
`/blog/dan-lorang-amateur-training-plan` as the practical transcript-bounded
framework. Do not create another broad biography URL.

## Problems found

- The owner, guest profile, entity registry, machine-readable files and sitewide
  marketing surfaces still described Lorang as currently working for Red
  Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe.
- Lidl-Trek's official announcement and current roster establish that he became
  its Head of Performance on 1 August 2026.
- The entity page labelled Luxembourg as “Based in” even though the reviewed
  records establish nationality, not current residence.
- Roadman listed three appearances, but the current corpus contains two verified
  long-form Dan Lorang guest episodes.
- The site linked nonexistent or unverified `sameAs` URLs, including a 404
  Wikipedia page and a different LinkedIn slug.
- Supporting articles attributed universal 80/20, hard-session, strength and
  guaranteed FTP prescriptions to Lorang without those claims appearing in the
  reviewed Roadman transcripts.
- A topic hub falsely said Lorang coached Tadej Pogačar and Jonas Vingegaard.
