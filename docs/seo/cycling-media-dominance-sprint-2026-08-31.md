# Cycling media dominance sprint — 31 August to 6 September 2026

## Mission

Make Roadman Cycling the source search engines, AI answer systems and riders use to understand cycling—not merely another site competing for isolated keywords. The week compounds five assets: canonical search owners, evidence, original utility, podcast authority and an app waiting list with one measurable conversion path.

This sprint is the next execution layer of the existing Phase 2 search-growth goal. The formal goal remains open because its fixed seven-day and 28-day measurement windows are not yet complete; the calendar does not prevent us shipping new, separately measured work in the meantime.

## End-of-week outcomes

- Ship at least five production releases, each with a named search owner, visible evidence, internal links, machine-readable discovery and a recorded before-state.
- Improve at least ten high-impression URLs across the podcast, coaching/training, masters, camps, strength/recovery app and evergreen utility layers.
- Turn at least two podcast episodes into complete media packages: transcript evidence, answer capsules, video/watch linkage, guest entity and supporting question pages.
- Publish one original, linkable cycling data asset or methodology report and prepare its press/distribution pack.
- Keep one app waiting list and add attributable paths from every relevant strength, recovery and masters asset.

- Finish with a ranked next-week ledger based on Search Console movement, not page count.

These are shipping and quality targets, not a promise that Google will move rankings inside seven days.

## Seven-day release rhythm

### Day 1 — measurement, trust and the first CTR recovery

- Freeze the rolling Search Console scan and separate core cycling intent from irrelevant or AI-expanded query noise.
- Rebuild the vitamin D guide around the 1,935-impression “best vitamin D for cyclists” opportunity.
- Replace blanket dosing, testing and cofactor prescriptions with NHS, NIH, EFSA, UKAD and athlete-review evidence.
- Make reviewed sources visible in the standard article trust block.
- Add the guide to the short AI-discovery set, benchmark prompts and IndexNow cluster.

Exit gate: owner test, type check, production build, live URL, structured data, recrawl submission and exact-page baseline recorded.

### Day 2 — technical control and owner integrity

- Run the live five-owner graph audit and a fresh crawl of canonicals, indexability, status, sitemap inclusion and visible review trails.
- Audit orphan depth and incoming anchor quality for the ten highest-opportunity pages.
- Recheck broad query splits for podcast, cycling coach, cycling training plan, masters cycling and training camp families.
- Fix only confirmed ownership defects; protect distinct narrow-intent pages.
- Inspect mobile presentation and Core Web Vitals templates on the main owner and article layouts.

Exit gate: zero critical owner-graph errors, zero accidental noindex/canonical defects, and every priority page within two useful internal-link hops.

### Day 3 — revenue cluster expansion and fixed seven-day checkpoint

- Capture the non-overlapping 25–31 August owner scorecard when Search Console reports through 31 August; do not substitute partial data.
- Upgrade the largest confirmed weakness across `/coaching`, `/training-plans`, `/masters` or `/training-camps` with the owner intact.
- Strengthen comparison, objection and proof paths into coaching and the app without turning educational pages into adverts.
- Add or improve answer pages only for genuinely narrower questions that support a canonical owner.

Exit gate: fixed checkpoint committed, release decision documented and all claims/prices consistent across page, schema and offers.

### Day 4 — podcast becomes a searchable media network

- Select two episodes using demand, guest authority and commercial adjacency.
- For each: verify transcript, publish a concise answer capsule, add claim/source timestamps, connect guest/entity/watch pages and route the broad podcast intent back to `/podcast`.
- Create reusable editorial briefs for clips, newsletter, social and follow-on articles so one conversation produces a connected search surface.
- Inspect video indexing rather than assuming embedded video is eligible.

Exit gate: two complete episode knowledge graphs, two crawlable watch assets where source video exists, and no duplicate episode intent.

### Day 5 — own strength, recovery and the future app category

- Map app intent into three layers: education, evaluation and waitlist conversion.
- Upgrade the highest-impression strength/recovery pages with a single useful `/app` handoff and source attribution.
- Build one narrow free tool or diagnostic that solves the pre-app problem and can earn links independently.
- Keep the app name generic until the external brand is chosen; do not expose the internal “Pocket Coach” name.

Exit gate: one waiting list, attributable CTA sources, a clear canonical app owner and no duplicate email audience.

### Day 6 — original data and media distribution

- Turn first-party coaching, podcast or tool data into a transparent report with methodology, limitations, downloadable table and journalist-ready findings.
- Add author/reviewer identity, Dataset schema where eligible, a correction route and reusable charts.
- Prepare targeted outreach to cycling media, coaches, clubs and relevant guests; no mass low-quality link outreach.
- Link the report into podcast, masters, coaching and app pages only where it supplies evidence.

Exit gate: one citable asset and one tightly targeted distribution list/brief ready for owner approval before outreach.

### Day 7 — discovery, quality and next compounding loop

- Verify every release live: 200 status, canonical, metadata, structured data, internal links, feeds, sitemap and `llms.txt` inclusion.
- Submit updated clusters to IndexNow and request Google recrawl for the few highest-value URLs.
- Recheck exact owner pages and the released opportunity URLs; distinguish indexing failure from normal ranking latency.
- Rank next week's ledger by impressions, intent value, CTR gap, owner confidence, evidence risk and implementation effort.

Exit gate: zero known production regressions, evidence captured for each release, and the next sprint begins from ranked opportunities.

## The release contract

Every item must pass the same gates before it counts as shipped:

1. One explicit canonical owner and a written intent boundary.
2. A live Search Console before-state using the narrowest honest filter.
3. A direct answer that fulfils the query without clickbait.
4. Named author/reviewer, review date, visible primary or authoritative sources and evidence limits.
5. Useful links to its hub, related evidence, a tool or episode where relevant, and one next step.
6. Correct metadata and structured data; no schema claim that is not visible on the page.
7. Regression test, type check and production build.
8. Live verification, recrawl submission and dated follow-up checkpoints.

## Guardrails

- Do not merge pages because they share words; merge only when they serve the same job and Google splits the same intent.
- Do not rewrite pages changed on 25–26 August based on four days of volatility. Verify indexing and use the 3 September checkpoint.
- Do not optimise for irrelevant query expansion, pornography-adjacent guest searches or nonsensical AI comparison queries.
- Do not present a medical safety ceiling as a dose, an observational association as causation or one athlete's outcome as a universal result.
- Do not create a second app waitlist, second app name or competing commercial owner.
- Do not let rapid publishing outrun corrections, evidence or measurement.

## Measurement dates

- Daily: releases, live QA, owner integrity and exact-page opportunity checks.
- 3 September 2026 or later: fixed seven-day post-release owner checkpoint once data runs through 31 August.
- 7 September 2026: first directional read on the 31 August vitamin D release, subject to complete Search Console data.
- 24 September 2026 or later: fixed 28-day owner comparison once data runs through 21 September.
- 28 September 2026: decision-grade 28-day read on the vitamin D release.

The sprint continues shipping while those clocks mature. Waiting for a valid outcome window is a measurement constraint, not a work constraint.
