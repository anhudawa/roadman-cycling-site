## Reading experience and evidence debt

Mobile QA of the country guides and Dragon Ride article found multiple panels before the article body: hero, short answer, takeaways, main-resource links and audience guidance. On inspected routes the actual reporting began roughly 3,400–3,700 CSS pixels into the document. Review these panels as a single reading sequence. Retain what answers a distinct question and consolidate repetition; preserve useful internal links and conversion tracking.

A newsletter overlay also obscured an article during browser review. Investigate its scroll/exit triggers on normal mobile and desktop visits. Test a compact invitation or an inline form against subscription and coaching outcomes. This observation is not proof of a ranking penalty. Google recommends making content accessible and using small banners instead of full-page promotional interruptions: [interstitial guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/avoid-intrusive-interstitials).

The unchanged Wicklow event and plan copy contains precise minimum W/kg, cardiac-drift, pacing and finishing assertions without directly supporting evidence beside them. PR340 removes fabricated calendar-day schema from these templates; that does not validate their old prose. A subsequent source review must correct or remove those assertions, with the course edition identified. This is higher priority than producing another generic event guide. Other observed archive debt includes generic comparison verdicts and inaccurate-looking podcast summaries; check the original material before making corrections.

## AEO and the whole Roadman business

Treat the podcast, articles, tools, coaching and community as complementary resources. Use each page to answer its actual question, then provide the relevant next step. Google says existing search fundamentals apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode, with no special AI text file or schema required: [AI-feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features). Prioritise verifiable original material, crawlable links, accurate visible information and useful media.

Measure qualified coaching applications, Not Done Yet membership, newsletter subscriptions, camp enquiries and eventual Good Legs adoption separately. Good Legs covers strength and recovery within the five-pillar programme; user-confirmed Not Done Yet access begins when the app launches. Billing implementation remains deferred. Neither citations nor keyword positions establish revenue.
