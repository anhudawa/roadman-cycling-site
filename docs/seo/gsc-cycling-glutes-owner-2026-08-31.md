## Owner decision

Keep `/blog/glute-activation-cyclists-power-leaks` as the broad cycling-glutes owner. It already holds almost the entire query history. Permanently redirect the overlapping `/blog/cycling-glute-activation-power-guide` and replace every active internal reference to the retired URL.

The owner was rebuilt to:

- answer how cyclists use their glutes before introducing exercises;
- give five practical strength exercises and an optional three-minute primer;
- separate immediate readiness from progressive strength adaptation;
- correct switched-off-muscle, bridge-diagnosis, guaranteed-watt and saddle-position claims;
- cite cycling EMG, warm-up, exercise-selection, hypertrophy and cyclist resistance-training research;
- route readers into the single app audience through `source=glute-guide`;
- add AI benchmark prompt 356 and priority crawler discovery coverage.
