# Massage-gun search owner — GSC decision (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/massage-guns-cyclists-worth-it` as the single broad owner for
massage-gun benefits, buying and safe-use intent for cyclists. The broader
recovery-tools answer, foam-rolling owner, sports-massage article and recovery
hub remain supporting surfaces and now link to the reviewed owner.

No second editorial page owns the same commercial-investigation intent.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the owner around the real mixed intent: whether a gun works, whether
  it is worth buying, before versus after cycling, dose, safety and device
  selection.
- Added the June 2026 recovery meta-analysis, which found no clear soreness or
  maximum-strength benefit and warned that dose analyses were exploratory.
- Removed claims that massage guns have two proven recovery benefits, reliably
  reduce soreness, activate muscles, flush lactate, break scar tissue or share
  one established mechanism with foam rolling.
- Removed universal 15-second, 60–90-second and one-to-two-minute protocols.
- Removed the unsupported claim that mid-range and premium devices produce the
  same physiological effect; the new buyer guide separates practical features
  from the absence of a proven price-response relationship.
- Added a conservative anatomy, injury and symptom boundary plus the published
  rhabdomyolysis case without turning one case into an incidence estimate.
- Aligned the recovery hub, recovery-tools answer, foam-rolling answer and
  sports-massage surface with the owner.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=massage-gun-guide` inside the existing
  single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added bounded LLM discovery, curated IndexNow discovery and AI benchmark
  prompt 366.
