# Persistent cycling fatigue owner refresh — 2026-08-31

## Search Console baseline

The 90-day page-filtered Google Search Console view for
`/blog/cycling-chronic-fatigue-when-tiredness-persists-guide` recorded:

- **15 clicks**;
- **741 web impressions**;
- **2.0% CTR**;
- **6.8 average position**; and
- **301 Google AI-feature impressions**.

Much of the remaining query set was anonymised. Visible demand included
`profound fatigue` and ME/CFS-related language, confirming that this owner can
surface beside clinically sensitive searches and must distinguish symptoms from
diagnoses accurately.

## Ownership decision

Keep this URL as the persistent, unexplained and post-viral fatigue owner. Keep
`/blog/cycling-fatigue-signs-when-to-back-off` as the short-term symptom and
training-decision owner, `/blog/overtraining-vs-overreaching-cyclists` as the
category-comparison owner and `/blog/cycling-recovering-from-overtraining-guide`
as the OTS recovery owner.

## Trust and answer upgrade

The refreshed owner:

- removes the unsupported three-to-four-week medical diagnosis threshold;
- removes universal blood-panel, ferritin, vitamin and cortisol prescriptions;
- removes self-directed iron treatment and fixed ferritin targets;
- removes fixed post-viral, ME/CFS and return-to-cycling calendars;
- separates persistent fatigue as a symptom from ME/CFS as a diagnosis;
- makes delayed post-exertional worsening a first-class decision point;
- incorporates NHS, NICE and CDC guidance alongside athlete-fatigue, iron,
  REDs and overtraining evidence;
- states the NICE boundary against fixed incremental graded exercise therapy
  for ME/CFS; and
- routes interested riders into the single Beehiiv app-waitlist audience with
  `source=persistent-fatigue-guide`.
