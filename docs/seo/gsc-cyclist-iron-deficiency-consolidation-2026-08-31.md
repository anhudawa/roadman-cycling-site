## Ownership decision

Keep `/blog/iron-deficiency-cyclists-masters` as the canonical owner because it
has 68% of cluster clicks, the strongest position and more than four times the
AI visibility of either later duplicate. Broaden its answer beyond masters while
retaining a dedicated masters section.

Permanently redirect the two later pages and update every active internal link,
topic list and discovery surface to the owner.

Keep `/blog/blood-testing-cyclists-what-to-check-guide` as the broader
multi-marker testing owner. The retained iron page owns iron-deficiency risk,
symptoms, ferritin interpretation, food, hepcidin, treatment and training
decisions.

## Trust and answer upgrade

The consolidated owner:

- removes a universal 50–150 ng/mL athlete-optimal ferritin range;
- removes guaranteed FTP, watt, VO2max and recovery-time gains;
- removes fixed six-month screening and supplement-retest calendars;
- removes claims that pedalling causes foot-strike haemolysis;
- removes a universal ferrous-bisglycinate and alternate-day protocol;
- removes ferritin-based intravenous-iron criteria;
- makes a complete iron pattern and investigation of the cause central;
- separates iron deficiency, iron-deficiency anaemia and REDs;
- covers menstruation, donation, diet, energy availability, gastrointestinal
  health and unexplained deficiency in men or postmenopausal riders;
- cites WHO, NHS, IOC and athlete screening, hepcidin, absorption and treatment
  evidence; and
- routes interested riders into the single Beehiiv app-waitlist audience with
  `source=iron-guide`.
