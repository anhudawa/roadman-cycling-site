# Masters strength podcast package — 31 August 2026

## Why this phase is next

Search Console recorded 359 clicks and 8,400 strength-query impressions over
the three months ending 29 August, with 4.3% CTR and average position 7.7. The
largest individual query was “strength training for cyclists” at 94 clicks and
1,625 impressions. This is established demand beside Roadman's forthcoming
strength-and-recovery app, not a speculative new topic.

The podcast coverage audit then identified
`ep-new-study-finally-confirms-what-winning-masters-cyclists-have-known` as the
strongest incomplete strength episode. It scored 79/100 for opportunity but was
missing chapters, reviewed citations and presenter context.

## The trust problem

The episode summaries promoted several conclusions the cited meta-analysis did
not establish: a uniquely greater effect in masters riders, a universal two-day
optimum, no possible VO2max trade-off and a mandatory compound-lift list. The
raw conversation remains available as the historical transcript, while every
retrievable editorial surface now carries the reviewed evidence boundary.

## Evidence boundary

The cyclist-only review pooled 17 controlled studies, 262 adults (60 women) and
programmes lasting 5–25 weeks with 1–3 weekly sessions. Heavy strength training
improved cycling efficiency, anaerobic power and a combined performance outcome.
It did not significantly change VO2max, power at VO2max, maximal metabolic
steady state or anaerobic capacity.

The authors rated the evidence low-certainty and said it could not support
robust recommendations about optimal implementation. It did not establish a
special over-40 subgroup, one mandatory exercise list or exactly two weekly
sessions as the universal optimum.

## Package contents

- 10 transcript-verified chapters across the 16-minute episode;
- five reviewed primary, systematic and public-health sources;
- five reviewed evidence claims, four bounded FAQs and three short first-party
  quotes;
- rewritten title, description, answer capsule, takeaways and AI citation block;
- explicit links to the strength evidence owner, strength-session planner and
  single app waiting list; and
- regression coverage plus curated discovery submission.
