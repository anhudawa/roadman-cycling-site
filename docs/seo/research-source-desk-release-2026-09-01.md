# Roadman research source desk — release brief

Prepared: 1 September 2026

Public owner: `/about/press`

Asset catalogue: `/feeds/research-assets.json`

## Purpose

The press page is now a usable source desk rather than a brand-bio page with one
featured report. It reads the same four-asset registry used by Roadman's public
research catalogue, so editors and AI systems see one maintained name, type,
version, method page, download and evidence boundary for each asset.

This phase makes no outreach or earned-coverage claim. External messages still
require Roadman owner approval and should be sent individually using a relevant
asset, not as a generic link-building campaign.

## Editorial routes

| Asset | Correct editorial use | Claim to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amateur Cycling Performance Report 2026 | Cite directional percentile estimates with the stated population and source method | A representative study of all cyclists |
| Cycling Podcast Archive Study 2026 | Analyse Roadman's frozen, searchable on-site archive snapshot | A study of the global podcast market or listener demand |
| Sportive Readiness Index 2026 | Use or critique the five-domain event-preparation framework | A validated score, medical clearance or completion predictor |
| Amateur Cyclist Fuelling Benchmarks 2026 | Audit a plan and actual ride against evidence-bounded intake ranges | A survey of what amateur riders eat or a universal prescription |

## Release checks

- `/about/press` must show all four registry entries and each evidence boundary.
- The podcast entry must offer the CSV, not stop at the JSON endpoint.
- The machine-readable catalogue must remain linked from the visible source
  desk.
- Research type labels must stay explicit in the page and feed.
- No outreach should be sent as part of deployment.

## Measurement

- Press-page clicks to each report and download.
- Earned citations and unlinked mentions by asset.
- Referring domains to the method pages rather than only the homepage.
- Media enquiries that name an asset or ask a methodology question.
- AI-citation checks must preserve each asset's evidence boundary.
