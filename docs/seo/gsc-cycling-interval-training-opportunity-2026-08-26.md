# Cycling interval training owner decision — 26 August 2026

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cycling-interval-training-beginners` as the canonical owner for the broad **cycling interval training** intent.

- Rebuild it as a sourced beginner-to-intermediate guide covering definition, readiness, session selection, intensity monitoring, progression and safety.
- Permanently redirect `/blog/cycling-interval-sessions-complete-guide` to the established owner. It is a later, weaker page with the same broad job.
- Keep `/blog/cycling-interval-session-library-guide` live for the distinct **workout library** job.
- Keep `/blog/cycling-vo2max-intervals` and threshold/sprint pages focused on their narrower interval types.

This preserves the URL Google already rewards, transfers the duplicate's history and backlinks, and gives search engines and AI systems one unambiguous broad answer.

## Problems corrected

The old owner contained unsupported or overconfident rules, including:

- a mandatory six-to-eight-week base period;
- a fixed one-then-two-sessions weekly progression;
- a claim that sweet spot provides 90% of threshold benefit at a fraction of the fatigue;
- a progression said to have “never failed anyone”;
- guaranteed transformation within weeks;
- a universal warm-up/cool-down framework and metabolite-clearing claim; and
- interval guidance that blurred broad, VO2max and workout-library ownership.

The replacement separates published evidence from coaching templates, cites six reviewable claims, adds six FAQs and a six-step first-session HowTo, and routes specialist intent to the appropriate companion page.

## Rollback boundary

Keep the permanent redirect. If broad-query performance materially declines after a full comparison window, test title and snippet language on the owner rather than recreating the duplicate. Recreate a separate page only if Search Console demonstrates a genuinely different query job that the workout library and specialist guides do not serve.
