  and an explicit statement that Roadman has not made an app-effectiveness
  claim.
- A five-stage claim ladder: decision-rule verification, closed-beta usability,
  coach-review agreement, prospective real-world pilot and comparative
  effectiveness.
- A public measurement dictionary covering activation, adherence, reason

  denominators, attrition, null findings, protocol deviations and claim labels.

  standard and earning evidence for the product.
- Discovery links from `/app`, `/app/methodology`, the sitemap, product feed,
  MCP and both LLM reference files.

## Product claim boundary

Usability is not effectiveness. Adherence is not causality. Coach agreement is
not proof that a prescription is optimal or medically safe. Until a suitable
prospective comparative study supports a defined outcome for a defined product
version and population, Roadman will not describe the app as proven to improve
cycling performance, prevent injury, diagnose recovery or provide medical
clearance.
