## Decision

Retain `/blog/cycling-recovery-week-what-to-actually-do` as the canonical owner because its URL and existing internal-link graph match the core recovery-week intent. Permanently redirect the duplicate rest-week guide and update all active internal references to the owner.

The rebuilt page:

- gives a direct seven-day plan and answers the TSS modifier;
- treats a 40–60% volume cut as a starting range informed by taper evidence, not a universal recovery-week law;
- distinguishes a race taper from a recurring training recovery week;
- removes unsupported fixed 3:1, masters-age, HRV, heart-rate and day-by-day feeling rules;
- cites systematic reviews and consensus statements directly;
- sends app interest into the single Beehiiv audience through `source=recovery-week`;
- adds AI benchmark prompt 354 and pins the owner in AI discovery surfaces.
