# Cyclist blood-testing owner refresh — 2026-08-31

## Search Console baseline

The 90-day page-filtered Google Search Console view for
`/blog/blood-testing-cyclists-what-to-check-guide` recorded:

- **18 clicks**;
- **1,181 web impressions**;
- **1.5% CTR**;
- **7.1 average position**; and
- **386 Google AI-feature impressions**.

Visible queries included `cycling blood tests` (14 impressions),
`bloodtest+cycling` (5), `cycling+bloodtest` (3) and a thyroid-marker query.
The page is already page-one adjacent and visible in AI search, making trust and
snippet quality more valuable than publishing a second generic blood-panel page.

## Ownership decision

Keep this URL as the broad owner for cyclist blood tests, marker selection,
sample preparation, frequency and result interpretation. Keep the ferritin,
vitamin D, thyroid, hormone, REDs, fatigue and OTS pages as narrower owners for
their respective diagnosis or decision questions.

## Trust and answer upgrade

The refreshed owner:

- removes claims that clinical reference intervals “lie” to athletes;
- removes a universal athlete-optimal-range table;
- removes fixed ferritin, thyroid, testosterone and CRP performance targets;
- removes the universal twice- or three-times-yearly testing calendar;
- removes the claim that every sample should be fasted, morning and taken in a
  recovery week;
- removes claims that FBC, CRP or cortisol-to-testosterone ratio diagnoses
  overtraining or readiness;
- starts with the clinical or preventive-health question that a test should
  answer;
- cites NICE thyroid and Endocrine Society testosterone guidance plus athlete
  testing, iron, REDs, inflammation and workload-biomarker evidence; and
- routes interested riders into the single Beehiiv app-waitlist audience with
  `source=blood-testing-guide`.
