# Cycling strength search measurement release

Released: 1 September 2026

## Outcome

Roadman now has an executable before/after scorecard for two page-one strength
opportunities:

1. Cycling strength-and-conditioning synonym demand, owned by
   `/blog/cycling-strength-training-guide`.
2. The `cycling gym` head term, owned by
   `/blog/cycling-gym-exercises-best`.

The release freezes exact filters, expected owners, seven- and 28-day baselines,
visible page ownership and Google's reporting lag. It prevents a future report
from quietly renaming a query, changing an owner or comparing unequal periods.

## Decision

No content was rewritten in this release. Both pages were materially refreshed
on 26 August and their seven-day hold finishes on 2 September. The 11 September
directional read and 2 October decision read remain the valid checkpoints.

The immediate opportunity is CTR: `cycling gym` produced 121 impressions at
position 8.9 and no clicks over 28 days, while the intended owner already held
119 visible page-row impressions. That makes a measured snippet test more
appropriate than a new page or another broad rewrite.
