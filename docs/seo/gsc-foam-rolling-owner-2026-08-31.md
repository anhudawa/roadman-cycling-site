# Foam-rolling search owner — GSC decision (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cycling-foam-rolling-self-massage-guide` as the single broad owner
for foam rolling for cyclists. The direct answer
`/answers/foam-rolling-massage-recovery` remains a concise supporting surface
and now links to the reviewed owner.

The sports-massage and massage-gun articles retain their narrower product and
service intents. They should support—not duplicate—the broad foam-rolling
decision.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the owner around direct cyclist decisions: soreness, range of motion,
  acute power, long-term performance, timing, dose, IT-band claims and safety.
- Corrected the old claim that the 2019 meta-analysis included 49 studies and
  produced a universal 10–15% soreness reduction; it included 21 studies and
  reported small, outcome-specific effects.
- Removed fixed “optimal” ten-minute, rolling-speed and tender-spot protocols.
- Removed mechanical claims about releasing fascia, changing tissue compliance,
  breaking adhesions, lengthening the IT band and rolling the psoas.
- Added the small negative acute cyclist study and the new positive but limited
  six-month cyclist trial without overstating either.
- Added boundaries for wounds, fractures, acute injury, unexplained swelling,
  clot concern and neurological symptoms.
- Aligned the recovery hub and the two main recovery direct-answer surfaces.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=foam-rolling-guide` inside the existing
  single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added bounded LLM discovery, curated IndexNow discovery and AI benchmark
  prompt 365.

## Measurement

Compare rolling 28-day and three-month performance after recrawl:

  `before`, `after`, `recovery`, `IT band`, `routine` and `power`;
- Google AI-feature impressions and monthly citation results for prompt 365;
- app-waitlist conversions attributed to `foam-rolling-guide`; and
- emergence of another page for the same broad intent.

IndexNow supports participating engines. Google discovery continues through the
canonical URL, internal links, sitemap and normal Search Console workflows.
