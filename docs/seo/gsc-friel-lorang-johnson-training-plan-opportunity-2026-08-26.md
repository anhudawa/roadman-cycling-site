# Friel, Lorang and Johnson training-plan comparison — GSC opportunity and evidence decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026  
**GSC property:** `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`  
**Search type:** Web  
**Baseline window:** 24 May–23 August 2026  
**Release URL:** `/blog/cycling-training-plan-build-friel-lorang-johnson`

## Decision

Preserve the ranking URL and make it the supporting owner for named comparisons of Joe Friel, Dan Lorang and Dylan Johnson. Do not let it own broad cycling-training-plan commercial or methodology intent.

| Intent                                    | Destination                                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Roadman coached training-plan service     | `/training-plans`                                        |
| General cycling training-plan methodology | `/topics/cycling-training-plans`                         |
| Choosing a plan format                    | `/blog/cycling-how-to-choose-a-training-plan-guide`      |
| Friel/Lorang/Johnson comparison           | `/blog/cycling-training-plan-build-friel-lorang-johnson` |

The comparison URL stays indexable. No redirect, canonical change or deindexing is justified by the available data.

## Search opportunity

The page recorded **44 clicks from 3,294 impressions**, **1.3% CTR** and **average position 14.7** across 68 visible GSC query rows in the baseline window. That is a strong page-two opportunity: it already earns named-expert demand but the previous title and generic plan-builder framing did not match that demand precisely.

Named query examples included:

| Query                          | Clicks | Impressions |
| ------------------------------ | -----: | ----------: |
| `joe friel training plans`     |      2 |          19 |
| `lorang method`                |      1 |          27 |

| `dylan johnson training plans` |      0 |          36 |
| `the lorang system`            |      0 |          33 |

The exact broad query `cycling training plan` produced only 1 click and 4 impressions for this URL. Named-method comparison is therefore the useful boundary; expanding generic plan keywords would increase cannibalisation with `/training-plans` and `/topics/cycling-training-plans` without evidence of a broad-query advantage.

## Evidence audit

The previous article blended three unlike sources into one prescriptive framework and contained claims the cited interviews did not support:

- an `80% of value over 12+ weeks` quotation attributed to Dan Lorang was not found in the cited Roadman transcript;
- fixed phase lengths, weekly intensity hierarchies and recovery-week rules were presented as universal;
- Dylan Johnson's 2025 30–35-hour/10–15-hour alternation was described as a transferable model even though Johnson called it a personal experiment, not the gold standard, and said he knew of no study on the exact sequence;
- Lorang's role was stale after his 1 August 2026 move to Head of Performance at Lidl-Trek;
- daily monitoring and life-stress statements were more absolute than the source material supported.

The release replaces those claims with a dated source ledger and explicit evidence labels:

- Friel: coaching framework and expert interview;
- Lorang: practitioner interview about sustainable load, recovery and context;
- Johnson: pre-outcome elite N=1 experiment;
- Galán-Rioja et al. 2023: systematic review finding no evidence for one periodisation winner across eight-to-twelve-week studies in trained road cyclists;
- Mølmen et al. 2019: cautious block-periodisation evidence from small, generally low-quality studies;
- Rosenblat et al. 2025: no overall polarised-versus-pyramidal difference for the stated outcomes and measurement method, with athlete-level interaction.

## Release contract

- Keep the URL, publication history and existing internal references.
- Change title and keywords to named-comparison intent.
- Add `primaryHub: cycling-training-plans`, reviewer, last-reviewed and updated dates.
- State Roadman's commercial conflict and route service facts to `/training-plans`.
- Register the article as supporting—not canonical—content in search ownership and the knowledge graph.
- Add it to both LLM discovery files, the pinned content set, AI benchmark prompt 265 and IndexNow.
- Preserve corrections visibly, including the unsupported Lorang attribution.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim the three sources form one Roadman method.
- Do not scale Johnson's elite volume into an amateur prescription by percentage.
- Do not restore the unsupported Lorang `80%` claim unless a primary recording and exact timestamp substantiate it.
- Do not describe one periodisation or intensity-distribution model as universally best.
- Recheck role labels and dated experiments during future reviews.
