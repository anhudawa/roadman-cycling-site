## Problems corrected

The former cluster stated or implied that:

- 80/20 is an exact time-based weekly prescription;
- research three-zone labels are interchangeable with seven cycling power zones;
- all elite endurance athletes converge on the same distribution;
- moderate training provides minimal or no adaptation;
- polarised training universally beats other models;
- two hard sessions or one hard session per four easy sessions fits every cyclist; and
- amateur implementation can be chosen from weekly hours alone.

The replacement names the zone model and counting method, separates session goal from time in zone, reports elite cyclist distributions, summarises recent comparative evidence and gives an audit process without universal dose promises.

## Evidence record

| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Where did 80/20 originate?                    | Seiler and Kjerland 2006, PMID 16430681; Seiler 2010, PMID 20861519        | Descriptive endurance-athlete evidence, not an exact biological optimum          |
| Do all elite cyclists train polarised?        | Solli et al. 2023, PMID 37964776                                           | Both pyramidal and polarised distributions occur; sport and phase matter         |
| Does polarised always produce better results? | Rosenblat et al. 2019, PMID 29863593; Bellinger et al. 2024, PMID 38717713 | Results depend on outcome, population and a limited evidence base                |
| What does cyclist-specific evidence show?     | Filipe et al. 2025, PMID 39788807                                          | No significant polarised-versus-non-polarised difference across trained cyclists |
| Polarised or pyramidal?                       | Molmen et al. 2025, PMID 39888556                                          | No significant overall winner; performance level may modify response             |
| Is one 8–12-week model established?           | Almquist et al. 2023, PMID 36640771                                        | Seven eligible cyclist studies did not establish one superior model              |

## Rollback boundary

Keep the canonical owner and permanent redirects. If CTR does not improve after a stable comparison window, test the owner's title and description. Do not restore duplicate broad pages or unsupported claims about an exact ratio, useless moderate training, universal superiority or fixed hard-session frequency.
