# GSC decision: cycling HRV search owner

Date: 2026-08-31
Property: `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`
Window: latest three months (2026-05-30 to 2026-08-29)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cycling-hrv-training-guide` as the broad HRV-for-cyclists owner. Permanently redirect `/blog/cycling-heart-rate-variability-guide` to it.

The owner has the stronger history, a query-matched URL, 52 of the two pages' 61 clicks and an average position of 6.7. The second page serves the same measurement, interpretation and training-decision intent. Consolidation gives search engines, AI systems and readers one reviewed answer instead of two contradictory sets of thresholds.

Keep narrower pages live where they solve a distinct problem, including resting heart rate, autonomic recovery, sleep debt, post-illness return and general fatigue. Those pages should link to the HRV owner rather than repeat a separate universal HRV protocol.

## Editorial changes

- Rebuilt the owner around a 60-second multi-input training decision rather than one app traffic light.
- Defined HRV, metrics and measurement boundaries before explaining training use.
- Removed universal 7-day, 60-day, 10%, 15% and 20% prescriptions.
- Removed the claim that high always means recovered or ready and low always means fatigue, illness or overtraining.
- Replaced product rankings and fixed accuracy claims with resting-versus-exercise and ECG-versus-optical evidence boundaries.
- Bounded HRV-guided training claims to systematic reviews: possible benefits for some outcomes and responder rates, but small or uncertain performance superiority.
- Added the 2025 experienced-male-cyclist study as supportive evidence for combining HRV, wellbeing and resting heart rate, with its sample and design limits stated.
- Added clinical boundaries for persistent symptoms, chest pain, fainting, palpitations and unexplained breathlessness.
- Aligned the `what is HRV` and `should cyclists use HRV` direct answers with the reviewed owner.
- Routed app interest to `/app?source=hrv-guide`, inside the existing single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added AI benchmark prompt 360 and pinned the owner in the LLM discovery documents.
