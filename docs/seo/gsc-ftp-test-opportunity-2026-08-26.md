# FTP test search-owner decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**Window:** Three months through 24 August 2026

## Decision

- `/answers/ftp-test-guide` owns broad **ftp test**, **which FTP test** and protocol-selection intent.
- `/tools/ftp-test` owns **FTP test calculator** and fixed-equation result conversion.
- `/answers/20-minute-ftp-test` owns the 20-minute protocol.
- `/answers/how-to-do-a-ramp-test` owns the ramp protocol.
- `/blog/when-to-test-ftp-cycling` owns timing and retest-frequency intent.
- FTP benchmark pages may explain measurement context, but they do not own test selection.

The established answer URL already had 72% of the exact-query impressions. The intervention strengthens that incumbent rather than creating or redirecting to another broad guide.

## Trust and product changes

- Replaced the legacy answer's universal 20-minute, ramp, freshness and retest claims with a method-selection framework.
- Added six visible primary or official references and Article citation nodes through the answer template.
- Made the answer explicit that 20-minute, ramp, sustained and modelled estimates are not automatically interchangeable.
- Added measurement-device, calibration, cooling, pacing and time-to-exhaustion boundaries.
- Narrowed the tool to fixed-factor conversion and linked it to the informational owner.
- Removed the tool's unsupported hard-coded W/kg ability labels.
- Reused the shared gap-free FTP-zone calculation instead of maintaining an overlapping second zone table.
- Replaced “gold standard,” exact one-hour and universal ±3–5% claims in the calculator methodology.
- Added an explicit FTP task map, AI discovery entries, IndexNow priority URLs and a monthly AI-citation prompt.

## Measurement plan

### Seven-day directional check — earliest 5 September 2026

- Confirm Google has recrawled the answer and calculator.
- Compare owner share for `ftp test`, `which ftp test` and `ftp test calculator`.
- Check that protocol-qualified impressions continue to land on the 20-minute, ramp and timing pages.

### Twenty-eight-day decision check — earliest 26 September 2026

- Compare clicks, impressions, CTR, position and page mix with this baseline and a matched preceding period.
- Success means `/answers/ftp-test-guide` becomes the dominant broad-intent click owner and `/tools/ftp-test` gains qualified calculator impressions.
- If benchmark pages still dominate broad clicks, inspect the exact modifiers and snippets before changing their established canonicals.
