# Pogačar Tour preparation search-owner decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**Window:** Three months through 24 August 2026

## Decision

- `/blog/tdf-2026-contenders-preparation-lessons` keeps its established canonical and becomes the reviewed owner for **tour de france pogačar preparation**.
- `/blog/andy-mcgrath-pogacar-not-just-a-freak` retains athlete-profile and biography intent.
- `/blog/pogacar-training-secrets` remains a narrower commentary page and must not make unsupported 2026 programme claims.
- No redirect or new URL is created because the incumbent owns virtually all exact-query visibility.

The intervention fixes freshness, click-through rate and factual trust. The old preview remained in future tense after the Tour and described preparation patterns that did not match the official 2026 start-line record. The maintained owner now gives the public preparation facts, final result, comparison with other contenders, evidence-backed amateur lessons and explicit boundaries around private training data.

## Trust and answer-layer changes

- Replaced the pre-race contenders headline with an exact Pogačar preparation title and post-race result framing.
- Added the documented 16 pre-Tour competition days and Romandie and Suisse wins.
- Added the official final top-five, winning time, 6:26 margin and five-stage total.
- Corrected the old full-Classics versus light-altitude comparison using the official start-line record.
- Separated public race facts from unpublished training load, power, altitude and nutrition details.
- Compared Pogačar, Vingegaard, Evenepoel and Del Toro without claiming one preparation template.
- Added six FAQs, five reviewed claims, official-source links and a correction record.
- Added measured taper and environmental-training evidence without turning pooled research into a universal prescription.
- Added AI discovery, priority recrawl and a monthly citation-monitoring prompt.

## Measurement plan

### Seven-day directional check — earliest 5 September 2026

- Confirm Google has recrawled the established URL and is showing the new Pogačar-first title and description.
- Compare CTR for Pogačar preparation, training, Tour 2026 and pre-Tour race modifiers.
- Confirm the profile and secrets articles remain support rather than becoming competing broad owners.

### Twenty-eight-day decision check — earliest 26 September 2026

- Compare clicks, impressions, CTR and average position with this baseline and a matched preceding period.
- Success means the incumbent retains at least 95% of exact-query impressions and improves CTR from 0.1% while gaining qualified preparation and training visibility.
- If position remains around 8 but CTR does not improve, inspect the live SERP and test the title and description before changing the URL or canonical.
