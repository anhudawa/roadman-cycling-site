# Amateur bike-fit “one change” search opportunity — 25 August 2026

## Scope

Read-only Google Search Console review for the `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`
property. Search type was Web and the date range was 24 May–23 August 2026.
No validation or indexing control was used.

## Page-level demand

`/blog/bike-fit-one-change-amateurs-should-make` recorded:

- 60 clicks
- 3,130 impressions
- 1.9% CTR
- average position 6.1
- 147 visible query rows

The visible rows are highly fragmented. The largest displayed query had only
five impressions, and many of the first rows were broad variants such as
`bicycle exercise`, `bike fitting`, `cleats` and `pro cyclist bike fit`.
Most page-level impressions are therefore anonymised rather than represented by
a clear visible head query. Visible rows must not be summed as a replacement for
the page total.

## Content risk in the previous version

The page's title, description, answer capsule and body told every amateur to
move both cleats rearward by 5–10mm. It promised instant improvement, greater
posterior-chain recruitment, more power and less calf fatigue. It also treated
hip rocking as proof of a high saddle, prescribed a fixed 5mm drop and claimed
a professional fit returned comfort and watts within a few rides.

Those claims conflict with the reviewed cleat and bike-fit owners. They also go
beyond the current position evidence and create a prominent trust contradiction
on a URL already averaging position 6.1.

## Decision

1. Keep the URL and answer the narrow premise: there is no one component change
   every amateur cyclist should make.
2. Make the highest-value first action procedural—record the setup, check
   mechanical safety, identify recent changes and test one reversible variable.
3. Route the complete home-fit intent to `/blog/bike-fit-guide-cyclists` and
   cleat installation/measurement to
   `/blog/cycling-knee-tracking-cleat-setup-guide`.
4. Compare the cleat and saddle-height evidence without turning kinematic
   findings into guaranteed performance or injury outcomes.
5. Keep original Roadman expert episodes as related context without claiming
   that a guest reviewed the article or prescribed one rule for every rider.
6. Separate fitter, mechanic and clinician responsibilities.

## Measurement

Compare this URL at the 7-day and 28-day checkpoints. Watch clicks, impressions,
CTR, average position, visible-query relevance and whether broad how-to demand
moves toward the canonical bike-fit and cleat owners.
