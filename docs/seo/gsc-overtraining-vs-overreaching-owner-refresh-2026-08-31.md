## Ownership decision

Keep this URL as the comparison owner for acute fatigue, functional
overreaching, non-functional overreaching and overtraining syndrome. Preserve
`/blog/cycling-fatigue-signs-when-to-back-off` as the symptom and immediate
back-off owner, and preserve `/blog/cycling-recovering-from-overtraining-guide`
as the longer recovery/return owner.

## Evidence and answer upgrade

The refreshed comparison owner:

- answers the difference in the first paragraph and a comparison table;
- removes fixed days/weeks/months diagnostic boundaries;
- explains that classification is partly retrospective and OTS is a diagnosis
  of exclusion;
- removes the claim that functional overreaching is necessary or guarantees a
  supercompensation rebound;
- separates monitoring inputs from diagnostic markers;
- covers illness, low energy availability, REDs, sleep, mood and other
  differentials without diagnosing them;
- cites the ECSS/ACSM consensus and six systematic or critical reviews; and
- routes app interest into the single Beehiiv audience with
  `source=overreaching-guide`.
