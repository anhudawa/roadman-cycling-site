The sitewide report recorded 1,639,123 generative-AI impressions in the same

surface, not a speculative future project.

## Release decision

The complete evidence rewrite published on 26 August is preserved. The search
title and the page's canonical job are not changed again before their planned
cohort review. This release adds the demonstrated missing answer:

- a direct “90 g carbohydrates per hour” section;
- two current, label-verified gel-and-drink examples from official manufacturer
  information;
- visible arithmetic rather than a product endorsement;
- a three-hour shopping calculation;
- fluid, sodium, caffeine, tolerance and formulation-change boundaries; and
- a dedicated AI benchmark prompt matching the observed buyer-style query.

## Measurement

Track Web impressions, clicks, CTR and position for the product-plan query and
the broader `cycling fueling` family. In the Generative AI report, compare the
page's seven-day and 28-day impressions without interpreting impressions as
clicks. The quality check is whether generated answers preserve three facts:
the examples are arithmetic rather than rankings, fluid remains a separate
decision, and products must be checked and rehearsed because formulations and
tolerance can change.
