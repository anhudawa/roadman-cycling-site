# Resting-heart-rate search owner — GSC decision (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/resting-heart-rate-masters-cyclists` as the single broad owner for
cyclist resting heart rate, including normal values, professional-cyclist
comparisons, measurement, training context, masters riders and health red
flags. No competing page currently warrants consolidation or a redirect.

The URL is retained despite its masters-oriented slug because it already has
meaningful demand and rankings. The title, metadata and answer structure now
serve the broader query family without sacrificing its established URL equity.

existing owner instead of asking a new URL to mature.

## What changed

- Reframed the owner around the broad cyclist query rather than an invented
  masters-only benchmark table.
- Added a 60-second decision table, normal-versus-athletic-bradycardia context,
  professional-cyclist comparison, measurement protocol, wearable limitations,
  masters cardiovascular boundaries, RHR-versus-HRV distinctions and eight
  direct FAQs.
- Removed universal 3, 5, 7 and 10 bpm commands, fixed athlete bands, illness
  predictions and claims that a professional team uses one threshold.
- Checked claims against exercise and athlete-monitoring reviews, wrist-device
  validity evidence, international athlete ECG recommendations, American Heart
  Association guidance and the 2026 masters-athlete cardiovascular consensus.
- Aligned the recovery hub, masters hub, problem finder and three related direct
  answers so search visitors do not receive contradictory thresholds elsewhere.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=rhr-guide`, inside the existing single
  `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added the owner to bounded LLM discovery, curated IndexNow discovery and AI
  benchmark prompt 362.

## Measurement

Compare rolling 28-day and three-month performance after recrawl:

- clicks, impressions, CTR and average position for the owner;
- non-brand queries containing `cyclist`, `cycling`, `resting heart rate`,
  `professional`, `pro`, `masters`, `low`, `high` and `normal`;
- Google AI-feature impressions and monthly citation results for prompt 362;
- app-waitlist conversion events attributed to `rhr-guide`; and
- accidental emergence of a second URL for the same broad intent.

IndexNow supports participating engines; Google discovery continues through the
canonical URL, internal links, sitemap and normal Search Console workflows.
