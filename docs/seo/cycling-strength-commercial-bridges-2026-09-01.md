# Cycling strength commercial bridges — 1 September 2026

## Decision

`/strength-training` is the sole indexable owner for Roadman's fixed, paid 12-week cycling strength course. Four high-authority educational resources now link to it without changing their informational search ownership:

- `/blog/cycling-strength-training-guide`
- `/blog/cycling-strength-training-12-week-beginner-plan`
- `/blog/cycling-gym-exercises-best`
- `/topics/cycling-strength-conditioning`

The pages explicitly separate three jobs:

1. fixed self-guided course → `/strength-training`;
2. upcoming adaptive strength and recovery app → `/app` with source attribution; and
3. individual bike-and-gym oversight → `/coaching`.

## Search baseline

Search Console on 1 September 2026 showed the existing strength cluster already had material demand:

- `strength training for cyclists`: 144 clicks, 2,080 impressions, 6.9% CTR and average position 6.5;
- `cycling strength training`: 33 clicks, 1,220 impressions, 2.7% CTR and average position 9.1; and
- `/blog/cycling-strength-training-guide` generated 98 clicks and 1,672 impressions for the first query.

This phase is an internal authority and conversion-routing change, not a rewrite of the recently reviewed informational owners.

## Measurement contract

Protect the existing owner pages from another material rewrite while the link change settles. Compare course-page impressions, non-branded clicks, referring landing pages and checkout clicks after 7, 14 and 28 days. Keep page-level acquisition attribution for the app waitlist, but keep every signup in the same Beehiiv audience.
