# Time-crunched cyclist search-owner decision — 26 August 2026

## Decision

Keep and strengthen the established owner:

`/blog/time-crunched-cyclist-8-hours-week`

Do not redirect or merge the benchmark, training-framework or related training
articles. They answer narrower follow-up intents and should support this URL.
The established page already owns the broad exact query decisively; the main
opportunity is better CTR and stronger evidence, not a URL change.

## Problems found

- The title was longer and less plan-focused than the query's practical intent.
- The answer capsule presented exactly two hard sessions, an 80/20 split and a
  long ride as universally required.
- The article claimed research proved training structure matters more than
  volume after six hours, which the cited work did not establish.
- Threshold was described as the session every rider should always protect,
  regardless of event demand or diagnosed limiter.
- Moderate-intensity riding was treated as inherently ineffective even though
  multiple intensity distributions can improve endurance performance.
- A fixed fourth-week deload and fixed percentage reduction were presented as
  evidence-based laws rather than coaching options.
- Source limitations, female underrepresentation and the small direct cycling
  evidence base were not disclosed.

## Changes prepared

- Short, intent-matched title: `Time-Crunched Cyclist: 8-Hour Training Plan
  (2026)`.
- Direct answer capsule and five FAQs written for search and AI extraction.
- General and recovery-limited weekly templates, with goal-specific choices for
  gran fondo, time-trial, climbing, punchy racing and high-stress weeks.
- Four visible claim/evidence/implication rows and six linked primary or
  synthesis sources.
- Explicit evidence boundary around the small six-week male-cyclist trial and
  broader mixed-sport reviews.
- Human review date, reviewer scope, canonical training-plan hub, expert entity,
  crawler discovery and AI benchmark prompt.
- Existing support pages retained and linked without changing the owner URL.
