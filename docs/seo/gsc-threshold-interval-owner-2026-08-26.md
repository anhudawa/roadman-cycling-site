# Cycling threshold-interval search-owner decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**UI window observed:** 25 May–24 August 2026 (`3 months`; last updated approximately 5.5 hours before collection)

## Decision

- `/answers/threshold-intervals-guide` is the canonical owner for **threshold intervals**, **threshold training cycling**, threshold-workout formats, pacing, progression and weekly placement.
- `/blog/cycling-threshold-intervals-guide` permanently redirects to that established owner. It was the only indexable page serving the same complete job.
- `/glossary/threshold` retains the short definition and Zone 4 terminology job.
- `/blog/cycling-threshold-power-explained-guide` retains LT1, LT2, MLSS and threshold-model comparison intent.
- `/answers/improve-threshold-power` retains the wider sustainable-power improvement and limiter-diagnosis job.
- `/blog/sweet-spot-vs-threshold-vs-polarised-comparison` and `/compare/sweet-spot-vs-threshold` retain comparison intent.
- `/podcast/ep-2540-secret-to-improving-threshold-dose-frequency-duration` retains first-party episode intent and supports the answer rather than being folded into it.

The decision follows the incumbent rather than moving demand to a new URL. The answer already had **117 clicks and 6.81K impressions**, and it won every material exact threshold-interval query. The later blog had **44 clicks and 2.82K impressions** overall but only one exact-query click across the measured head terms.

### Why the definition and comparison pages remain

For `threshold cycling`, Search Console reported 1 click, 129 impressions and position 10.9. The comparison article held the click, while the glossary had 47 impressions, the interval answer 33 and the improvement answer 14. The retired blog had only one impression at position 74.

For `what is threshold in cycling`, the glossary held 45 page-row impressions at position 9.4; the interval answer held six. This supports keeping the concise definition separate from workout execution.

## Trust and answer-layer changes

- Replaced the compulsory `2×20 at 95–105% FTP` framing with scalable `3×8`, `4×10`, `3×12` and `2×20` coaching formats.
- Made clear that FTP, critical power, lactate threshold, MLSS and ventilatory thresholds are related but not interchangeable individual anchors.
- Removed universal claims about the best workout, exact weekly frequency, fixed carbohydrate timing and guaranteed adaptation.
- Added eight visible research sources, Article citation nodes, Anthony Walsh review and an explicit evidence-limit statement.
- Added power, heart-rate, breathing, RPE, environment, measurement and next-day-response boundaries.
- Added a five-step progression that changes one variable at a time.
- Repointed active editorial links to the answer, removed the duplicate blog from blog collections and added a permanent redirect.
- Added the owner to short and full AI discovery, IndexNow priority submission and the monthly AI-citation benchmark.
