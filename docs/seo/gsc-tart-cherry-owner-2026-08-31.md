# Tart-cherry search owner — GSC decision (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/cycling-tart-cherry-juice-recovery-guide` as the single broad owner
for tart-cherry cycling recovery, performance, sleep, dose, timing, product and
safety intent. Anti-inflammatory-food, recovery-food, supplement, Tour recovery
and podcast pages remain supporting surfaces and link to the reviewed owner.

No second editorial page should make a competing universal dose or recovery
promise.

## Search Console baseline

Latest three-month Search Console view on 31 August 2026:

| URL | Clicks | Web impressions | CTR | Average position | Google AI-feature impressions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `/blog/cycling-tart-cherry-juice-recovery-guide` | 47 | 6,948 | 0.7% | 9.4 | 1,350 |

Visible queries included `tart cherry juice cycling`, `tart cherry juice for
cycling`, `tart cherry juice cycling recovery`, `cherry juice cycling`,
`cycling cherry juice`, `why do cyclists drink cherry juice` and `cherry juice
cycling recovery`.

The page-one average position, low CTR and large AI-search footprint make this
an unusually strong opportunity: the established URL needs a clearer answer and
better evidence boundaries, not a competing article.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the owner around the mixed intent: recovery, soreness, performance,
  sleep, dose, timing, product format, buying and safety.
- Added the April 2026 athlete meta-analysis of 19 trials. It found selected
  maximal-voluntary-contraction and C-reactive-protein effects, no significant
  pooled soreness, CK, IL-6, TNF-alpha or range-of-motion effect, substantial
  heterogeneity and very-low-to-moderate certainty.
- Put the small positive 2015 cyclist trial beside the directly relevant 2024
  carbohydrate-matched cyclist trial, which was null for performance and
  recovery outcomes.
- Kept the 2020 endurance-performance meta-analysis visible while making the
  later null cyclist evidence and small pooled effect explicit.
- Replaced fixed sleep-duration promises with the 2025 seven-study review's
  limited and heterogeneous conclusion.
- Removed a universal 30 ml twice-daily protocol, exact cherry equivalents,
  ibuprofen equivalence, guaranteed soreness reduction, daily adaptation
  warnings, fixed prices and unsupported product interchangeability.
- Added a label-first product checklist, medication and medical-context boundary
  and anti-doping batch-testing consideration.
- Aligned the recovery hub, anti-inflammatory-food guide, supplement guides and
  the search-visible summary of the legacy tart-cherry podcast episode.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=tart-cherry-guide` inside the existing
  single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added bounded LLM discovery, curated IndexNow discovery and AI benchmark
  prompt 367.

## Measurement

Compare rolling 28-day and three-month performance after recrawl:

- clicks, impressions, CTR and average position for the owner;
- non-brand queries containing `tart cherry`, `cherry juice`, `cycling`,
  `recovery`, `sleep`, `dose`, `timing`, `before`, `after` and `soreness`;
- Google AI-feature impressions and monthly citation results for prompt 367;
- app-waitlist conversions attributed to `tart-cherry-guide`; and
- emergence of a second page for the same broad intent.

IndexNow supports participating engines. Google discovery continues through the
canonical URL, internal links, sitemap and normal Search Console workflows.
