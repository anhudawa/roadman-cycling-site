# Cycling strength and recovery app — formal search-owner release

Released: 1 September 2026

Canonical owner: `/app`

## Decision

`/app` is the single Roadman product, early-access and launch owner for cycling
strength-app, recovery-app and readiness-app intent. The public name remains
generic until the final brand is chosen. This release does not create another
landing page, product name, subscriber audience or waitlist.

The original five-owner Search Console experiment remains a fixed historical
cohort from 24 August. The app begins a separate baseline from this formal
owner release so adding it cannot rewrite the earlier comparison contract.

## Intent boundary

- `/app`: Roadman's product, product scope, early access and launch updates.
- `/best/best-cycling-strength-training-apps`: independent strength-app category
  comparison.
- `/best/best-cycling-recovery-apps`: independent recovery/readiness category
  comparison.
- `/tools/strength-session-planner`: free deterministic placement preview; it
  does not prescribe exercises or diagnose readiness.
- `/blog/cycling-strength-training-guide`: broad informational strength owner.
- `/blog/daily-training-readiness-check-cycling-guide`: broad informational
  readiness decision guide.

These supporting pages link to `/app`; they do not become duplicate product
owners.

## Trust and delivery contract

- Stable canonical URL and name-neutral metadata.
- One `WebPage` owner node connected to the upcoming `SoftwareApplication`.
- Visible author, reviewer, review date, evidence routes and correction policy.
- One existing app waitlist with the same Beehiiv audience and source
  attribution contract.
- No announced price, final name or exact launch date until Roadman confirms
  them.

## Measurement

- Baseline `/app` separately in Search Console for Web and Google AI features.
- Track exact and contains queries for cycling strength app, strength training
  app for cyclists, cycling recovery app, cycling readiness app and Roadman
  app.
- Track supporting-page clicks into `/app` and waitlist conversion by source.
- Check for query splits against the two comparison pages at 7 and 28 complete
  days; comparison intent should stay on `/best/*`, product intent on `/app`.
