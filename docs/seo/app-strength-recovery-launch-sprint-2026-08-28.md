# Roadman app search launch sprint

**Decision date:** 2026-08-28

## Permanent owner

- Canonical URL: `/app`
- Public descriptor: `Roadman cycling strength and recovery app`
- The internal Pocket Coach name must not appear publicly.
- The URL remains stable when the final product name, price and App Store URL are announced.
- Until launch details are confirmed, the page must not invent a product name, price, availability date, review rating or Android release.

Every commercial discovery page links to the permanent `/app` owner. The unreleased Roadman app is disclosed but excluded from rankings until it is available and can be tested. Product name, price, launch date and App Store claims remain exclusive to `/app` after approval.

## Compressed release sequence

### Release 1 — now

- Ship `/app` with accurate product boundaries and a Beehiiv/CRM-tagged early-access capture.
- Add `WebPage`, `SoftwareApplication`, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data without a fabricated price or offer.
- Add sitewide footer, sitemap, strength-page, strength-hub, recovery-hub, LLM discovery and IndexNow links.
- Establish AI-search benchmark prompts for the app category.

### Release 2 — next 48 hours

- Pull Search Console query/page data for strength, gym, recovery, soreness, readiness and app terms.
- Assign one owner to every meaningful query family and remove overlapping exact-match language from support pages.
- Correct unsupported fixed recovery rules before expanding the cluster.

### Release 3 — following 3–5 days

- Turn the existing readiness and recovery tools into the acquisition path for `/app`.
- Build a public strength-placement planner around time available, ride priority and gym-day conflicts.
- Publish web-to-app route contracts so every useful web result can deep-link to the matching app action at launch.

### Release 4 — week two

- Ship commercial and comparison owners: cycling strength app, recovery app, cycling app with gym work, and masters strength app. **Implemented 2026-08-31.**
- Add the final name, App Store URL, verified pricing, screenshots and complete software-app offer markup only when approved.
- Implement iOS Universal Links and measure search → waitlist → install → first-session activation.

## Measurement

- Search: impressions, clicks, CTR, position and query-to-page ownership by cluster.
- Conversion: early-access signup rate by source and page.
- Launch: waitlist-to-install, install-to-onboarding and first/second strength-session completion.
- AI search: monthly citation checks for the eight app benchmark prompts.
