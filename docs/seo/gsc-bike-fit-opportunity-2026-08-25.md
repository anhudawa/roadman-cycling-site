# Bike-fitting search opportunity — GSC decision record

**Decision date:** 25 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**Performance window:** 24 May–23 August 2026 (three months; Search Console last updated approximately 6.5 hours before collection)

## Decision

- `/blog/bike-fit-guide-cyclists` remains Roadman's canonical owner for the head-term and complete DIY road-bike fitting intent.
- `/topics/bike-fitting` becomes the knowledge-directory owner: evidence limits, expert interviews and routes to narrower fit questions. Its title, description and keywords no longer target the exact phrase `bike fitting guide`.
- `/blog/mtb-bike-fit-basics` remains indexable for mountain-bike and trail-cockpit intent. Its small appearance for the broad exact query does not justify a redirect because its user job is materially different.
- The canonical guide receives the CTR, evidence, reviewer, medical-boundary and direct-answer upgrade rather than being replaced with a new URL.

## Search Console evidence

The canonical page recorded **141 clicks, 23,270 impressions, 0.6% CTR and average position 7.0** across all Web queries during the window. Search Console exposed 204 query rows for the page.

The exact query **`bike fitting guide`** recorded **5 clicks, 8,452 impressions, 0.1% CTR and average position 6.7**. This is a snippet and trust opportunity on an already competitive ranking, not a justification for publishing another guide.

| URL | Clicks | Impressions | Role after remediation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `/blog/bike-fit-guide-cyclists` | 4 | 7,990 | Canonical complete road/DIY guide |
| `/blog/mtb-bike-fit-basics` | 1 | 457 | Distinct mountain-bike fit guide |
| `/topics/bike-fitting` | 0 | 8 | Knowledge directory and route map |

Search Console notes that filtered card totals and table rows can be partial, so the exposed URL rows do not need to add exactly to the card total. The ownership decision rests on the canonical page receiving approximately 95% of the exposed impressions and on the distinct MTB intent.

Other visible queries for the canonical page included `bike fit guide`, `KOPS bike fit`, `25 degree knee angle cycling`, `bike fitting manual`, `Hamley method saddle height`, `bike fit knee over pedal` and `DIY bike fit`. This supports a method-led answer rather than a price list or product roundup.

## Content and trust defects found

The incumbent ranked page had a stray list item immediately after frontmatter, which could contaminate the opening and snippet. It also made claims that were too universal or unsupported, including:

- guaranteed watt and injury-prevention returns from a fit;
- a fixed global fit price and a five-hours-per-week buying threshold;
- a universal two-year re-fit schedule;
- an unqualified 25–35-degree knee-angle prescription without naming static or dynamic measurement;
- front/back knee-pain maps presented as adjustment diagnoses;
- a blanket 5–10mm rearward cleat move claimed to engage glutes and hamstrings;
- universal float, bar-width and crank-length prescriptions; and
- an eight-percent power-loss claim presented as a general outcome.

The topic hub repeated stronger claims: 10–20 guaranteed watts, pain elimination, injury prevention, universal age-based positions, exact symptom diagnoses and a fixed $150–$300 price.

## Remediation

1. Put the exact query first in a concise 42-character SEO title and give the snippet a clear DIY/evidence promise.
2. Replace the opening with a direct answer and reversible ten-minute baseline.
3. Separate static, dynamic and anthropometric saddle-height methods.
4. Treat KOPS as a reference rather than a rule or diagnosis.
5. Replace universal cleat and crank prescriptions with the limits of current controlled studies.
6. Add cited claims, named human review, reviewed date, primary research links, corrections route and clinical/mechanical boundaries.
7. Give the topic hub a directory role and remove its exact head-term title/keyword overlap.
8. Route one high-priority AI benchmark prompt and the next IndexNow release to the canonical guide.

## Measurement

Use the frozen 24 August site baseline for overall Phase 2 comparison. For this page-level batch, retain the three-month figures above as diagnostic context, then compare the exact canonical URL and exact query after a complete 28-day post-release window. Track clicks, impressions, CTR, average position and the number of competing Roadman URLs.

Do not judge CTR from same-day or incomplete post-release data. A title or snippet change is successful only if Google recrawls the page and a complete comparison window shows improvement without loss of relevant impressions.
