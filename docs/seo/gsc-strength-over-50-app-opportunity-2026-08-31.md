## Why this page needed a trust rebuild

The previous version was a strong search and AI retrieval surface feeding into
fixed prescriptions and unsupported guarantees. It claimed:

- two sessions beats five;
- loaded resistance is the only proven way to preserve fast-twitch recruitment
  and on-bike power;
- bands and bodyweight cannot be strength training;
- one cycling-specific exercise list has lower injury exposure with the same
  stimulus as heavy bilateral lifting;
- no meaningful weight or speed downside;
- one universal base, build and race-season frequency;
- deterministic 24-, 48- and 72-hour recovery windows;
- one masters-specific protein and sleep prescription; and
- a cyclist meta-analysis had demonstrated a favourable over-40 or over-50
  subgroup.

The current cyclist-only meta-analysis did not report that masters subgroup and
graded outcome certainty low. The old page therefore conflicted with the broad
strength owner, the corrected masters podcast and the upcoming app's stated
readiness guardrails.

## Release decision

The stable URL and its high-performing search title are preserved. The content
now separates:

1. the substantial older-adult strength and concurrent-training evidence;
2. the promising but low-certainty cyclist-performance evidence; and
3. the missing evidence for one exact over-50 cycling protocol.

The page provides a conservative two-session starting framework, lower-volume
progression, adaptable movement patterns, two bike-week placement options,
in-season volume reduction, safety escalation and explicit outcome tracking. It
links into the strength-session planner, recovery screen and training-readiness
check.

## Product handoff

This is the highest demonstrated masters-strength feeder into the upcoming
strength and recovery app. The CTA states the app's actual job: fit 30-, 45- or
60-minute strength around existing rides and use readiness guardrails to hold or
reduce working-set volume with an explanation. It routes to the single early-
access list at `/app?source=strength-over-50-guide`.

The page also answers the demonstrated PDF demand with a free printable 10-week
plan at `/downloads/strength-training-for-cyclists-over-50-plan.pdf`. The download
contains the adaptable sessions, progression boundaries, bike-week worksheet,
readiness guardrails and session log, and routes to the same app list.
