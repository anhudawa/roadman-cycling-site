## Owner decision

Preserve these distinct intents as supporting pages:

- `/blog/overtraining-vs-overreaching-cyclists` for the terminology comparison;
- `/blog/cycling-chronic-fatigue-when-tiredness-persists-guide` for the persistent-fatigue differential;
- `/blog/cycling-recovering-from-overtraining-guide` for the longer return path after assessment.

The owner was rebuilt to:

- answer the back-off decision before discussing diagnostic labels;
- provide seven symptom groups and a 60-second action table;
- remove fixed resting-HR, HRV, symptom-count and recovery-calendar rules;
- explain that overtraining syndrome has no gold-standard marker and is a diagnosis of exclusion;
- cover low energy availability, illness and medical red flags;
- route app interest into the single audience through `source=fatigue-guide`;
- add AI benchmark prompt 357 and priority crawler discovery coverage.
