## Change and measurement contract

Framework edition 1.0 replaces the placeholder. It gives a direct answer, five evidence domains, confirmed/partial/not-yet-demonstrated states, 16/12/8/4/1-week review points, official 2026 event-demand examples, research boundaries, a CSV worksheet, a cite block and explicit limitations. It removes unsupported claims about representative rider benchmarks, back-testing and a predictive pass score.

Recheck Web queries, CTR, position and Generative AI impressions after 7, 14 and 28 days. Do not rewrite the page during the first 14 days unless delivery, indexing, event-source accuracy or a material evidence/safety error requires it.
