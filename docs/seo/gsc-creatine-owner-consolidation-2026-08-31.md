# Creatine-for-cyclists owner — GSC consolidation (31 August 2026)

## Decision

Keep `/blog/creatine-for-cyclists-thirty-day-data` as the single long-form owner
for creatine benefits, endurance, sprints, strength, masters, dose, timing,
loading, cycling, weight, cognition, safety and product intent.

Permanently redirect the three overlapping articles:

- `/blog/cycling-creatine-supplementation-guide`;
- `/blog/creatine-for-cyclists-30-day-experiment`; and
- `/blog/creatine-for-cyclists-30-day-protocol`.

The concise `/answers/should-cyclists-take-creatine` page remains a supporting
AI-answer surface and links to the consolidated owner.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the strongest URL as the complete evidence and decision owner.
- Added the 2023 trained-endurance meta-analysis and its null pooled result.
- Added the 2026 endurance and mixed-sport scoping review, with repeated-sprint
  promise separated from inconsistent direct cycling outcomes.
- Anchored the strongest positive case in creatine plus resistance training,
  while making cycling transfer a separate question.
- Bounded masters claims to older-adult resistance-training evidence with study
  mean ages of 57–70.
- Replaced the universal 1–2 kg promise with the 143-study average of 0.86 kg
  and individual monitoring.
- Replaced cognitive-performance guarantees with the conflicting reviews and
  EFSA's conclusion that cause and effect was not established.
- Kept Anthony's 30-day result as transparent first-party reporting and made its
  uncontrolled n=1 limitation impossible to miss.
- Consolidated all internal links, topic mapping, glossary and priority recrawl
  on the strongest owner.
- Routed product interest to `/app?source=creatine-guide` inside the existing
  single `app-waitlist` Beehiiv audience.
- Added bounded LLM discovery and AI benchmark prompt 368.
