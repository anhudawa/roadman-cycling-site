# Unbound Gravel 2026 search-owner decision

**Decision date:** 26 August 2026

**Source:** Google Search Console Performance for `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`

**Window:** Three months through 24 August 2026

## Decision

- `/blog/unbound-gravel-2026-complete-guide` owns broad **unbound gravel 2026**, results, winners, times and race-story intent.
- `/podcast/mads-wurtz-schmidt-muddiest-unbound-2026-win` provides first-person winner context without replacing the verified result owner.
- `/blog/unbound-gravel-200-training-guide` and `/plan/unbound-gravel` retain preparation and training-plan intent.
- The official UNBOUND Gravel and Life Time pages remain the primary authorities for complete timing, routes, rules and athlete instructions.

The exact query already has one dominant Roadman URL. This is therefore a freshness and click-through-rate intervention, not a cannibalisation or redirect job. The established URL keeps its canonical and is converted from a stale pre-race preview into the maintained post-race result owner.

## Exact-query baseline: `unbound gravel 2026`

| Metric           | Baseline |
| ---------------- | -------: |
| Clicks           |       27 |
| Impressions      |    3,339 |
| CTR              |     0.8% |
| Average position |      8.6 |

Top pages in Search Console:

| URL                                                     | Clicks | Impressions |  CTR | Position | Assigned role                |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -----: | ----------: | ---: | -------: | ---------------------------- |
| `/blog/unbound-gravel-2026-complete-guide`              |     27 |       3,333 | 0.8% |      8.6 | Results and race-story owner |
| `/podcast/mads-wurtz-schmidt-muddiest-unbound-2026-win` |      0 |          14 |   0% |      2.6 | Winner interview support     |

The remaining pages had only isolated one-impression appearances. Page impressions are not additive because one result set can contain or attribute more than one site URL.

## Trust and product changes

- Replaced the pre-race preview, predictions and future tense with verified 30 May 2026 outcomes.
- Published exact men's and women's top-five results and times from Life Time's official race report.
- Added a direct answer, four takeaways, five FAQs, reviewed date and three evidence claims.
- Added visible links to the official results, route, athlete-guide and rules pages.
- Separated official historical facts from Roadman's practical interpretation.
- Removed unsupported universal tyre-pressure, fuelling, electrolyte and hydration prescriptions.
- Connected the result owner to Roadman's Mads Würtz Schmidt interview and distinct Unbound training resources.
- Added explicit LLM discovery, IndexNow priority URLs and a monthly AI-citation prompt.

## Measurement plan

### Seven-day directional check — earliest 5 September 2026

- Confirm Google has recrawled the updated result owner and is showing post-race title and description language.
- Compare CTR and page ownership for `unbound gravel 2026`, `unbound gravel 2026 results`, `unbound gravel winners 2026` and winner-name modifiers.
- Confirm training and podcast modifiers continue to land on their distinct supporting pages.

### Twenty-eight-day decision check — earliest 26 September 2026

- Compare clicks, impressions, CTR, position and page mix with this baseline and a matched preceding period.
- Success means the established owner retains at least 90% of exact-query impressions while CTR improves from the 0.8% baseline.
- If position holds near page one but CTR does not improve, test the title and description against the dominant live result formats before changing the URL or canonical.
