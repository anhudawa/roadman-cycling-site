the head term and its commercial variants. Its limitation is evidence quality
and search usefulness, not a missing URL.

## Trust defects found

The incumbent used a universal packet schedule and treated marketing shorthand
as settled physiology. It claimed that:

- every rider should take the first gel after 20–30 minutes and then follow a
  fixed interval;
- 2:1 glucose-to-fructose was the universal gold standard;
- glucose-only products imposed one hard absorption ceiling;
- 80–120 g/h suited most cyclists and any effort over 90 minutes required more
  than 60 g/h;
- every gel always needed water;
- caffeinated gels should be saved for the final third because earlier use
  “wastes” the effect;
- a fixed number of exposures or a six-to-eight-week schedule guaranteed gut
  adaptation; and
- gels were the primary or superior fuel source.

The page had one cited carbohydrate link, no review date, no reviewer boundary,
no visible evidence table, no product-testing disclosure and no structured
test protocol. It ranked products without having run a common product test.

## Changes prepared

- Retitled the stable owner `Best Cycling Gels 2026? How to Choose and Use
  Them`, with an honest answer to the “best” modifier rather than a fabricated
  product winner.
- Replaced packet intervals with a grams-per-hour calculation that counts gel,
  drink, chew and food carbohydrate together.
- Separated established 30–60 and up-to-90 g/h guidance from emerging 120 g/h
  research in trained endurance athletes.
- Added five source-bounded claims, six FAQs, named editorial review, a
  product-testing disclosure and a six-step HowTo protocol.
- Added primary and systematic evidence for endurance performance, carbohydrate
  dose, 120 g/h boundaries, food versus supplement formats, gut training,
  hydrogels and caffeine.
- Corrected the cycling-nutrition hub so it no longer repeats a mandatory
  60–90 g/h floor, a universal 2:1 ratio or a guaranteed gut-training schedule.
- Pinned the owner in bounded LLM discovery, added it to the recurring IndexNow
  set and added AI benchmark prompt 240.
