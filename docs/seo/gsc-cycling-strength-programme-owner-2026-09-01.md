# Cycling strength programme commercial owner

**Captured:** 2026-09-01  
**Source:** Google Search Console, Web search and Generative AI features  
**Window:** Last three months

## Demand baseline

The sitewide query filter `strength training for cyclists` reported:

- 144 clicks
- 2,080 web impressions
- 6.9% CTR
- average position 6.5

The broader `cycling strength training` filter reported:

- 33 clicks
- 1,220 web impressions
- 2.7% CTR
- average position 9.1

Visible rows included `strength training for cyclists` (94 clicks / 1,625
impressions), `cycling strength training` (25 / 907), `cycling strength training
plan` (3 / 129) and `cycling strength training program` (4 / 42).

The evidence guide already owns the broad informational family. Its visible
query-filter page table showed 98 clicks / 1,672 impressions, ahead of the
masters guide at 31 / 291. The product URL `/strength-training` appeared with 83
Generative AI feature impressions in the path-filter table.

Google warns that filtered totals and tables can be partial. The query numbers
are a directional commercial baseline, not an exact page scorecard.

## Ownership problem found

Two public pages claimed the same course:

- `/strength-training` was the working $65 Stripe checkout owner.
- `/sc` advertised $95, linked its purchase buttons to an open programme view
  and made a second set of commercial claims.

The two pages disagreed on price and split the commercial job. Both also used
guaranteed transfer, pain, injury and performance language that conflicted with
the reviewed evidence guide.

## Decision

1. Keep `/blog/cycling-strength-training-guide` as the informational owner.
2. Keep `/strength-training` as the only self-guided programme and checkout
   owner at the live Stripe price of $65.
3. Permanently redirect `/sc` to `/strength-training`.
4. Keep `/sc/programme` and its exercise materials available to customers but
   mark the complete `/sc` course area `noindex, follow`.
5. Separate the fixed course, the upcoming app and human coaching by job.
6. Remove guaranteed FTP, watt, pain, injury-prevention, body-mass and universal
   age claims from the commercial owner.
7. Send product-adjacent app interest to the single `/app` list with
   `source=strength-plan` attribution.

## Measurement
