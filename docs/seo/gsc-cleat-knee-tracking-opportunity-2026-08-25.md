# Cleat position and knee-tracking search ownership — 25 August 2026

## Scope

Read-only Google Search Console review for the `sc-domain:roadmancycling.com`
property. Search type was Web and the date range was 24 May–23 August 2026.
No validation or indexing control was used.

## Exact-query split

The exact query `cleat position knee pain` recorded 1 click, 36 impressions, a
2.8% CTR and an average position of 11.8. Three pages appeared for the query:

| URL | Clicks | Impressions |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `/blog/knee-pain-cycling-what-to-check-first` | 1 | 48 |
| `/blog/cycling-knee-pain-causes-fixes` | 0 | 1 |
| `/blog/cycling-knee-tracking-cleat-setup-guide` | 0 | 1 |

Rows can overlap because Google aggregates anonymised and page-level data
differently; they should not be summed as a replacement for the query total.

The checklist already has the strongest signal for a pain-led query. The cleat
guide should not try to displace it with a symptom map.

## Cleat-guide demand

Across all visible queries, `/blog/cycling-knee-tracking-cleat-setup-guide`
recorded:

- 28 clicks
- 1,940 impressions
- 1.4% CTR
- average position 6.3
- 32 query rows

Visible query variants include `knee position cycling` (9 impressions),
`cleat position cycling knee pain chart` (5), `knee tracking inward cycling`
(3) and several one- or two-impression variants around outside knee pain and
British Cycling. This is enough demand to retain the URL, but its prior copy
overreached by treating a frontal knee path as a diagnosis and mapping pain
locations to universal cleat, stance, spacer and wedge changes.

## Decision

1. Keep `/blog/cycling-knee-tracking-cleat-setup-guide` as the owner of cleat
   installation, measurement, float, stance terminology and repeatable video
   observation.
2. Let `/blog/knee-pain-cycling-what-to-check-first` own pain-led diagnostic
   checks, including the exact `cleat position knee pain` query.
3. Let `/blog/cycling-knee-pain-causes-fixes` remain the broad evidence and
   clinical-boundary owner.
4. Remove universal knee-tracking lines, pain-location diagnoses, fixed
   rearward moves and automatic spacer/wedge prescriptions from the cleat page.
5. Cite primary research for evidence limits and official manufacturer pages
   only for compatibility, adjustment and installation—not health outcomes.

## Measurement

Compare the cleat guide, checklist and exact query at the 7-day and 28-day
checkpoints. Watch clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, query overlap
and whether setup-led variants consolidate on the cleat guide while pain-led
variants remain with the checklist.
