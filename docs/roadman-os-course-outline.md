# Roadman OS — 12-Week Course Outline

**Working Title:** The Roadman Operating System
**Price:** $297–397
**Delivery:** Custom members area on roadmancycling.com
**Structure:** 12 weeks, progressive unlock, all 5 pillars woven through every week

---

## Design Principles

1. **Week-based, not topic-based** — Every week delivers across Training, Nutrition, S&C, Recovery, and Le Metier. No more siloed modules.
2. **Assessment-first** — Plateau Diagnostic determines personalised starting point before Week 1 content unlocks.
3. **Progressive disclosure** — Content unlocks weekly. Members can't skip ahead. Each week builds on the last.
4. **Tool-integrated** — Fuel Planner, Race Predictor, and TrainingPeaks plans are embedded at the point of need, not in a separate "resources" tab.
5. **Expert content at the right moment** — Prof Seiler on polarised training drops in Week 3 when members understand zones. Dr Dunne's FFTWR masterclass drops in Week 4 when the Fuel Planner unlocks.
6. **Accountability built in** — Weekly check-in, milestone celebrations, community challenges.
7. **Existing NDY content repurposed** — ~60-70% of current classroom content migrates into the OS structure. Nothing wasted.

## Pre-Course: Assessment & Setup

**Unlocks immediately on purchase. Must complete before Week 1 unlocks.**

### Plateau Diagnostic
- Complete the diagnostic questionnaire (existing tool on roadmancycling.com)
- Outputs: current training profile, key limiters, personalised 12-week focus areas
- Maps member to one of 4 persona archetypes (guides content weighting)

### Training Setup
- Enter FTP (or schedule a threshold test — repurpose NDY "How To Do The Perfect Threshold Test" video)
- Select training availability (hours/week: 6, 8, 10, or 12)
- Select discipline focus (Road, Road + TT, Gran Fondo, Gravel)
- Select training phase (Base, Build, Race Prep, Off-Season)
- TrainingPeaks plan auto-assigned based on selections

### Body Composition Baseline
- Enter current weight, target weight (if applicable), body comp goal (lose/maintain/gain)
- Sets deficit/surplus % for Fuel Planner calculations
- No judgement framing — "This is your starting point, not your identity"

### Community Onboarding
- Introduce yourself post (templated prompt)
- Community guidelines (repurpose from NDY)
- How to get help / who to message (repurpose from NDY)
