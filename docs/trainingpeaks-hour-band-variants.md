# TrainingPeaks Hour-Band Plan Variants

**Date:** 11 May 2026
**Purpose:** Define 4 hour-band variants (6, 8, 10, 12 hrs/week) for the Roadman Method Week 1 assessment flow.

---

## Context

All 7 existing Anthony Walsh TrainingPeaks plans cluster at 8-9 hrs/week Intermediate. The Week 1 assessment asks members to select their available training hours, but there are no plans to assign outside the ~8hr band.

This document defines the scaling logic and session modifications needed to create 4 hour-band variants for each plan type (Road Race, Gravel Race, Sportive, Base, Build, FTP Booster, VO2 Max Booster).

---

## Weekly Template (Anthony's Preferred Day Pattern)

| Day | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Role | REST | Key 1 | Endurance | Key 2 | REST | Long Ride | REST |

All 4 hour-band variants use this day pattern. The adjustments are to session duration, not to the day structure.

## Hour-Band Definitions

### Band 1: Low Volume (6 hrs/week)

**Target rider:** Time-crunched professional, parent with young kids, new to structured training.

| Day | Session | Duration | Notes |
|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| Mon | REST | 0 | — |
| Tue | Key Session 1 | 45-60 min | Shortened intervals, fewer reps |
| Wed | Z2 Endurance | 60 min | Minimum effective Z2 dose |
| Thu | Key Session 2 | 45-60 min | Shortened structured work |
| Fri | REST | 0 | — |
| Sat | Long Ride | 120 min | Reduced from 3hr to 2hr |
| Sun | REST | 0 | — |

**Scaling rules:**
- Drop 1 interval rep from each key session (e.g., 5×2min VO2 → 4×2min)
- Long ride capped at 2 hours
- Endurance ride = 60 min minimum
- Recovery weeks: 4.5 hrs (25% reduction)
- Race-specific phase: allow up to 7hrs for 2 build weeks

**Session modifications by phase:**

| Phase | Tue | Thu |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Base | 30s Sprints (45min, 4 reps) | Mixed Cadence Spin (60min) |
| VO2 | 20/30/40 VO2 (45min, 1.5 sets) | 5×2min VO2 (45min, 4 reps) |
| Threshold | Aero-Threshold Ramps (60min, 2 blocks) | 4×5min Threshold (60min, 3 reps) |
| Race Specific | Race Start Prep (60min, 3 reps) | Breakaway O/U (60min, 3 reps) |
| Taper | Taper Reps (50min) | Pre Race Day (45min) |

### Band 3: High Volume (10 hrs/week)

**Target rider:** Serious age-grouper, targeting competitive results, flexible schedule.

| Day | Session | Duration | Notes |
|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| Mon | REST | 0 | — |
| Tue | Key Session 1 | 70-100 min | Full session + extended warm-up/cooldown |
| Wed | Z2 Endurance | 90-105 min | Extended Z2 |
| Thu | Key Session 2 | 75-100 min | Full session + extended cooldown |
| Fri | Z2 Easy Spin | 60 min | **Added session** — easy Z2 or recovery spin |
| Sat | Long Ride | 180-210 min | 3-3.5 hours |
| Sun | REST | 0 | — |

**Scaling rules:**
- Add a 5th training day (Friday easy spin, 60 min)
- Extend long ride by 30 min
- Extend Wednesday endurance by 15 min
- Key sessions get +10 min warmup/cooldown
- Recovery weeks: 7.5 hrs

**Session modifications by phase:**

| Phase | Fri Addition |
|-------|-------------|
| Base | Z2 Endurance 60min |
| VO2 | Z2 Endurance 60min |
| Threshold | Z2 Endurance 60min |
| Race Specific | Z2 Endurance 60min with 4×6s openers |
| Taper | REST (drop back to 4 days) |

### Band 4: Elite Volume (12 hrs/week)

**Target rider:** Experienced racer, racing calendar, can sustain high training load.

| Day | Session | Duration | Notes |
|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| Mon | REST | 0 | — |
| Tue | Key Session 1 | 80-120 min | Full session + extra set |
| Wed | Z2 Endurance | 105-120 min | Extended Z2 |
| Thu | Key Session 2 | 80-120 min | Full session + extra set |
| Fri | Endurance + Openers | 75 min | **Added session** — Z2 with neuromuscular openers |
| Sat | Long Ride | 210-240 min | 3.5-4 hours |
| Sun | Z2 Recovery Spin | 60 min | **Added session** — very easy spin, active recovery |

**Scaling rules:**
- 6 training days, 1 full rest day (Monday only)
- Add 1 interval rep to key sessions where appropriate
- Long ride extends to 4 hours in build weeks
- Sunday becomes active recovery (not rest)
- Recovery weeks: 9 hrs

**Session modifications by phase:**

| Phase | Tue Extra | Thu Extra | Fri | Sun |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-----|-----|
| Base | +1 sprint rep | +1 cadence block | Z2 60min + 4×6s sprints | Z2 Recovery 60min |
| VO2 | +1 VO2 set | +1 VO2 rep | Z2 75min + 4×6s sprints | Z2 Recovery 60min |
| Threshold | +1 threshold rep | +1 ramp block | Z2 75min + 4×6s openers | Z2 Recovery 60min |
| Race Specific | +5min at race pace | +2 O/U reps | Z2 75min + race openers | Z2 Recovery 60min |
| Taper | Standard (no extra) | Standard | REST | REST |

## Periodisation Across Hour Bands

The mesocycle structure (3 build + 1 recovery) stays identical across all 4 bands. The periodisation phases (Base → VO2 → Threshold → Race Specific → Taper) are the same. Only the session volumes change.

| Metric | 6hr | 8hr | 10hr | 12hr |
|--------|-----|-----|------|------|
| Training days/week | 4 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| Rest days/week | 3 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Long ride (build week) | 2hr | 3hr | 3.5hr | 4hr |
| Long ride (recovery week) | 1.5hr | 2hr | 2.5hr | 3hr |
| Weekly TSS (build week) | ~280 | ~420 | ~520 | ~620 |
| Weekly TSS (recovery week) | ~200 | ~300 | ~380 | ~450 |
| Key session count | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 (+1 opener) |

## Fuel Planner Integration

Each hour band produces different daily energy demands, which the Fuel Planner automatically accounts for. The session IF and duration drive the energy calculation — no separate nutrition plan is needed per hour band.

| Band | Typical training day kcal (FTP 250) | Long ride day kcal |
|------|-------------------------------------|-------------------|
| 6hr | Rest +150-250 kcal | Rest +300-400 kcal |
| 8hr | Rest +200-350 kcal | Rest +400-600 kcal |
| 10hr | Rest +250-400 kcal | Rest +500-700 kcal |
| 12hr | Rest +350-500 kcal | Rest +600-900 kcal |

## Week 1 Assessment Flow

The assessment asks 3 questions that determine plan assignment:

1. **Discipline:** Road Race / Gravel Race / Sportive / General Fitness
2. **Available hours:** 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 hrs/week
3. **Current phase:** Base (new to structured training) / Build (have a base) / Race Prep (event in <8 weeks)

This creates a matrix of: 4 disciplines × 4 hour bands × 3 starting phases = 48 plan variants.

In practice, the 16-week plans cover all phases, so the starting phase determines which week the member begins at (Week 1 for Base, Week 5 for Build, Week 9 for Race Prep). This reduces the actual plans needed to: 4 disciplines × 4 hour bands = **16 TrainingPeaks plans**.
