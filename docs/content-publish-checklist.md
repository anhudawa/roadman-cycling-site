# Content Publish Checklist

Run every item before any new article goes live. If any item fails, fix it before publishing.

---

## 1. Brief and approval

- [ ] Approved content brief exists (all 7 gate questions answered)
- [ ] Brief was approved by Anthony before writing started

## 2. Slop term check

Scan the full article for every term below. If any appear, rewrite the sentence.

**Kill list (never use):**
- "Here's what nobody tells you..."
- "Here's the thing..."
- "Here's the thing that nobody tells you..."
- "Let's dive in" / "dive into" / "deep dive" / "deep dives"
- "Game-changer"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Secret to..."
- "You won't believe..."
- "The truth about..."
- "Here's why..." (replace with something specific)
- "Everything you need to know about..."
- "In today's world..."
- "It's no secret that..."
- "At the end of the day..."

**Claude fingerprint words (catch and rewrite):**
- "genuinely" used as filler emphasis
- "straightforward"
- "Let me break it down" / "Let me pull them out" / "Let me explain"
- "The honest [x]" pattern
- "Here's the/what/why" pivot phrases (overused; vary transitions)

**Batch pattern check:** If publishing multiple articles in one session, read them together. Identical intro rhythms, repeated filler words, and same CTA structure across a batch are visible to readers even when individual posts pass the kill list.

- [ ] All slop terms and fingerprint words cleared

## 3. Language and style

- [ ] British English throughout (colour, organise, programme, centre, defence, favour, etc.)
- [ ] Editorial quality 9.5/10 -- Rouleur magazine benchmark
- [ ] Every sentence earns its place with specificity, named experts, real numbers, or genuine insight
- [ ] No sentence could appear unchanged on a generic fitness website

## 4. Content rules

- [ ] No heavy compound lifts prescribed (no deadlifts, barbell squats, barbell rows -- injury risk for 35-55 audience)
- [ ] No unverified medical or health claims
- [ ] All facts cited with source (study, podcast episode, named expert, or coaching observation)
- [ ] Evidence level stated where applicable
- [ ] No episode numbers used (YouTube count inflates numbers)
- [ ] No hardcoded counts that will go stale (community size, article count, YouTube subs)

## 5. Pricing and currency

- [ ] All pricing in USD ($)
- [ ] NDY pricing is $195/month (not old tiered pricing)
- [ ] Girona Training Camps are the sole EUR exception (EUR995/person)
- [ ] No GBP used anywhere
