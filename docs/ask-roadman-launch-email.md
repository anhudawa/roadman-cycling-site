**BODY:**

You know when you're mid-ride, something doesn't feel right — maybe your power's dropped, maybe the fuelling plan isn't landing, maybe you just watched three wheels drift away on a climb you should have been fine on — and you want a straight answer?

Not a Reddit thread. Not a 45-minute podcast you have to scrub through. Just the answer.

That's what Ask Roadman is.

I've spent the last five years sat across from some of the best coaches, sports scientists, and riders in the world — Professor Seiler, Dan Lorang, Dr. David Dunne, Lachlan Morton, the list goes on. Over 1,400 conversations. And now every one of those conversations is searchable.

**Here's how it works.**

You go to roadmancycling.com/ask, type your question, and Ask Roadman pulls the answer directly from the podcast archive. It tells you who said it, which episode it came from, and gives you the timestamp so you can go listen to the full context yourself.

It's grounded. Every answer cites the source. If the podcast hasn't covered your question, it'll tell you that too — no making things up.

**It's free.** All you need is your email. We'll send you a one-tap sign-in link and you're in for a week. And yes, we'll add you to the Saturday Spin — but you already knew that, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

Here's the good news: you don't have to guess anymore. 1,400+ episodes of evidence-based coaching, searchable in seconds.

[Ask your first question →](https://roadmancycling.com/ask)

Talk soon,
Anthony

P.S. — Ask Roadman can be wrong. It's grounded in what the guests actually said on the pod, but it's not a substitute for a coach who knows your numbers. For anything medical or injury-related, see a professional first.
