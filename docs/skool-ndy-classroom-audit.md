# Skool NDY Classroom Audit

**Date:** 11 May 2026
**Purpose:** Full structural audit of the "Not Done Yet" (NDY) paid Skool community classroom to inform the Roadman OS course design.

---

## Overview

The NDY classroom contains **9 modules** organised across the 5 Roadman pillars (Training, Nutrition, S&C, Recovery) plus onboarding, skills academy, live support, tools, and 1:1 access. Content is a mix of original video, podcast conversation clips, downloadable resources, and weekly live Q&A recordings.

**Key stats:**
- 9 modules total (1 in Draft state)
- ~70+ individual lessons
- Completion rates are 0% across all modules except Academy (8%)
- Multiple lessons marked (Draft) indicating incomplete/unpublished content
- Content posted primarily by Sarah Egan, with some by Anthony

### Strengths (What Roadman OS Should Preserve)

1. **Expert access is genuine** — Seiler, Lorang, Friel, Millar, Phil Burt, Nick Littlehales. This isn't fake authority; it's real World Tour access. The Roadman OS must preserve and expand this.

2. **5-pillar framework is sound** — Training, Nutrition, S&C, Recovery + Academy/Skills covers the complete cyclist. The framework just needs proper articulation.

3. **Exercise library is well-curated** — 18 cyclist-specific exercises with no heavy compounds. Short-form video format works for reference.

4. **Weekly Q&A cadence is strong** — 24 consecutive weeks of live sessions shows operational consistency and genuine community engagement.

5. **Masterclass format works** — Academy's 8% completion rate (vs. 0% elsewhere) suggests long-form, expert-led masterclasses are what members actually consume.

6. **FFTWR methodology is a differentiator** — Dr David Dunne's "Big Blocks" and the Impey connection give genuine scientific credibility to the nutrition approach.

### Weaknesses (What Roadman OS Must Fix)

1. **No progressive structure** — Content is organised by topic, not by journey. There's no "Week 1, do this. Week 2, do this." Members are dropped into a library with no roadmap.

2. **Draft content in critical areas** — The 5 Pillars introduction, Reverse Periodisation (the training philosophy), and most of Recovery are incomplete. Members hit dead ends.

3. **Near-zero completion rates** — 0% across 8 of 9 modules. The content exists but members aren't consuming it systematically. This is a structural problem, not a content problem.

4. **No assessment or diagnostic** — Members start with no baseline. No FTP input, no body composition, no goal-setting framework. The Plateau Diagnostic solves this.

5. **No integration between pillars** — Training, Nutrition, S&C, and Recovery exist in silos. A training day should automatically inform nutrition (Fuel Planner), recovery, and S&C scheduling.

6. **Conversations are unstructured** — 13+ podcast conversation clips are dumped without context, timestamps, or key takeaway summaries. High value, low accessibility.

7. **No training plan delivery** — "Beginning Your Training Plan" exists but there's no visible TrainingPeaks integration or plan selection flow in the classroom itself.

8. **Rider Support is unsearchable** — 24 Q&A recordings with no topic index. Members can't find answers to specific questions.

9. **No accountability mechanism** — No check-ins, no progress tracking, no milestone celebrations beyond Skool's built-in completion percentage.

10. **60 min call is a dead end** — Premium 1:1 access is reduced to "Pop me a dm" with no structure, no booking, no follow-up system.

## Implications for Roadman OS Design

The NDY classroom has **good content in a bad container**. The expert conversations, exercise library, and nutrition framework are genuinely valuable. The problem is structural: there's no journey, no progression, no integration between pillars, and no accountability.

Roadman OS should:

1. **Reorganise by week, not by topic** — 12 weeks, each week unlocking content across all 5 pillars simultaneously
2. **Start with assessment** — Plateau Diagnostic determines starting point, FTP, body comp goals, available hours
3. **Connect the pillars** — Training day → automatic Fuel Planner prescription → appropriate S&C → recovery protocol
4. **Progressive disclosure** — Don't dump everything at once. Week 1 = foundations. Week 6 = advanced. Week 12 = race preparation.
5. **Embed expert content at the right moment** — Prof Seiler on polarised training appears in Week 3 when members are ready for it, not in a random library
6. **Build accountability** — Weekly check-ins, milestone unlocks, community challenges
7. **Preserve the Q&A** — Weekly live sessions continue but with structured topics matching the current week's content
8. **Tool integration** — Fuel Planner, Race Predictor, Plateau Diagnostic all embedded at relevant points in the 12-week journey

The existing NDY content should migrate into the OS structure, not be abandoned. Approximately 60-70% of current content can be directly repurposed; the rest needs to be completed (Draft items) or created fresh.
