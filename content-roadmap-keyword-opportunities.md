# Roadman Cycling: 50 Keyword Opportunities to Own

**Content Roadmap | Q3-Q4 2026**
**Compiled: 9 June 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

This document identifies 50 high-value search terms that Roadman Cycling should target over the next 3 months. Each term has been selected based on four criteria: meaningful search volume (1K+ monthly), alignment with Roadman's audience (serious amateur cyclists, 35-55), weakness in current top results, and fit with the five content pillars (coaching, nutrition, S&C, recovery, community).

Search volumes are estimated from cross-referencing SeoLabs, Clicks.so, Salience UK cycling data, and SERP competition analysis. All volumes are global monthly estimates unless marked (UK) for UK-specific terms.

**Where Roadman already ranks well:** FTP benchmarks, FTP improvement, FTP training zones, masters training over 40, cycling over 50, strength training for cyclists, nutrition periodisation, race day nutrition, sleep optimisation, power-to-weight ratio, sportive preparation, gran fondo training plan, Etape du Tour training plan, zone 2 vs endurance training.

**Where the gaps are:** The 50 terms below represent topics where Roadman either doesn't rank at all or ranks outside the top 10 despite having podcast content that covers the subject matter.

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Build Hub Pages First

The highest-impact move is creating four hub pages that link to existing and new content:

- **Cycling Training Plan** (hub) -> sweet spot, polarised, VO2 max, intervals, indoor, base training, periodisation
- **Cycling Nutrition** (hub) -> weight loss, carb loading, diet plan, fasting, supplements, race day nutrition
- **Cycling Recovery** (hub) -> sleep, foam rolling, overtraining, active recovery, yoga/stretching
- **Cycling Injury Prevention** (hub) -> knee pain, back pain, bike fit, saddle sores, common injuries

These four hub pages alone target ~18K+ monthly searches and create the internal linking architecture that Google rewards.

### 2. Leverage the Podcast Archive

Roadman has 1,400+ podcast episodes. For every blog post in this roadmap, there is almost certainly an existing episode that covers the topic in depth. Each blog post should:

- Reference and link to the relevant episode(s)
- Pull direct quotes from expert guests (Seiler, Lorang, etc.)
- Embed the episode audio or YouTube clip
- This creates a content flywheel: blog post captures search traffic -> listener discovers podcast -> podcast listener joins community

### 3. Interactive Tools = Email Captures

Three calculator/tool opportunities identified in this research:

- **FTP Calculator** (links to FTP test guide)
- **Calories Burned Cycling Calculator** (links to weight loss guide)
- **W/kg Calculator** (links to power-to-weight guide)

Each tool should gate a "personalised plan" or "detailed analysis" behind an email capture. Based on Roadman's current 27-48% email open rates, these leads are highly valuable.
